# Understand household invitation expiry

## How long an invitation works

A household invitation link works for 7 days after it is sent. The person you invite must use the link to join the home before it expires.

## What happens when it expires

After 7 days, the link can no longer be used to join the household. The owner needs to send a new invitation.

## Send a new invitation

Only the home owner can invite members. To send a new invitation:

1. Open **Settings > Household > Invite** in the app or web dashboard.
2. Enter the person's email address.
3. Send the invitation.

The new invitation has a link that works for 7 days. After the person joins, they become a household member and can change the temperature, schedules and away mode.
