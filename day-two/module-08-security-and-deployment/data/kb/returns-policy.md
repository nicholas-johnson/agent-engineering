# Returns Policy

## Scope

This policy explains the 30-day period for returning Dormouse products. It applies to the Dormouse Thermostat, Dormouse Radiator Valve and Dormouse Room Sensor.

## Return period

You may return a Dormouse product within 30 days of purchase. The return period is for deciding whether to keep the product; it is separate from the product's 2-year warranty.

## Products and accessories

Return the product you are sending back and any accessories supplied with it. For a Dormouse Radiator Valve, include the adapters provided in the box if they are part of your return. For a Dormouse Thermostat, include its front cover and wall plate if you are returning the complete thermostat.

## Before returning a thermostat

If you are giving the thermostat to someone else or moving house without it, remove its personal setup first. A factory reset removes Wi-Fi details, schedules and the link to your account.

To reset it, hold the dial and the boost button down together for 10 seconds, until the display shows RESET. The thermostat restarts as if it were new.

## Contacting Dormouse

To arrange a return, contact Dormouse in the app under Help > Contact support. Provide the product details and the purchase information requested by support. Follow the return instructions provided before sending the product.

## After the 30-day period

The 30-day return period does not extend the return window. If your product develops a fault after that period, the Dormouse Thermostat, Dormouse Radiator Valve and Dormouse Room Sensor have a 2-year warranty. Contact Dormouse in the app under Help > Contact support for help with a warranty issue.

## Safe handling

Handle products carefully while preparing them for return. The thermostat is powered by mains electricity through its wall plate and must be wired to the boiler by a qualified installer. Do not remove it from the wall or disconnect its wiring unless a qualified installer has made it safe to do so.

## Policy limits

This policy describes the 30-day return period and how to contact Dormouse to arrange a return. It does not specify refund timing, return postage arrangements or product condition requirements. Check the instructions from Dormouse support for the details that apply to your return.
