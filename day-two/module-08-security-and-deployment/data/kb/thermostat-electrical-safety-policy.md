# Thermostat Electrical Safety Policy

## Purpose and scope

This policy covers safe use of the Dormouse Thermostat, including its mains power and connection to a boiler. It applies to anyone who handles, uses or installs the thermostat.

The thermostat is powered by mains electricity through its wall plate and must be wired to the boiler by a qualified installer. Do not install or wire it yourself. Follow the installer's instructions and any applicable electrical and boiler safety requirements.

This policy concerns the thermostat. It does not replace the instructions or safety requirements for your boiler or other heating equipment.

## Installation and wiring

Have a qualified installer fit the thermostat and connect it to the boiler. The thermostat is designed for use with combi, system and conventional gas boilers. Do not connect it to equipment outside the intended system.

Do not alter the wiring, wall plate or boiler connection after installation. If wiring or the wall plate appears loose, damaged or incorrectly fitted, do not attempt to repair it. Ask a qualified installer to inspect it.

The thermostat operates indoors, between 0 and 40 degrees. Do not install or use it outside this temperature range.

## Safe everyday use

Use the thermostat only as intended: to measure room temperature and control the heating system. Do not paint over the thermostat or cover it in a way that could interfere with its operation.

Clean it with a soft, dry cloth. Do not use sprays or cleaning products. Do not open the thermostat or attempt repairs to its mains-powered components.

If the thermostat or its wall plate appears damaged, stop using it and contact a qualified installer for advice. Do not handle exposed wiring or attempt to make the installation safe yourself.

## Power interruptions and backup batteries

The thermostat has two AA backup batteries behind the front cover. They keep the clock and schedules during a power cut for up to 48 hours. They do not power the heating: while mains power is off, the thermostat cannot switch the heating on.

If the display shows E-402, mains power has been interrupted and the thermostat is running on its backup batteries. Have the mains supply and installation checked by a qualified installer if the interruption is unexpected or continues.

If the display shows E-401, replace the two AA batteries within a month. To reach them, gently pull the bottom of the front cover towards you. Replace the batteries once a year, or when E-401 appears. Do not treat the backup batteries as a substitute for mains power.

## Responding to errors

The display and app show an error code when the thermostat detects a problem. Follow the guidance for the code in the user guide.

- **E-217, temperature sensor disconnected:** The thermostat turns the heating off to be safe. Contact Dormouse support in the app under Help > Contact support to arrange a replacement.
- **E-305, boiler not responding:** Check that the boiler is switched on and that its pressure is between 1 and 1.5 bar. Then check the boiler's own display for a fault.
- **E-312, boiler reports a fault:** Refer to the boiler's manual or contact a registered gas engineer.
- **E-402, mains power interrupted:** The thermostat cannot switch the heating on while mains power is off. Have the supply checked by a qualified installer if needed.

Do not attempt to bypass a safety response, repair the thermostat's electrical components or work on the boiler wiring.

## Limits and safety features

Frost protection is always on and cannot be turned off. If the room falls below 5 degrees, the thermostat turns on the heating, even when heating is off or away mode is on.

Frost protection is a thermostat function, not a guarantee that pipes or the property will remain safe in every situation. During a mains power cut, the thermostat cannot switch the heating on. Take account of the boiler and property's own safety requirements.

The thermostat's temperature accuracy is 0.3 degrees. Its stated operating range is 0 to 40 degrees indoors.

## Support and maintenance

For thermostat errors or product support, use Help > Contact support in the Dormouse app. For installation, wiring, mains supply or boiler work, contact a qualified installer or, where the boiler has a fault, a registered gas engineer.

Do not dismantle, modify or attempt to repair the thermostat. Keep the product and its electrical installation in safe condition, and follow the guidance for battery replacement and error codes in the user guide.
