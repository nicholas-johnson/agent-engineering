# Manage location sharing for household members

## How location sharing works

Geofencing uses the location of household members' phones to turn Away mode on or off. Each member chooses in the Dormouse app whether to share their location.

Away mode turns on when every member who shares their location is more than 1 km from home. It turns off when the first of those members comes back within 1 km.

## Choose whether to share your location

Each household member manages their own location-sharing choice in the app. Choosing not to share means their phone's location is not used for geofencing.

The geofencing rules apply to members who share their location. When all of those members are more than 1 km from home, Away mode turns on. When the first returns within 1 km, Away mode turns off.

## Stop geofencing for the whole home

The home owner or a household member can stop geofencing for the whole home:

1. Open the Dormouse app.
2. Go to Settings > Away > Geofencing.
3. Turn geofencing off.

When geofencing is stopped for the home, it no longer turns Away mode on or off using members' locations.

## What location information is stored

Dormouse does not store phone locations. It stores only whether each member is home or away. This status is used to apply the geofencing rules.
