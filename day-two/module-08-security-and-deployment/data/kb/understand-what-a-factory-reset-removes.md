# Understand what a factory reset removes

A factory reset removes the thermostat's Wi-Fi details, schedules and link to your Dormouse account. After the reset, the thermostat restarts as if it were new, and you can pair it again.

## What the reset removes

The reset clears:

- **Wi-Fi details:** the thermostat no longer has the network information it used to connect to Wi-Fi.
- **Schedules:** the thermostat's saved heating periods are removed.
- **The account link:** the thermostat is no longer linked to your Dormouse account.

## What this means

You will need to pair the thermostat again to connect it to Wi-Fi and your Dormouse account. Pairing is done in the Dormouse app. Keep your phone within 5 metres of the thermostat while pairing.

The reset removes schedules from the thermostat. Set up a schedule again in the app or web dashboard if you want the thermostat to follow scheduled temperatures. You can also use the dial to change the target temperature.

## When to reset

Reset the thermostat before giving it to someone else or moving house without it. This removes its Wi-Fi details, schedules and account link so the next person can pair it with their own account.

## Reset the thermostat

1. Hold the dial and the boost button down together for 10 seconds.
2. Release them when the display shows RESET.
3. Wait for the thermostat to restart.

The thermostat is then ready to be paired again.
