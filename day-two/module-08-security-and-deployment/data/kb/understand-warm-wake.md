# Understand Warm Wake

## What Warm Wake does

Warm Wake is a heating feature that starts the heating before a schedule period. It helps prepare the home for the target temperature set for that period.

## The sunrise symbol

While Warm Wake is heating ahead of a schedule period, the thermostat display shows a sunrise symbol. The symbol indicates that Warm Wake is active.

## When it is not active

The sunrise symbol is shown while Warm Wake is heating ahead of a schedule period. When the symbol is not shown, Warm Wake is not currently heating ahead of a schedule period.
