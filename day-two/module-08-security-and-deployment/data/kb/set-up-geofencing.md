# Set up geofencing

Geofencing uses household members' phone locations to switch away mode on and off. It works with the Dormouse app and a home with geofencing enabled.

## How geofencing works

Geofencing checks whether each household member who shares their location is within 1 km of home.

- Away mode turns on when every member sharing their location is more than 1 km from home.
- Away mode turns off when the first of those members comes back within 1 km.

Away mode keeps the home at the away temperature until it is turned off. The away temperature is 16 degrees unless it has been changed in Settings > Away > Away temperature.

## Choose whether to share your location

Each household member chooses whether to share their location in the app. The choice applies to that member; it does not change other members' sharing choices.

Geofencing uses the location of members who share their location to decide whether the home is away. If you do not share your location, your phone's location is not used for that decision.

Phone locations are never stored. The system keeps only whether each member is home or away.

## Stop geofencing for the home

The owner or a household member can stop geofencing for the whole home in Settings > Away > Geofencing. This stops geofencing from automatically switching away mode based on members' locations.

You can still turn away mode on or off yourself. In the app, tap Away to turn it on. On the thermostat, choose Menu > Away. To turn it off, use the app or thermostat controls.

## Check the away temperature

When away mode is on, the home is kept at the away temperature. To see or change that temperature, go to Settings > Away > Away temperature. Changing the away temperature does not change whether geofencing is enabled or which household members share their location.

## Household membership

A home has one owner and can have up to eight members. Members can change away mode. Only the owner can invite or remove members, or add or remove devices.

If a member is added to or removed from the household, review the location-sharing choices in the app so they reflect who should take part in geofencing. Each member makes their own choice about sharing.

## Frost protection

Frost protection remains active whether away mode is on or off. If the room falls below 5 degrees, the thermostat turns the heating on to help stop pipes freezing. The display shows a snowflake while frost protection is heating.

## If geofencing does not behave as expected

Check that geofencing is enabled in Settings > Away > Geofencing, and that the relevant household members have chosen to share their location in the app. Geofencing switches away mode on only when every member who shares their location is more than 1 km from home. It switches away mode off when the first of them comes back within 1 km.

For help, contact support in the app under Help > Contact support.
