# Phone Location Privacy Policy

## Purpose and scope

This policy explains how Dormouse uses household members' phone locations for geofencing. Geofencing can turn Away mode on and off based on whether household members who share their location are near home.

This policy covers phone location information used for that purpose. It does not describe other account or thermostat data.

## Location sharing is a choice

Each household member chooses in the Dormouse app whether to share their location. Geofencing uses the locations of members who have chosen to share. A member can choose not to share their location.

To stop geofencing for the whole home, go to Settings > Away > Geofencing.

## How geofencing uses location

When geofencing is on, the system uses the location of each household member who shares their location to determine whether they are home or away:

- Away mode turns on when every member who shares their location is more than 1 km from home.
- Away mode turns off when the first of those members comes back within 1 km.

Geofencing changes Away mode. Away mode keeps the home at the away temperature until it is turned off. Frost protection remains active even when Away mode is on.

## What the system keeps

Phone locations are never stored. The system keeps only whether each household member is home or away.

The stored home-or-away status is used for geofencing and the associated Away mode behaviour. It is not a record of the member's phone location.

## Managing geofencing

Each member manages their own choice to share location in the app. To stop geofencing for the entire home, go to Settings > Away > Geofencing.

Turning geofencing off for the home stops geofencing from turning Away mode on or off. It does not change the away temperature. You can still turn Away mode on or off in the app or on the thermostat.

## Household members

A home can have one owner and up to eight members. Each member makes their own location-sharing choice in the app. Household membership does not, by itself, mean that a member shares their location.

Only the owner can invite or remove household members. To invite someone, go to Settings > Household > Invite. Invitations work for 7 days.

## Contacting Dormouse

For help with location sharing or geofencing, contact Dormouse in the app under Help > Contact support.
