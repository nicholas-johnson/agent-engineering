# Understand how location privacy works with geofencing

Geofencing uses the location of household members' phones to turn away mode on and off. It uses each member's location to decide whether they are home or away, but phone locations are never stored. The system keeps only whether each member is home or away.

## How geofencing affects away mode

Away mode turns on when every household member who shares their location is more than 1 km from home. It turns off when the first of those members comes back within 1 km.

Each member chooses whether to share their location in the app. Geofencing uses the locations of members who have chosen to share, and the system keeps their home-or-away status rather than a record of where their phones have been.

## Choose whether to share your location

Each household member manages their own location sharing in the app. Choosing not to share means geofencing will not use that member's phone location to make its away-mode decision.

Location sharing is separate for each member. One person can choose to share while another does not.

## Turn geofencing off for the home

To stop geofencing from changing away mode, go to **Settings > Away > Geofencing**.

Turning geofencing off stops its automatic away-mode changes. You can still turn away mode on or off yourself in the app, or choose **Menu > Away** on the thermostat. Away mode keeps the home at its away temperature until you turn it off.

## What the system keeps

Phone locations are never stored. The system keeps only whether each member is home or away. It does not keep a history of where a member's phone has been.

This is separate from heating history. Heating history is kept for two years; it records heating activity, not a record of phone locations.
