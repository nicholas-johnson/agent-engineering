# Identify your thermostat hardware revision

A Dormouse Thermostat's serial number identifies its hardware revision. Revisions A, B and C were made in 2024, 2025 and 2026 respectively.

## Find the serial number

Look for the serial number on your thermostat. Its first three characters identify the hardware revision. You do not need to open the thermostat to interpret the prefix.

## Match the prefix to a revision

| Serial-number prefix | Hardware revision | Year |
|---|---|---|
| DMA | A | 2024 |
| DMB | B | 2025 |
| DMC | C | 2026 |

For example, a serial number beginning with **DMB** identifies a revision B thermostat.

## Check the firmware separately

The hardware revision and firmware version are different details. To check the firmware, press the dial to open the menu, then choose **Menu > About**. The thermostat covered by this guide runs firmware 4.1.
