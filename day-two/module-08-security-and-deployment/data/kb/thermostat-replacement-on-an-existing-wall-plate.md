# Thermostat Replacement on an Existing Wall Plate

This guide covers replacing a Dormouse Thermostat while keeping an existing wall plate. It is for installers and confident customers. The thermostat must be wired to the boiler by a qualified installer.

## Before you begin

Check that the replacement is a Dormouse Thermostat and that the existing installation is suitable:

- The system has a gas boiler: combi, system or conventional.
- The thermostat is wired to the boiler and powered by mains through its wall plate.
- A 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network is available. The thermostat cannot join a 5 GHz network.
- You have access to the Dormouse app and a Dormouse account.

The thermostat is for indoor use between 0 and 40 degrees. If you are unsure whether the wiring, boiler or wall plate is suitable, have the installation checked by a qualified installer before proceeding.

## Check the existing installation

Before removing the old thermostat, note how it is installed and check the wall plate and wiring for visible damage. Do not proceed if the wall plate is damaged or the wiring appears unsafe. The thermostat is mains-powered, so have a qualified installer disconnect and reconnect the wiring.

Check that the replacement can be positioned securely on the existing wall plate. Do not assume that a different thermostat's wall plate or wiring arrangement is compatible. If the existing wall plate cannot securely support the Dormouse Thermostat, stop and arrange a suitable installation.

## Remove and fit the thermostat

1. Have a qualified installer isolate the mains supply before handling the thermostat or its wiring.
2. Remove the old thermostat from the wall plate without pulling or disturbing the wiring.
3. Check that the wall plate is secure and that its connections are in good condition.
4. Fit the Dormouse Thermostat to the existing wall plate, following the fitting instructions supplied with the thermostat.
5. Have the qualified installer reconnect the mains supply and confirm that the thermostat starts normally.

Do not attempt to alter boiler wiring unless you are qualified to do so. If the thermostat does not start, or an error appears, check the error code in the user guide before continuing.

## Check power and operation

After power is restored, check that the display is on and showing the room temperature and target temperature. Turn the dial to change the target temperature, then press it to confirm. A manual temperature change lasts until the next schedule period starts.

If the display shows E-402, mains power is interrupted and the thermostat is running on its backup batteries. It keeps the clock and schedules for up to 48 hours, but cannot switch the heating on. Have the mains supply checked by a qualified installer.

If the display shows E-217, the temperature sensor is disconnected and the thermostat turns the heating off to be safe. Contact Dormouse support in the app under Help > Contact support.

## Restore the network and account connection

A replacement thermostat must be paired to connect it to Wi-Fi and your Dormouse account. Pairing requires the Dormouse app and a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network.

1. Hold the dial down for 5 seconds, until the display shows PAIR.
2. In the app, choose Add thermostat and follow the steps.
3. Keep your phone within 5 metres of the thermostat until the app says pairing has finished.

Pairing must finish within 3 minutes of PAIR appearing. If it takes longer, the display shows E-104. Hold the dial for 5 seconds to start pairing again, and keep the phone within range.

If the Wi-Fi network's name or password has changed, pair the thermostat again. For E-101, check that the router is on and offers a 2.4 GHz network.

## Restore schedules and household access

Schedules are managed in the app or the web dashboard. After pairing, check that the replacement thermostat has the intended schedule. Each day can have up to six periods. On the thermostat, Menu > Schedule shows today's periods, but changes must be made in the app or web dashboard.

Check that the thermostat is assigned to the correct zone and that any Dormouse Room Sensors or Dormouse Radiator Valves for that zone are still available in the home. A home can have up to four thermostats, each controlling its own zone.

Household members can change temperatures, schedules and away mode. Only the owner can add or remove devices, invite members or remove them. If the replacement is not available to the household, the owner can check device settings in Settings > Home.

## Confirm heating and settings

Test the heating using the controls available to you, and check that the target temperature responds as expected. The boost button starts a one-hour boost at the configured boost temperature; press it again to cancel. Frost protection is always on and cannot be turned off.

Review the settings that affect day-to-day operation, including away mode and the boost temperature. The default away temperature is 16 degrees, and the default boost temperature is 21 degrees. Settings can be changed in the app or web dashboard.

If the thermostat shows E-110, it cannot reach the Dormouse cloud. Check that the router has an internet connection. The thermostat continues to run schedules, but changes made in the app wait until it reconnects.

## Final checks and support

Confirm that the thermostat is online in the app, that the correct home and zone are shown, and that the schedule and household access are as intended. Explain that the display dims after 20 seconds without a touch; turning the dial wakes it and changes the target temperature.

For other error codes, follow the action given in the user guide. E-305 indicates that the boiler is not responding; check that it is switched on and its pressure is between 1 and 1.5 bar, then check the boiler's own display. For E-312, refer to the boiler's manual or call a registered gas engineer.

Contact Dormouse support in the app under Help > Contact support.
