# Android app: Account settings screen guide

## About this guide

This guide covers account settings in the Dormouse app for Android and iOS, and the web dashboard. Most settings are available in all three. Menu paths are the same in the app and web dashboard.

The guide applies to the Dormouse Thermostat with firmware 4.1. To check the thermostat's firmware, press the dial to open the menu, then choose Menu > About.

## Sign in

Sign in to the app or web dashboard with your email address and password. A password must have at least 10 characters.

If you forget your password, choose Forgot password? on the sign-in screen and enter your email address. You will receive a password reset link. The link works for 30 minutes and can only be used once. If it expires, request a new link.

## Two-step verification

Two-step verification adds a 6-digit code to your password when you sign in on a new phone or browser. The code is sent by email, or comes from an authenticator app if you have set one up.

To turn on two-step verification, go to Settings > Account > Two-step verification and follow the instructions on screen. You will need the code as well as your password when signing in on a new device or browser.

Two-step verification is an account setting. It does not change how the thermostat controls heating, follows schedules or connects to Wi-Fi.

## Account settings

Go to Settings > Account to find account options. Two-step verification is under Settings > Account > Two-step verification.

Account sign-in uses your email address and password. Use the password reset process if you cannot remember your password. Reset links expire after 30 minutes and can be used once.

## Household and home settings

Account settings apply to your sign-in. Home settings are separate and control access and devices for a home.

A home has one owner and can have up to eight members. Members can change the temperature, schedules and away mode. Only the owner can add or remove devices, invite members or remove them.

To invite a member, go to Settings > Household > Invite and enter their email address. The invitation link works for 7 days. If it expires, the owner must send a new invitation.

## Location sharing and geofencing

Each household member chooses whether to share their phone's location in the app. When geofencing is enabled, away mode turns on when every member who shares their location is more than 1 km from home. It turns off when the first of them comes back within 1 km.

To stop geofencing for the whole home, go to Settings > Away > Geofencing. Phone locations are not stored; the system keeps only whether each member is home or away.

## Notifications

Each household member chooses their own notifications in Settings > Notifications. The app can notify you about thermostat error codes, low backup batteries, heating that has been on for 2 hours while everyone is away, installed firmware updates and monthly energy reports.

A notification setting applies to the member who changes it. Other members choose their own notification preferences.

## Help and support

For help with the app or account, go to Help > Contact support in the app.

If the thermostat shows an error code, the app shows the same code. Check the code in the thermostat user guide for its meaning and recommended action.
