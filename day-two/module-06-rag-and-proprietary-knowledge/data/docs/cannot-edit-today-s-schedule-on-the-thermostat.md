# Cannot Edit Today's Schedule on the Thermostat

## What you can do on the thermostat

The thermostat can show you today's schedule, but it cannot be used to change the schedule. Use the app or web dashboard to edit schedule periods.

On the thermostat, open the menu by pressing the dial when the display shows the room temperature. Turn the dial to **Schedule** and press it to see today's periods. This view is for checking the schedule; it does not provide controls for editing it.

## Change the schedule in the app

1. Open the Dormouse app and choose the home with the thermostat whose schedule you want to change.
2. Open the **Schedule** tab.
3. Choose the day you want to edit.
4. Change the period start times or target temperatures as needed.

Each day can have up to six periods. A period has a start time and a target temperature, and lasts until the next period starts.

## Change the schedule in the web dashboard

1. Sign in to the Dormouse web dashboard.
2. Open the **Schedule** tab.
3. Choose the day you want to edit.
4. Change the period start times or target temperatures as needed.

Most settings can be changed in the app or web dashboard. The schedule is one of the settings that must be edited in one of these, rather than on the thermostat.

## Apply the same periods to another day

If you want to use the same periods on more than one day, edit the schedule for one day in the app or web dashboard, then choose **Copy day** and select the day or days to copy it to.

Check the selected days before leaving the schedule. Copying a day sets those days to use the copied periods.

## Understand a manual temperature change

Turning the thermostat dial changes the target temperature; it does not edit a schedule period. A temperature change made by hand lasts until the next schedule period starts.

To change what the thermostat is scheduled to do at a particular time, edit the relevant day in the app or web dashboard. To make a temporary change until the next period, use the dial or the app to set the target temperature.

## Check which thermostat controls the zone

A home can have up to four Dormouse Thermostats, each controlling its own zone with its own schedule. If your home has more than one thermostat, make sure you are viewing the schedule for the thermostat and zone you intend to change.

A Dormouse Room Sensor can measure the temperature in another room of a zone, and Dormouse Radiator Valves can control radiators in that zone. These devices do not replace the zone thermostat's schedule.

## If changes do not appear

Allow time for the app or web dashboard to show the updated schedule. If the thermostat is not connected to the Dormouse cloud, changes made in the app wait until it reconnects. Check for an error code on the display or in the app.

For **E-110**, check that your router is connected to the internet. The thermostat keeps running its schedules while it cannot reach the Dormouse cloud, but app changes wait until it reconnects. For other error codes, follow the guidance in the thermostat user guide.

## Get help

If you have checked the correct thermostat and zone, edited the schedule in the app or web dashboard, and the change still does not appear, contact Dormouse support in the app under **Help > Contact support**.
