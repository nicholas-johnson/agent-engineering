# Understand thermostat temperature accuracy

## What the accuracy figure means

The Dormouse Thermostat has a temperature accuracy of 0.3 degrees. This describes how closely its measured temperature can match the actual room temperature. It is not a guarantee that the temperature shown will always be exactly the same as a reading from another thermometer.

The thermostat displays the room temperature in large numbers and the target temperature underneath. The target is the temperature you have set; the room temperature is the thermostat's measurement of the room.

## Room temperature and target temperature

The two readings serve different purposes:

- **Room temperature** is the temperature measured by the thermostat.
- **Target temperature** is the temperature the thermostat is set to reach.

Changing the target does not change the room temperature reading immediately. Turn the dial or use the app to change the target. A manual change lasts until the next schedule period starts.

The target can be set from 5 to 30 degrees, in half-degree steps.

## Why readings can differ

A reading from another thermometer may differ from the thermostat's reading. The accuracy figure describes the thermostat's temperature measurement; it does not mean that every other thermometer will show the same value. Readings may also be taken at different positions in the home, where the temperature can differ.

A Dormouse Room Sensor can measure temperature in another room of the same zone. It also measures humidity. Up to three Room Sensors can join a zone. A Dormouse Radiator Valve can also join a zone and heat its radiator to its own temperature.

The thermostat, Room Sensor and Radiator Valve may therefore measure or control conditions in different places. Their readings should be understood in relation to where each device is installed.

## Using a Room Sensor

To add a Room Sensor to a zone, go to Settings > Home > Add a device. A sensor measures the temperature and humidity in the room where it is placed. It communicates with the thermostat for its zone over a low-power radio link; it does not use Wi-Fi.

A zone can include one Room Sensor in addition to its thermostat, with up to three Room Sensors per zone. A home can have up to four thermostats, each controlling its own zone and schedule.

## Heating behaviour and temperature readings

The thermostat controls heating based on its temperature measurement and the active target. The display shows a flame when the thermostat is asking the boiler for heat.

Frost protection is always on. If the room falls below 5 degrees, the thermostat turns on the heating, even when heating is off or away mode is on. A snowflake appears while frost protection is heating.

A boost heats the home at the boost temperature for one hour. The boost temperature is 21 degrees unless changed in Settings > Heating > Boost temperature.

## If a reading seems wrong

Check that you are comparing the room temperature with another room-temperature reading, rather than with the target temperature. If the readings come from different rooms or positions, they may not match.

If the display shows E-217, the temperature sensor is disconnected. The thermostat turns the heating off to be safe. Contact Dormouse support in the app under Help > Contact support to arrange a replacement.

For other error codes, check the thermostat's error-code guidance. The app shows the same code as the thermostat and can notify you about it.

## Caring for the thermostat

Use the thermostat indoors, between 0 and 40 degrees. To clean it, wipe it with a soft, dry cloth. Do not use sprays or cleaning products, and do not paint over it.

The thermostat is powered by mains through its wall plate. Two AA backup batteries keep the clock and schedules during a power cut for up to 48 hours, but the thermostat cannot switch the heating on while mains power is off. If the display shows E-402, mains power has been interrupted and the thermostat is running on its backup batteries.
