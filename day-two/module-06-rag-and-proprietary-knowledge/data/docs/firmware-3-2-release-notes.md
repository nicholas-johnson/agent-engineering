# Firmware 3.2 release notes

Released 26 May 2025.

## Monthly energy reports

Monthly energy reports are available in the new Energy tab in the app. Reports arrive on the 1st of each month and show the heating hours for each day, compared with the previous month, plus an estimate of gas used in kWh.

The estimate depends on your boiler size. Enter it at Settings > Home > Boiler size.

## Dormouse cloud connection

The thermostat now displays E-110 when it cannot reach the Dormouse cloud. Before firmware 3.2, the Wi-Fi symbol flashed to indicate this.

When E-110 appears, check that your router is connected to the internet. The thermostat continues running its schedules, but changes made in the app wait until the thermostat reconnects.
