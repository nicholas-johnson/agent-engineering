# Radiator Valve Fitment on M30 x 1.5 Valve Bodies

## Purpose and compatibility

This guide explains how to fit a Dormouse Radiator Valve to a compatible radiator valve body with an M30 x 1.5 connection. The Dormouse Radiator Valve controls the temperature of the radiator it is fitted to and joins a Dormouse Thermostat zone.

Check the valve body connection before starting. This guide applies to M30 x 1.5 valve bodies. The Dormouse Radiator Valve is supplied with adapters for other common valve types, but this guide does not cover fitting those adapters.

## Equipment and preparation

You will need a Dormouse Radiator Valve and two AA batteries. Check that the radiator valve body is accessible and that its connection is clean and undamaged.

The Dormouse Radiator Valve is battery powered. It does not need a mains power connection or a Wi-Fi connection. It communicates with the thermostat for its zone using a low-power 868 MHz radio link. Only the thermostat uses Wi-Fi.

Before fitting, identify the zone the radiator is intended to join. A zone can include up to ten Dormouse Radiator Valves. Each valve heats its own radiator to its own temperature.

## Check the valve body

Confirm that the radiator valve body has an M30 x 1.5 connection. Do not force the Dormouse Radiator Valve onto a connection that does not match.

Inspect the exposed connection and surrounding area. Remove dirt that could prevent the valve from seating properly. Do not use tools or methods that could damage the valve body.

If the connection is not M30 x 1.5, use the appropriate adapter supplied in the box for a compatible valve type. Do not assume that an adapter will fit every non-M30 connection.

## Fit the Dormouse Radiator Valve

1. Make sure the radiator valve body connection is accessible and ready for the valve.
2. Align the Dormouse Radiator Valve with the M30 x 1.5 connection.
3. Fit the valve onto the connection, keeping it straight as it engages.
4. Secure it so that it is seated on the valve body. Do not force it or overtighten it.
5. Check that the valve is stable and correctly aligned.

If the valve does not engage smoothly, stop and check the connection and alignment. Do not force the threads. Recheck that the valve body is M30 x 1.5 and that no dirt or obstruction is preventing a proper fit.

## Insert the batteries

The Dormouse Radiator Valve runs on two AA batteries. Fit the batteries in the valve, following the polarity markings inside its battery compartment. Use two AA batteries and make sure they are seated correctly before closing the compartment.

The batteries typically last about two years. Replace them when needed, using two AA batteries. The thermostat's backup battery instructions apply to the thermostat, not to the Radiator Valve.

## Add the valve to a zone

After fitting, add the Dormouse Radiator Valve to the intended zone using the app or web dashboard:

1. Open Settings > Home > Add a device.
2. Follow the instructions to add the valve.
3. Confirm that it is assigned to the correct zone.

The valve communicates with that zone's thermostat over a low-power 868 MHz radio link. The valve itself does not connect to Wi-Fi. If the home has more than one thermostat, take care to add the valve to the zone controlled by the thermostat intended for that radiator.

## Check operation

Once added to its zone, check that the Dormouse Radiator Valve is recognised in the app or web dashboard and is assigned to the intended zone. The zone's thermostat and schedule govern the zone, while each Radiator Valve heats its own radiator to its own temperature.

If the valve is not listed or does not appear to respond, check that it has two correctly installed AA batteries and that you added it to the intended zone. Consult the thermostat's user guide for details about adding devices and managing zones.

## Care and product limits

The Dormouse Radiator Valve uses two AA batteries, which typically last about two years. Keep the valve and its battery compartment in good condition, and replace the batteries when required.

The product information in this guide applies to fitting on M30 x 1.5 valve bodies. Use an appropriate supplied adapter for other common valve types only when it matches the valve body. Do not force a fit or use an incompatible connection.
