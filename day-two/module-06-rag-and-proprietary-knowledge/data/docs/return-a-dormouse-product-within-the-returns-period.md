# Return a Dormouse product within the returns period

## Returns period

You can return a Dormouse product within 30 days of purchase. This article explains how to prepare a product for return and what to check before you send it back.

## Before you return a thermostat

If you are returning a Dormouse Thermostat, remove it from your account and reset it before handing it over or sending it back. A factory reset removes the thermostat's Wi-Fi details, schedules and link to your account.

To reset it:

1. Hold the dial and the boost button down together for 10 seconds.
2. Release them when the display shows RESET.
3. Wait for the thermostat to restart.

After the reset, the thermostat is ready to be paired again.

## If you are moving house

If you are taking the thermostat with you, you may not need to reset it. A factory reset is intended for when you give the thermostat to someone else or move house without it. Resetting removes the schedules and account link, so make sure you want to remove those before continuing.

## Prepare other products

Remove a Dormouse Radiator Valve or Dormouse Room Sensor from its zone before returning it, if it is part of your home setup. The thermostat, valves and sensors work together as a zone, and removing a device helps prevent it remaining associated with the setup.

Handle batteries carefully when preparing a product. The thermostat has two AA backup batteries behind its front cover. A Radiator Valve uses two AA batteries, and a Room Sensor uses one CR2032 coin cell.

## Get help with your return

Contact Dormouse support in the app under Help > Contact support. Include the product you want to return and when you purchased it, so the support team can help with the next steps.

The 30-day returns period applies to products. If you are unsure whether your purchase is still within the period, contact support through the app.
