# Understand heating history retention

## What heating history shows

Dormouse keeps heating history for two years. This history records how many hours the heating was on each day. It helps you review heating activity over time.

Heating history is separate from the monthly energy report. The report arrives in the app's Energy tab on the 1st of each month. It compares the number of hours the heating was on each day with the previous month and estimates gas use in kWh.

## How long history is kept

Heating history is kept for two years. Information older than that is not retained as heating history.

The two-year retention period applies to heating history; it does not change your thermostat's schedules or settings. Each thermostat in a home controls its own zone and has its own schedule.

## View a monthly energy report

To see a monthly energy report:

1. Open the Dormouse app.
2. Select the Energy tab.
3. Open the report for the month you want to review.

Reports arrive on the 1st of each month. They show daily heating hours compared with the month before, along with an estimate of gas used in kWh. The estimate depends on your boiler size. To enter or update it, go to Settings > Home > Boiler size.

## History and thermostat operation

Heating history is a record of heating activity. Your thermostat continues to control heating according to its settings and schedule; keeping history for two years does not extend or change a schedule.

A schedule can have up to six periods per day. On the thermostat, Menu > Schedule shows today's periods. To change periods, use the Schedule tab in the app or web dashboard.

## History, reports and phone locations

Heating history and energy reports are different features. The history is retained for two years, while the monthly report summarises heating hours and estimated gas use for a month.

Geofencing uses the location of household members' phones to turn away mode on or off. Phone locations are never stored; Dormouse keeps only whether each member is home or away. This is separate from heating history retention.
