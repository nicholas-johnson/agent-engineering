# Understand boiler pressure checks for a boiler fault

## When to check boiler pressure

If the thermostat shows E-305, it means the boiler is not responding. The user guide says to check that the boiler is switched on and its pressure is between 1 and 1.5 bar. You can also check the boiler's own display for a fault.

Boiler pressure is shown on the boiler, not on the Dormouse Thermostat. This article explains how to find and read that pressure. Boiler controls and displays vary, so use the instructions supplied with your boiler if you are unsure how to check it.

## Find the pressure reading

Look for a pressure gauge or pressure reading on the boiler. It may be on the front panel or behind a flap. Some boilers show pressure on a digital display; others use a dial.

If you cannot find the reading, consult the boiler's manual. Do not remove the boiler casing to look for it.

## Read the gauge

A dial gauge usually has a needle and a scale marked in bar. Read the number the needle points to. A digital display may show the pressure directly, sometimes with "bar" beside the number.

The range to check for E-305 is 1 to 1.5 bar. For example, a reading of 1.2 bar is within this range. If the reading is below 1 bar or above 1.5 bar, it is outside the range stated in the Dormouse guide.

## If the pressure is between 1 and 1.5 bar

If the boiler is switched on and its pressure is between 1 and 1.5 bar, check the boiler's own display for a fault. The boiler manual explains what its fault indication means.

If the boiler reports a fault, look up that fault in its manual or call a registered gas engineer. Do not assume that a pressure reading within range means the boiler has no other fault.

## If the pressure is outside the range

If the pressure is below 1 bar or above 1.5 bar, follow the instructions for your boiler. Boiler designs differ, and the correct steps depend on the model. Do not adjust the pressure unless you know how to do so safely and the boiler's instructions tell you what to do.

If you cannot resolve the boiler issue using its instructions, contact a registered gas engineer. Do not open the boiler casing or attempt repairs to the boiler yourself.

## Check the thermostat after checking the boiler

Once you have checked that the boiler is switched on, checked its pressure and looked at its own display, allow the thermostat to try the heating again. If E-305 remains or the boiler still does not respond, contact Dormouse support in the app under Help > Contact support.

E-305 relates to the boiler not responding. If the boiler's own display reports a fault, use the boiler manual or contact a registered gas engineer about that fault.
