# Understand thermostat cleaning and care

## Clean the thermostat

Wipe the thermostat with a soft, dry cloth. This is all you need to clean its surface.

## Avoid sprays and cleaning products

Do not spray anything on the thermostat or use cleaning products. Apply the dry cloth directly to the surface instead.

## Do not paint over the thermostat

Do not paint over the thermostat. Keep its surface clear of paint.
