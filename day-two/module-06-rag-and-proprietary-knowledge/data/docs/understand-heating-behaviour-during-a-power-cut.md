# Understand heating behaviour during a power cut

## What happens when mains power is interrupted

The Dormouse Thermostat needs mains power to switch the heating on. During a power cut, it cannot turn the heating on, even if the room temperature falls below the target temperature or frost protection needs to heat the home.

The thermostat uses its backup batteries to keep the clock and your schedules for up to 48 hours. These batteries help preserve your schedule information during the interruption, but they do not keep the heating running.

## The E-402 message

When mains power is interrupted, the thermostat displays E-402. The app shows the same error code and can notify you if you have enabled notifications.

E-402 means the thermostat is running on its backup batteries. While this message is showing, the clock and schedules are kept for up to 48 hours, but the thermostat cannot switch on the heating.

## What to expect from the heating

The thermostat cannot call for heat while mains power is off. This applies regardless of the target temperature, schedule, or whether away mode is on. Frost protection is normally always on, but it also cannot switch on the heating without mains power.

When mains power returns, the thermostat can switch the heating on again. If E-402 remains after power has been restored, check that the thermostat has mains power.

## Backup battery duration

Two AA backup batteries keep the clock and schedules during a power cut for up to 48 hours. The duration is limited: the batteries do not provide heating control while mains power is off.

Replace the batteries once a year, or when the display shows E-401. When E-401 appears, replace the two AA batteries behind the front cover within a month. To reach them, gently pull the bottom of the front cover towards you.

## After a longer power cut

The backup batteries preserve the clock and schedules for up to 48 hours. If the power cut lasts longer, the guide does not guarantee that the clock and schedules will remain preserved. Check the time and schedule after power returns, and correct them in the app or web dashboard if needed.

You can view today's periods on the thermostat by choosing Menu > Schedule. Schedule changes can only be made in the app or web dashboard.
