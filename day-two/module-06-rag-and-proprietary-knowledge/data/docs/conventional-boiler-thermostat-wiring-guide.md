# Conventional Boiler Thermostat Wiring Guide

## Scope and safety

This guide covers connecting a Dormouse Thermostat to a conventional gas boiler. The thermostat must be wired to the boiler by a qualified installer. Do not attempt to connect or alter mains wiring unless you are qualified to do so.

The thermostat is powered from the mains through its wall plate. Wiring arrangements vary between boilers and installations. Use the boiler manufacturer's wiring diagram and the wiring information supplied for the specific installation. Do not assume that wire colours or terminal positions are the same across systems.

This guide does not provide a universal terminal-by-terminal wiring scheme. If the boiler or existing wiring cannot be identified with confidence, stop work and have a qualified installer assess the installation.

## System compatibility

The Dormouse Thermostat works with gas combi, system and conventional boilers. It controls one heating zone; each Dormouse Thermostat in a home controls its own zone and has its own schedule.

Before installation, confirm that the boiler is a compatible gas boiler and that the thermostat is to be installed as the controller for the intended zone. Check the boiler's installation information to identify its control connections and the required wiring arrangement.

The thermostat requires a Wi-Fi network on 2.4 GHz for pairing and app connectivity. Wi-Fi is not part of the boiler control wiring. Dormouse Radiator Valves and Dormouse Room Sensors communicate with their zone's thermostat over a low-power 868 MHz radio link; only the thermostat uses Wi-Fi.

## Thermostat installation details

The thermostat is 84 mm across and 24 mm deep, with a 2.4-inch round colour display. It is intended for indoor use between 0 and 40 degrees and is accurate to 0.3 degrees.

The thermostat is powered by mains electricity through its wall plate. Two AA backup batteries, fitted behind the front cover, keep the clock and schedules during a power cut for up to 48 hours. They do not allow the thermostat to switch the heating on while mains power is off.

The backup batteries are not the thermostat's normal power supply. Replace them once a year, or when the display shows E-401. To access them, pull the bottom of the front cover gently towards you.

## Before connecting the wiring

1. Identify the boiler type and the heating zone the thermostat will control.
2. Check the boiler's wiring diagram and installation instructions for the correct control connections.
3. Identify the existing heating controls and wiring arrangement before disconnecting anything.
4. Confirm that the thermostat location is indoors and within its specified operating range.
5. Make sure the installation has a suitable 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network available for setup.

Use the boiler manufacturer's instructions for electrical isolation and safe working procedures. Do not rely on the thermostat display as proof that wiring is isolated. If the existing wiring or boiler connections do not match the information available, do not guess or proceed by trial and error.

## Connecting to a conventional boiler

A conventional boiler installation must be wired by a qualified installer. Follow the boiler manufacturer's wiring diagram and the Dormouse installation information for the thermostat wall plate. Connect the thermostat only to the specified boiler control connections and power supply.

Do not infer terminal functions from labels or wire colours alone. Do not connect the thermostat to boiler terminals that are not identified for the required control arrangement. Where an existing programmer, room thermostat or other control is present, the installer must determine how it is arranged and whether it is to remain in use.

After making the connections, check the wiring against the relevant diagrams before restoring power. Secure the wall plate and thermostat as instructed for the installation. If the boiler reports a fault after wiring, consult the boiler's own display and manual; do not assume the thermostat is the cause.

## Checking operation

When power is restored, confirm that the thermostat starts normally and that the boiler responds to a heating demand. Turn the dial to set a target temperature, or use the app. The target temperature can be set from 5 to 30 degrees in half-degree steps.

The flame symbol means the thermostat is asking the boiler for heat. A change made by hand lasts until the next schedule period starts. The thermostat can also run a one-hour boost at the configured boost temperature. The default boost temperature is 21 degrees; it can be changed in Settings > Heating > Boost temperature.

Check that the control operates the intended zone and that the boiler behaves as expected under the boiler manufacturer's commissioning instructions. If the boiler does not respond, check that it is switched on and that its pressure is between 1 and 1.5 bar. Then check the boiler's own display for a fault.

## Pairing and setup

Pairing connects the thermostat to the home's Wi-Fi and Dormouse account. The thermostat needs a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network; it cannot join a 5 GHz network. Pair it using the Dormouse app on a phone running Android 12 or later, or iOS 17 or later.

1. Hold the dial down for 5 seconds, until the display shows PAIR.
2. In the app, choose Add thermostat and follow the steps.
3. Keep the phone within 5 metres of the thermostat until the app confirms that pairing has finished.

Pairing must finish within 3 minutes of PAIR appearing. If the display shows E-104, hold the dial for 5 seconds to start again and keep the phone within range.

## Commissioning the zone

Set up the schedule in the Schedule tab in the app or web dashboard. Each day can have up to six periods. On the thermostat, Menu > Schedule shows today's periods, but schedules can only be changed in the app or web dashboard.

A zone can include one thermostat, up to ten Dormouse Radiator Valves and up to three Dormouse Room Sensors. Add devices in Settings > Home > Add a device. Each thermostat controls its own zone and schedule.

Check that the displayed room temperature and target temperature are shown as expected. If the temperature sensor disconnects, E-217 appears and the thermostat turns the heating off to be safe. Contact Dormouse support in the app under Help > Contact support to arrange a replacement.

## Faults and handover

Use the display and app error code to identify problems. E-305 means the boiler is not responding: check that the boiler is on, its pressure is between 1 and 1.5 bar, and its own display shows no fault. E-312 means the boiler reports a fault; consult the boiler's manual or call a registered gas engineer.

E-110 means the thermostat cannot reach the Dormouse cloud. Check the router's internet connection. The thermostat continues running its schedules, but app changes wait until it reconnects. E-402 means mains power is interrupted; the thermostat can keep its clock and schedules for up to 48 hours on backup batteries, but cannot switch the heating on.

At handover, explain the controls, schedule setup, away mode and boost. Leave the boiler and thermostat operating information with the customer. For thermostat support, use Help > Contact support in the app.
