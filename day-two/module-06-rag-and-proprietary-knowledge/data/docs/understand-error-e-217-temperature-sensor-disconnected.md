# Understand error E-217: Temperature sensor disconnected

## What E-217 means

E-217 means the thermostat's temperature sensor is disconnected. Without a temperature reading, the thermostat cannot reliably control the heating.

## What happens to the heating

For safety, the thermostat turns the heating off while this error is present. Do not rely on the thermostat to heat your home until the issue has been resolved.

## Where you will see the error

The thermostat display shows E-217. The app shows the same error code and can notify you about it if you have enabled error notifications.

## Arrange a replacement

Contact Dormouse support to arrange a replacement. In the app, go to Help > Contact support. Include the E-217 code when you contact support so they know which issue you are reporting.

## After the replacement

Follow the instructions provided with the replacement to get your heating working again. The thermostat controls its own zone; if your home has other thermostats, they control their respective zones separately.
