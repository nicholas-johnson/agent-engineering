# Add a thermostat, room sensor or radiator valve

You can add a Dormouse Thermostat, Dormouse Room Sensor or Dormouse Radiator Valve to a home in the Dormouse app or web dashboard. The home can have up to four thermostats. Each thermostat controls its own zone, with its own schedule.

## Before you add a device

You need to be the home owner to add devices. Members can change temperatures, schedules and away mode, but only the owner can add or remove devices.

Check that the device is ready to be set up and that you can access the home in the app or web dashboard. To begin, go to Settings > Home > Add a device and choose the device you want to add.

## Add a thermostat

A thermostat controls a zone and connects to your Wi-Fi network and Dormouse account. It must be wired to a compatible gas boiler by a qualified installer.

When you add a thermostat, follow the setup steps in the app or web dashboard. To pair it:

1. On the thermostat, hold the dial down for 5 seconds, until the display shows PAIR.
2. In the app, choose Add thermostat and follow the steps.
3. Keep your phone within 5 metres of the thermostat until the app says setup has finished.

Pairing must finish within 3 minutes of PAIR appearing. If it does not, the thermostat shows E-104. Start pairing again by holding the dial down for 5 seconds.

The thermostat needs a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network; it cannot join a 5 GHz network. Once added, it controls its own zone and schedule.

## Add a room sensor

A Dormouse Room Sensor measures temperature and humidity in another room of a zone. Choose the zone you want it to join when you add it.

A zone can have up to three Room Sensors. The sensor communicates with its zone's thermostat over a low-power 868 MHz radio link. It does not use Wi-Fi.

The Room Sensor uses one CR2032 coin cell, which lasts about a year. Replace the battery when needed to keep the sensor operating.

## Add a radiator valve

A Dormouse Radiator Valve lets a radiator in a zone heat to its own temperature. Choose the zone you want the valve to join when you add it.

A zone can have up to ten Radiator Valves. Each valve communicates with its zone's thermostat over a low-power 868 MHz radio link. The valve does not use Wi-Fi.

The valve fits M30 x 1.5 valve bodies. Adapters for other common valve types are included in the box. It runs on two AA batteries, which last about two years.

## Choose the right zone

Each thermostat controls its own zone, with its own schedule. For example, one thermostat can control downstairs radiators and another can control upstairs radiators.

Add a Room Sensor or Radiator Valve to the zone whose thermostat should communicate with it. A sensor measures temperature elsewhere in that zone; a valve controls the temperature at its radiator. Valves and sensors communicate with the thermostat for their zone, rather than connecting directly to Wi-Fi.

## Check the device limits

A home can have up to four Dormouse Thermostats. Each zone can have up to three Dormouse Room Sensors and up to ten Dormouse Radiator Valves.

If you have reached a limit, you cannot add another device of that type within the home or zone. Check which devices are already assigned before starting setup.

## If setup does not finish

For a thermostat, check that the phone stays within 5 metres and that pairing finishes within 3 minutes of PAIR appearing. If the display shows E-104, hold the dial down for 5 seconds and start again.

If a thermostat cannot find your Wi-Fi network, check that the router is on and offers a 2.4 GHz network. The thermostat cannot connect to 5 GHz Wi-Fi.

If you need help adding a device, contact Dormouse support in the app under Help > Contact support.
