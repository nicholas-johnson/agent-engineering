# iOS app: Household screen guide

## About this guide

This guide explains how the Household screen works in the Dormouse iOS app. It covers who can invite or remove household members and which home controls members can use.

A home has one owner and can have up to eight members. The owner can invite members and remove them. Members can change the temperature, schedules and away mode.

## Open the Household screen

In the app, go to Settings > Household. The owner can use this screen to invite someone or remove a member. Members can view the household but cannot manage its membership.

The home owner is responsible for household invitations and membership. Other home controls are available to members as described below.

## Invite a member

Only the owner can invite someone to the home. To send an invitation:

1. Go to Settings > Household > Invite.
2. Enter the person's email address.
3. Send the invitation.

The person receives an email with a link to join the home. The invitation works for 7 days. If it expires before they join, the owner needs to send a new invitation.

A home can have up to eight members, in addition to its owner.

## Remove a member

Only the owner can remove a member from the household. Members cannot remove themselves or other members using the Household screen. If a member needs to leave the home, ask the owner to remove them.

## Controls available to members

Members can use the app to:

- Change the target temperature.
- Change the home's schedules.
- Turn away mode on or off.

These permissions apply to members of the home. They do not give members permission to manage household membership or add and remove devices.

## Location sharing and geofencing

Each household member chooses in the app whether to share their location. When members share their location, geofencing can turn away mode on or off based on whether members are near home.

Away mode turns on when every member who shares their location is more than 1 km from home. It turns off when the first of them comes back within 1 km. To stop geofencing for the whole home, go to Settings > Away > Geofencing.

Phone locations are not stored. The system keeps only whether each member is home or away.

## Notifications

Each member chooses their own notifications in Settings > Notifications. A member's notification choices do not change the notification settings of other household members.

The app can notify a member when the thermostat shows an error code, the backup batteries are low, the heating has been on for 2 hours while everyone is away, a firmware update has been installed, or a monthly energy report is ready.

## Screen access by role

| Action | Owner | Member |
|---|---:|---:|
| Invite a member | Yes | No |
| Remove a member | Yes | No |
| Change the temperature | Yes | Yes |
| Change schedules | Yes | Yes |
| Change away mode | Yes | Yes |
| Add or remove devices | Yes | No |

Only the owner can add or remove devices. To add a thermostat, room sensor or radiator valve, go to Settings > Home > Add a device.

## If an invitation expires

An invitation is valid for 7 days. After it expires, it can no longer be used to join the home. The owner can return to Settings > Household > Invite and send a new invitation.

The owner can also use the Household screen to manage membership. Members can continue to change the temperature, schedules and away mode, but they cannot invite or remove people.
