# Understand the thermostat display symbols

The display uses symbols to show what the thermostat is doing or whether it needs attention. This article explains what each symbol indicates.

## Flame: heating requested

The flame means the thermostat is asking the boiler for heat. It indicates a heating request from the thermostat; it does not describe the boiler's condition.

## Sunrise: Warm Wake

The sunrise symbol means Warm Wake is heating ahead of a schedule period. You may see it while Warm Wake is active.

## Snowflake: frost protection

The snowflake appears while frost protection is heating. Frost protection turns the heating on if the room temperature falls below 5 degrees, even when heating is off or away mode is on. It is always on and cannot be turned off.

## House with an arrow: away mode

The house with an arrow means away mode is on. Away mode keeps the home at the away temperature until you turn it off. The away temperature is 16 degrees unless you change it in Settings > Away > Away temperature.

## Upward arrow: boost

The upward arrow means a boost is running. A boost heats the home at the boost temperature for one hour. The boost temperature is 21 degrees unless you change it in Settings > Heating > Boost temperature. Press the boost button again to cancel the boost.

## Wi-Fi symbol with a cross: not connected to Wi-Fi

The Wi-Fi symbol with a cross means the thermostat is not connected to Wi-Fi. If the thermostat shows E-101, check that your router is on and offers a 2.4 GHz network. If you have changed the network name or password, pair the thermostat again.

## Battery: backup batteries low

The battery symbol means the backup batteries are low. Replace the two AA batteries behind the front cover within a month. Pull the bottom of the front cover gently towards you to reach them.
