# Check thermostat dimensions, display and accuracy

## Thermostat dimensions

The Dormouse Thermostat is 84 mm across and 24 mm deep. Check that there is enough space on the wall for the thermostat before installation.

## Display

The thermostat has a 2.4-inch round colour display. It shows the room temperature in large numbers, with the target temperature underneath. Turn the dial to make the target temperature larger while you change it.

After 20 seconds without a touch, the display dims.

## Temperature accuracy

The thermostat's stated temperature accuracy is 0.3 degrees.
