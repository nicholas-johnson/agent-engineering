# Turn away mode on or off

Away mode keeps your home at the away temperature until you turn it off. The away temperature is 16 degrees unless you change it.

## Turn away mode on in the app

1. Open the Dormouse app.
2. Tap **Away**.

Away mode turns on and the thermostat maintains the away temperature.

## Turn away mode on at the thermostat

1. Press the dial to open the menu.
2. Turn the dial to **Away**.
3. Press the dial to choose it.

## How the away temperature works

While away mode is on, the thermostat keeps your home at the away temperature instead of following its usual schedule. The default is 16 degrees. To set a different temperature, go to **Settings > Away > Away temperature** in the app or web dashboard.

Frost protection remains active in away mode. If the room falls below 5 degrees, the thermostat turns the heating on to help stop the pipes freezing.

## Turn away mode off

In the app, tap **Away** to turn away mode off. On the thermostat, press the dial to open the menu, choose **Away**, and press the dial.

When away mode is off, the thermostat returns to its normal schedule. A temperature change you make by hand lasts until the next schedule period starts.

## Geofencing

If geofencing is enabled, it can turn away mode on when every household member who shares their location is more than 1 km from home. It turns away mode off when the first of them comes back within 1 km. Each member chooses whether to share their location in the app.

To stop geofencing for the whole home, go to **Settings > Away > Geofencing**.
