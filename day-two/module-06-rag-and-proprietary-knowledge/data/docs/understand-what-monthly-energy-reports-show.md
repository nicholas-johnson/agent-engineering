# Understand what monthly energy reports show

## Where to find your report

Your energy report arrives on the 1st of each month in the Energy tab of the Dormouse app. It summarises your heating use for the previous month.

The report includes:

- How many hours the heating was on each day
- A comparison with the month before
- An estimate of the gas used, in kWh

## Daily heating hours

The report shows the number of hours the heating was on for each day of the month. Use this to see how heating use varied from day to day.

These figures are hours of heating, not a measurement of the temperature in your home or the amount of gas used.

## Comparison with the month before

The report compares the daily heating hours with those from the previous month. This helps you see how the amount of time the heating was on changed between the two months.

The comparison is based on heating hours. It does not show a comparison of gas use between the months.

## Estimated gas use

The report also gives an estimate of the gas used, measured in kWh. This is an estimate rather than a direct measurement. It depends on the size of your boiler, so the result may not reflect your use accurately if the boiler size saved for your home is wrong.

To enter or update the boiler size, go to **Settings > Home > Boiler size**.

## Reading the report

Consider the figures together:

- Daily heating hours show how long the heating was on each day.
- The comparison shows how daily heating hours differ from the month before.
- Estimated gas use gives an approximate amount of gas used across the report period.

The daily hours and the gas estimate describe different things. Heating hours are recorded as time; gas use is estimated from your boiler size.

## Report availability and history

A new report is available on the 1st of each month in the app's Energy tab. Heating history is kept for two years.

If a report's estimated gas use seems unexpected, check that the boiler size in **Settings > Home > Boiler size** is correct.
