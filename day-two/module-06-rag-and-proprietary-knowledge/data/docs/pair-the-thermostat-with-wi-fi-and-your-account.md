# Pair the thermostat with Wi-Fi and your account

Pairing connects your Dormouse Thermostat to your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network and your Dormouse account. Use the Dormouse app to complete pairing.

## Before you start

Check that you have:

- A Wi-Fi network on 2.4 GHz. The thermostat cannot join a 5 GHz network.
- The Dormouse app on a phone with Android 12 or later, or iOS 17 or later.
- A Dormouse account. You can create one in the app.

Keep your phone within 5 metres of the thermostat until the app confirms that pairing is complete.

## Start pairing on the thermostat

1. Hold the thermostat dial down for 5 seconds.
2. Wait for the display to show **PAIR**.

The thermostat is now ready to pair. Start the steps in the app while **PAIR** is showing.

## Complete pairing in the app

1. In the Dormouse app, choose **Add thermostat**.
2. Follow the steps shown in the app.
3. Keep your phone within 5 metres of the thermostat while the app completes pairing.
4. Wait for the app to say that pairing has finished.

Pairing connects the thermostat to both your Wi-Fi network and your Dormouse account. Do not leave the phone too far from the thermostat before the app confirms completion.

## Finish within three minutes

Pairing must finish within 3 minutes of the thermostat showing **PAIR**. If the time limit passes before the app completes pairing, the thermostat shows **E-104**. You will need to start pairing again.

## If pairing times out

If the thermostat shows **E-104**:

1. Hold the dial down for 5 seconds to start pairing again.
2. In the app, choose **Add thermostat** and follow the steps.
3. Keep your phone within 5 metres of the thermostat.
4. Complete pairing within 3 minutes of **PAIR** appearing.

If pairing times out again, check that your phone stays within range and that you can complete the app steps before the time limit.

## If the Wi-Fi network is not found

If the display shows **E-101**, check that your router is on and offers a 2.4 GHz network. The thermostat cannot join a 5 GHz network.

If you have changed your Wi-Fi network's name or password, pair the thermostat again.
