# Resolve error E-402: Mains power interrupted

## What E-402 means

E-402 means the thermostat's mains power has been interrupted. The thermostat is running on its two backup AA batteries, which keep the clock and your schedules for up to 48 hours.

While mains power is off, the thermostat cannot switch the heating on. The batteries preserve the clock and schedules; they do not power the heating.

## Understand the display

When E-402 appears, the thermostat is reporting that it is using its backup batteries. The app shows the same error code and can notify you about it.

The thermostat cannot call for heat while it is running on backup batteries. Your schedules are retained, but heating will not run until mains power is restored.

## Check the mains power

Check whether power to the thermostat has been interrupted. If the home has lost power, wait until it is restored. If power is available elsewhere in the home but the thermostat continues to show E-402, contact Dormouse support in the app under Help > Contact support.

The thermostat is wired to the boiler by a qualified installer. Do not open or alter the wiring to investigate the interruption.

## What the backup batteries preserve

The two AA batteries are behind the thermostat's front cover. During a power cut, they keep the clock and schedules for up to 48 hours. The thermostat cannot switch the heating on during this time.

Once mains power returns, the thermostat can resume controlling the heating according to its settings and schedule. If power remains off beyond the backup period, the batteries may no longer preserve the clock and schedules.

## Check the batteries

The thermostat displays E-401 when its backup batteries are low. If E-401 appears, replace the two AA batteries behind the front cover within a month.

To reach the batteries, gently pull the bottom of the front cover towards you. Replace the batteries once a year, or when E-401 appears. If E-402 remains after mains power is restored, contact Dormouse support in the app under Help > Contact support.

## Notifications

The app can notify you when the thermostat shows an error code. Each household member chooses their own notifications in Settings > Notifications. The app shows the same E-402 code as the thermostat.
