# Invite someone to your home

The owner of a Dormouse home can invite up to eight household members. Members can change the temperature, schedules and away mode. Only the owner can add or remove devices, invite members or remove other members.

## Send an invitation

1. Open the Dormouse app or web dashboard.
2. Go to Settings > Household > Invite.
3. Enter the person's email address.
4. Send the invitation.

The person will receive an email with a link to join your home.

## Invitation expiry

An invitation works for 7 days. If the person does not use the link in time, the owner needs to send a new invitation from Settings > Household > Invite.
