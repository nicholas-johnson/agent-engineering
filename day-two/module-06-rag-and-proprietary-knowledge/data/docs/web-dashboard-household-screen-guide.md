# Web dashboard: Household screen guide

## About this guide

This guide explains household members, invitations and permissions in the Dormouse web dashboard. The same household features are available in the Android and iOS apps.

A home has one owner and can have up to eight members. The owner can invite members and remove them. Members can change the temperature, schedules and away mode.

## Open Household

Open Settings > Household to view and manage household members. The owner can use this screen to invite someone or remove a member. Members can use the screen to see household information, but they cannot invite or remove people.

## Invite a member

Only the home owner can send invitations. To invite someone:

1. Go to Settings > Household > Invite.
2. Enter the invitee's email address.
3. Send the invitation.

The invited person receives an email with a link to join the household. The invitation is valid for 7 days. If it expires before the person joins, the owner needs to send a new invitation.

A home can have up to eight members. Check the household's existing membership before inviting another person.

## Invitation status and expiry

The Household screen shows the household's members. An invitation that has not been accepted does not make its recipient a household member.

Invitees must use the link in their invitation email to join. The link works for 7 days. After that, the invitation has expired and the owner must invite the person again.

If the person has not joined after 7 days, go to Settings > Household > Invite and send a new invitation. The new invitation has its own 7-day validity period.

## Household permissions

Household roles determine who can manage people and devices.

| Role | Permissions |
|---|---|
| Owner | Can change the temperature, schedules and away mode. Can add or remove devices, invite members and remove members. |
| Member | Can change the temperature, schedules and away mode. Cannot add or remove devices, invite members or remove members. |

There is one owner for each home. A home can have up to eight members in addition to its owner.

## Remove a member

Only the owner can remove a member. Use the Household screen to select the member and remove them. Members cannot remove themselves or other household members from this screen.

Removing a member ends their household access. It does not change the permissions of the owner or other members.

## Devices and household access

Device management is separate from everyday household controls. Members can adjust temperatures, edit schedules and turn away mode on or off, but only the owner can add or remove devices.

To manage devices, the owner goes to Settings > Home > Add a device. Household access does not give members permission to add or remove thermostats, sensors or radiator valves.

## Location sharing and geofencing

Each household member chooses in the app whether to share their phone's location. When geofencing is enabled, the thermostat uses the location-sharing status of household members to control away mode.

To stop geofencing for the whole home, go to Settings > Away > Geofencing. Phone locations are not stored; the system records only whether each member is home or away.

## Notifications and household settings

Each member chooses their own notifications in Settings > Notifications. A change to one member's notification preferences does not set preferences for everyone else in the household.

Household membership and permissions apply to the home. They do not replace each member's individual account sign-in or notification settings.
