# Understand who can manage a Dormouse home

## Roles in a home

Each Dormouse home has one owner and can have up to eight members. The owner and members have different permissions.

Members can change the temperature, schedules and away mode. Only the owner can manage devices and household membership.

## What members can do

Members can use the Dormouse app or web dashboard to:

- Change the temperature.
- Change schedules.
- Turn away mode on or off.

Each member also chooses their own notification preferences in Settings > Notifications. Household members choose individually whether to share their phone location for geofencing in the app.

## What only the owner can do

Only the owner can:

- Add or remove devices.
- Invite members.
- Remove members.

These permissions apply to the whole home. Members cannot add or remove a thermostat, Room Sensor or Radiator Valve, or manage who belongs to the household.

## Invite a member

To invite someone, go to Settings > Household > Invite and enter their email address. They receive an email with a link to join the home.

The invitation works for 7 days. If it expires before it is used, the owner needs to send a new invitation.

## Add or remove a device

The owner can add a thermostat, Room Sensor or Radiator Valve in Settings > Home > Add a device. A home can have up to four Dormouse Thermostats. Each thermostat controls its own zone.

Members cannot add or remove devices. Contact the owner if a device needs to be added to or removed from the home.

## Manage household membership

Only the owner can invite members or remove them. Members can change the temperature, schedules and away mode, but cannot manage the household membership.

A home can have up to eight members. If an invitation has expired, the owner can invite the person again using Settings > Household > Invite.

## How location sharing works

Geofencing uses the location of household members' phones to turn away mode on or off. Each member chooses whether to share their location in the app. Away mode turns on when every member who shares their location is more than 1 km from home, and turns off when the first of them comes back within 1 km.

To stop geofencing for the whole home, go to Settings > Away > Geofencing. This setting does not change who can add or remove devices or household members.

## Keep account access secure

Each person signs in to the Dormouse app and web dashboard with their own email address and password. Passwords must have at least 10 characters.

Two-step verification can be turned on in Settings > Account > Two-step verification. It asks for a 6-digit code as well as a password when someone signs in on a new phone or browser. The code comes by email, or from an authenticator app if one has been set up.
