# Understand error E-110: Cannot reach the Dormouse cloud

## What E-110 means

E-110 means your Dormouse Thermostat cannot reach the Dormouse cloud. The thermostat may still be connected to your Wi-Fi network, but it cannot currently communicate with the cloud.

The display shows E-110, and the app shows the same error code. The app can also notify you about the error if you have notifications enabled.

## What happens to your heating

Your thermostat keeps running its schedules while it cannot reach the Dormouse cloud. You do not need to reset it or re-enter your schedule just because E-110 appears.

The thermostat continues to control the heating according to its saved schedule. However, it cannot receive changes you make in the app until it reconnects.

## What happens to app changes

Changes made in the app while the thermostat is offline wait until it reconnects to the cloud. Once the connection is restored, the thermostat can receive those changes.

If you need to change the target temperature while the thermostat is offline, turn the dial on the thermostat. A temperature change you make by hand lasts until the next schedule period starts.

## Check your internet connection

Check that your router is connected to the internet. If it is not, restore the router's internet connection and allow time for the thermostat to reconnect.

The thermostat uses a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network. It cannot join a 5 GHz network. If the Wi-Fi network's name or password has changed, pair the thermostat again.

## When the error clears

E-110 clears when the thermostat can reach the Dormouse cloud again. Until then, schedules continue to run, but app changes wait.

If the thermostat shows a different error code, use that code to find the relevant guidance in the error-code list. For help with E-110, open the app and go to Help > Contact support.
