# Replace the Dormouse Room Sensor battery

## About the battery

The Dormouse Room Sensor uses one CR2032 coin cell. It typically lasts about a year. Battery life can vary with use, so replace the cell when it runs low.

## Before you start

Have a replacement CR2032 coin cell ready. Check that it is the correct type before opening the sensor. Do not use a different battery type.

## Replace the battery

1. Remove the sensor's cover to reach the battery compartment.
2. Take out the old CR2032 coin cell.
3. Fit the replacement CR2032 coin cell in the compartment, matching the polarity markings.
4. Refit the cover.

## Check the sensor

After replacing the battery, check that the sensor is back in place and connected to its zone. The Room Sensor measures temperature and humidity in another room of the zone. A zone can have up to three Dormouse Room Sensors.

Room Sensors communicate with their zone's thermostat over a low-power 868 MHz radio link. They do not connect to Wi-Fi.

## If readings do not return

Allow the sensor a short time to reconnect to its zone's thermostat. If its readings remain unavailable, check that the battery is fitted correctly and that the cover is secure. You can also check the thermostat and app for any displayed error or connection information.

For help, contact Dormouse support in the app under Help > Contact support.
