# Heating Starts Below Five Degrees

## What is happening

Frost protection is always on. If the room temperature falls below 5 degrees, the thermostat turns the heating on to help stop your pipes freezing.

This can happen even when the heating is off or Away mode is on. While frost protection is heating, a snowflake appears on the display.

## Check the room temperature

Look at the large number on the thermostat display. It shows the room temperature. The target temperature appears underneath.

If the room temperature is below 5 degrees, the heating is running because frost protection has activated. The thermostat will stop calling for heat when the room is no longer below this threshold.

## Check the display symbol

Look for the snowflake symbol. It appears while frost protection is heating.

Other symbols indicate different heating activity:

- A flame means the thermostat is asking the boiler for heat.
- An upward arrow means a boost is running.
- A sunrise means Warm Wake is heating ahead of a schedule period.

## Check whether heating is off or Away mode is on

Frost protection can still turn the heating on when you have switched the heating off or turned on Away mode. This is expected behaviour and frost protection cannot be turned off.

Away mode keeps the home at its away temperature, but it does not prevent frost protection from heating if the room falls below 5 degrees.

## If the room is not below five degrees

If the room temperature is 5 degrees or higher, check whether another heating setting is calling for heat:

1. Check the target temperature shown beneath the room temperature.
2. Check whether a boost is running. The display shows an upward arrow during a boost; press the boost button to cancel it.
3. Check today's schedule in Menu > Schedule on the thermostat. You can view today's periods there, but change them in the app or web dashboard.

A hand-set target temperature lasts until the next schedule period starts.

## If the heating does not stop

If the room is no longer below 5 degrees but the thermostat still appears to be asking the boiler for heat, check the display for an error code. The app shows the same code.

For example, E-305 means the boiler is not responding. Check that the boiler is switched on, its pressure is between 1 and 1.5 bar, and its own display shows no fault.

## Get help

If the behaviour does not match the guidance above, contact Dormouse support in the app under Help > Contact support. Include the room temperature, the symbols or error code shown, and whether heating was off or Away mode was on.
