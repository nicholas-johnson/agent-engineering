# Thermostat Has No Display

## Check the display

The Dormouse Thermostat display dims after 20 seconds without a touch. Turn the dial to wake it. If the display remains blank, check whether the thermostat has mains power.

## Check for a mains-power interruption

A blank display may indicate that mains power to the thermostat has been interrupted. If the display comes back on and shows E-402, the thermostat is running on its backup batteries.

E-402 means the mains power is interrupted. The thermostat can keep the clock and schedules for up to 48 hours on its backup batteries, but it cannot switch the heating on while mains power is off.

## Check the backup batteries

The thermostat uses two AA backup batteries behind the front cover. They support the clock and schedules during a power cut; they do not power the heating controls.

If the display shows E-401, the backup batteries are low. Replace both batteries within a month. To reach them, gently pull the bottom of the front cover towards you.

Replace the backup batteries once a year, even if E-401 has not appeared.

## Restore mains power

Check whether power to the thermostat has been interrupted and restore it if you can do so safely. Once mains power is restored, the thermostat can operate the heating again.

If the display stays blank after mains power is restored, contact Dormouse support in the app under Help > Contact support.

## Understand the limits of battery backup

Do not rely on the backup batteries to keep the home warm during a power cut. They preserve the clock and schedules for up to 48 hours, but the thermostat cannot call for heat without mains power.

If mains power remains off, the backup period may end before power returns. The thermostat then cannot maintain its clock and schedules on battery power alone.
