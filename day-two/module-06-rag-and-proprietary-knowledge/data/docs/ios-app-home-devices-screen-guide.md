# iOS app: Home devices screen guide

## About this guide

Use the Home devices screen to add Dormouse Thermostats, Dormouse Room Sensors and Dormouse Radiator Valves to your home. The screen is in the Dormouse app for iOS.

A home can have up to four thermostats. Each thermostat controls its own zone, with its own schedule. A Room Sensor can join a zone to measure temperature and humidity in another room. Up to ten Radiator Valves can join a zone, so each radiator in that zone heats to its own temperature.

Only the home owner can add or remove devices. Members can change temperatures, schedules and away mode.

## Open Home devices

1. Open the Dormouse app and sign in.
2. Open Settings.
3. Choose Home.
4. Choose Add a device.

The app guides you through adding the selected device. Keep the device near the zone's thermostat while adding it, and follow the instructions shown in the app.

## Add a thermostat

Choose the option to add a thermostat and follow the app's setup steps. A thermostat controls its own zone and needs to be wired to a compatible gas boiler by a qualified installer.

Before pairing, check that:

- Your home has a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network. The thermostat cannot join a 5 GHz network.
- The Dormouse app is installed on an iPhone running iOS 17 or later.
- You have a Dormouse account.
- Your phone is within 5 metres of the thermostat during pairing.

To start pairing on the thermostat, hold the dial down for 5 seconds, until the display shows PAIR. In the app, choose Add thermostat and follow the steps. Pairing must finish within 3 minutes of PAIR appearing. If it does not, the display shows E-104; start pairing again.

A home can contain up to four thermostats. Each thermostat controls its own zone and schedule.

## Add a Room Sensor

Choose the option to add a Dormouse Room Sensor and follow the app's instructions. A Room Sensor measures temperature and humidity in another room of a zone. Up to three Room Sensors can join one zone.

Room Sensors communicate with the thermostat for their zone over a low-power 868 MHz radio link. They do not connect to Wi-Fi. The sensor uses one CR2032 coin cell, which lasts about a year.

## Add a Radiator Valve

Choose the option to add a Dormouse Radiator Valve and follow the app's instructions. A valve joins a zone and lets its radiator heat to its own temperature. Up to ten Radiator Valves can join one zone.

Radiator Valves communicate with the thermostat for their zone over a low-power 868 MHz radio link. They do not connect to Wi-Fi. Each valve runs on two AA batteries, which last about two years.

The valve fits M30 x 1.5 valve bodies. Adapters for other common valve types are included in the box.

## Choose the right zone

When adding a Room Sensor or Radiator Valve, select the zone it should join. A zone is controlled by its thermostat. For example, one thermostat can control downstairs radiators and another can control upstairs radiators.

Check the selected zone before completing setup. A Room Sensor measures conditions in another room of its zone. A Radiator Valve controls the temperature at its own radiator within that zone.

## Device connections

The thermostat uses Wi-Fi to connect to your home network and Dormouse account. Room Sensors and Radiator Valves communicate with their zone's thermostat over a low-power 868 MHz radio link; they do not use Wi-Fi.

For thermostat pairing, keep the iPhone within 5 metres of the thermostat until the app confirms setup is finished. Follow any on-screen instructions for sensors and valves, including any guidance about keeping them near the zone thermostat during setup.

## Home device limits and permissions

| Device | Home or zone limit | Who can add it |
|---|---:|---|
| Thermostat | Up to four per home | Home owner |
| Room Sensor | Up to three per zone | Home owner |
| Radiator Valve | Up to ten per zone | Home owner |

A home has one owner and up to eight members. Members can change temperatures, schedules and away mode, but only the owner can add or remove devices, invite members or remove them.

## If setup does not finish

If thermostat pairing takes longer than 3 minutes after PAIR appears, the thermostat displays E-104. Hold the dial for 5 seconds to start again, then keep the iPhone within 5 metres and complete the app steps within the time limit.

If the thermostat cannot find your Wi-Fi network and displays E-101, check that your router is on and offers a 2.4 GHz network. If the network name or password has changed, pair the thermostat again.

If you need help with a device or setup, contact Dormouse support in the app under Help > Contact support.
