# Understand what happens during a firmware update

## What an update does

The Dormouse Thermostat updates its firmware automatically. Firmware updates are installed between 02:00 and 04:00 local time. You cannot postpone an update.

While an update installs, the thermostat displays **UPDATING** and then restarts. The process takes about two minutes. Heating pauses until the update has finished.

## When an update installs

The thermostat installs updates during the overnight update window. Keep in mind that heating will pause briefly while an update is in progress. Once the thermostat has restarted, it resumes normal operation.

## If an update fails

If an update fails, the display shows **E-510**. The thermostat keeps its current firmware and tries the update again the next night. There is nothing you need to do.

## Check the firmware version

To see which firmware version your thermostat has, press the dial to open the menu, then choose **Menu > About**.
