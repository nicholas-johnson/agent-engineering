# Android app: Household screen guide

## About this screen

The Household screen is where the owner invites people to join the home. It also shows who belongs to the household.

A home has one owner and can have up to eight members. Members can change the temperature, schedules and away mode. Only the owner can add or remove devices, invite members or remove them.

The same household features are available in the Dormouse app for Android and iOS, and in the web dashboard.

## Open Household

1. Open the Dormouse app or web dashboard and select the home.
2. Go to Settings > Household.

The Household screen shows the people who belong to the home and provides the option to invite someone. If you are a member rather than the owner, you cannot invite or remove people.

## Invite a household member

Only the owner can send an invitation.

1. On the Household screen, choose Invite.
2. Enter the invitee's email address.
3. Send the invitation.

The invitee receives an email with a link to join the home. The invitation is valid for 7 days. If it expires before the invitee joins, the owner needs to send a new invitation.

## Invitation expiry

An invitation expires 7 days after it is sent. Once it has expired, its link cannot be used to join the household.

To invite the person again, the owner sends a new invitation from Settings > Household > Invite. The new invitation has its own 7-day validity period.

## Owner and member permissions

The owner and household members have different permissions:

| Action | Owner | Member |
|---|---|---|
| Change the temperature | Yes | Yes |
| Change schedules | Yes | Yes |
| Change away mode | Yes | Yes |
| Add or remove devices | Yes | No |
| Invite household members | Yes | No |
| Remove household members | Yes | No |

These permissions apply to the household. Members can manage everyday heating controls, while device and membership management stays with the owner.

## Manage the household

The owner can invite members and remove them from the Household screen. A home can have up to eight members, in addition to its owner.

Members who need to leave the household should contact the owner. A member cannot remove themselves or invite another person.

## Location sharing and geofencing

Each member chooses in the app whether to share their phone's location. When geofencing is enabled, the thermostat uses household members' location status to control away mode.

Away mode turns on when every member who shares their location is more than 1 km from home. It turns off when the first of them comes back within 1 km. To stop geofencing for the whole home, go to Settings > Away > Geofencing.

## Get help

For help with household invitations or permissions, contact support in the app under Help > Contact support.
