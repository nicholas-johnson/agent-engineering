# E-104: Pairing timed out

## What E-104 means

E-104 means pairing did not finish within 3 minutes of the thermostat displaying PAIR. Pairing connects the thermostat to your Wi-Fi network and Dormouse account. Until pairing finishes, the thermostat is not connected to your account.

The display shows E-104 when the pairing time limit is reached. The app also shows the same error code and may notify you if you have enabled error notifications.

## Common causes

Pairing may time out if you do not complete the steps in the app within 3 minutes of the display showing PAIR. It can also take longer if your phone is too far from the thermostat or the Wi-Fi network is not suitable.

The thermostat needs a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network. It cannot join a 5 GHz network. Keep your phone within 5 metres of the thermostat until the app confirms that pairing has finished.

## Before you start again

Check that:

- Your router is switched on and offers a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network.
- You know the Wi-Fi network name and password.
- The Dormouse app is installed, and you can sign in to your Dormouse account.
- Your phone is within 5 metres of the thermostat.

If the Wi-Fi network name or password has changed, make sure you have the current details ready.

## Start pairing again

The thermostat needs to display PAIR before you can pair it. Follow these steps:

1. Hold the thermostat dial down for 5 seconds, until the display shows PAIR.
2. In the Dormouse app, choose Add thermostat and follow the steps.
3. Keep your phone within 5 metres of the thermostat.
4. Complete the app steps within 3 minutes of the display showing PAIR.
5. Wait for the app to confirm that pairing has finished.

If the thermostat shows E-104 again, start these steps again and keep the app open while you pair.

## If pairing still times out

Check that your phone stays close to the thermostat and that your router is on. Confirm that the network you are using is 2.4 GHz, not 5 GHz. If you have changed the Wi-Fi network name or password, use the current details when pairing.

Then hold the dial down for 5 seconds to show PAIR and try again. The 3-minute limit starts when PAIR appears, so begin the app steps promptly.

## Getting help

If pairing continues to time out after you have checked the network and followed the steps, contact Dormouse support in the app under Help > Contact support. Include the E-104 code and describe what happened while you were pairing.
