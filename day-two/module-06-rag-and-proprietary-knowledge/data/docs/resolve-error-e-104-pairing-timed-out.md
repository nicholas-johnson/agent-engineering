# Resolve error E-104: Pairing timed out

## What E-104 means

E-104 means pairing did not finish within 3 minutes of the thermostat showing PAIR. Pairing connects the thermostat to your Wi-Fi network and Dormouse account.

You need to start pairing again. Keep your phone within 5 metres of the thermostat until the app confirms pairing is complete.

## Start pairing again

1. Hold the thermostat dial down for 5 seconds, until the display shows PAIR.
2. In the Dormouse app, choose Add thermostat and follow the pairing steps.
3. Keep your phone within 5 metres of the thermostat while the app completes setup.

Pairing must finish within 3 minutes of PAIR appearing on the display. If it takes longer, the thermostat shows E-104 and you need to start again.

## Before you begin

Check that:

- The Dormouse app is installed and you are signed in to your Dormouse account.
- Your phone is within 5 metres of the thermostat and stays there during pairing.
- Your Wi-Fi network is on 2.4 GHz. The thermostat cannot join a 5 GHz network.

If the network name or password has changed since the thermostat was paired, start pairing again using the current network details.

## If pairing times out again

Hold the dial down for 5 seconds to show PAIR, then repeat the app steps. Keep the phone close to the thermostat and complete the process within 3 minutes.

If the display shows E-101 instead, the thermostat cannot find the Wi-Fi network. Check that your router is on and offers a 2.4 GHz network. If the display shows a different error, follow the instructions for that code in the user guide.
