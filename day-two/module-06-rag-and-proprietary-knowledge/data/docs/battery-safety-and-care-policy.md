# Battery Safety and Care Policy

## Scope

This policy covers the batteries used by Dormouse products:

- The Dormouse Thermostat uses two AA backup batteries behind its front cover.
- The Dormouse Radiator Valve uses two AA batteries.
- The Dormouse Room Sensor uses one CR2032 coin cell.

It sets out how to access, replace and care for these batteries. Follow the product instructions and any markings on the battery or battery compartment. If a product or battery is damaged, do not continue using it until it has been checked.

## Thermostat backup batteries

The thermostat's two AA backup batteries keep the clock and schedules during a power cut, for up to 48 hours. They do not power the heating: the thermostat cannot switch the heating on while mains power is off.

Replace the thermostat batteries once a year, or when the display shows E-401. E-401 means the backup batteries are low. Replace both AA batteries within a month of seeing this code.

To reach the batteries, gently pull the bottom of the thermostat's front cover towards you. Replace the batteries, then refit the cover. Dispose of used batteries through an appropriate battery recycling route.

## Radiator Valve batteries

The Dormouse Radiator Valve runs on two AA batteries. They last about two years. Replace them when they are depleted, following the battery access and replacement instructions supplied with the valve.

The valve communicates with its zone's thermostat over a low-power 868 MHz radio link. Only the thermostat uses Wi-Fi. A valve battery issue does not change the thermostat's backup-battery operation.

Use the battery type and orientation specified for the valve. Refit the battery cover securely after replacement. Do not leave used batteries inside the valve.

## Room Sensor battery

The Dormouse Room Sensor runs on one CR2032 coin cell, which lasts about a year. Replace it when depleted, following the sensor's battery access and replacement instructions.

The sensor measures temperature and humidity and communicates with its zone's thermostat over a low-power 868 MHz radio link. Only the thermostat uses Wi-Fi.

Keep coin cells away from children. A swallowed coin cell can cause serious injury. Store new and used coin cells securely, and dispose of used cells promptly through an appropriate battery recycling route. Do not leave a loose coin cell where a child could reach it.

## Safe handling and replacement

Handle batteries carefully. Keep them dry and away from heat, fire and direct sunlight. Do not crush, puncture, dismantle or recharge batteries unless they are specifically marked as rechargeable and the product instructions permit it. Do not mix old and new batteries, or different battery types, in the same product.

Before replacing a battery, check the product's battery compartment and the new battery for damage. Do not install a battery that is leaking, swollen, corroded or otherwise damaged. Avoid touching leaked material. If battery material contacts skin or eyes, rinse the affected area with plenty of clean water and seek medical advice.

Fit batteries with the positive and negative ends aligned to the markings in the compartment. Do not force a battery into place. Close the compartment or refit the cover securely before returning the product to use.

## Low batteries and power interruption

The thermostat displays E-401 when its backup batteries are low. Replace the two AA batteries within a month. If the thermostat shows E-402, mains power has been interrupted and it is running on its backup batteries. The batteries keep the clock and schedules for up to 48 hours, but heating cannot be switched on while mains power is off.

The Radiator Valve and Room Sensor use their own batteries. The thermostat's E-401 code applies to its backup batteries; do not treat it as an indication of the battery state of a valve or sensor. Follow the relevant product instructions when replacing those batteries.

## Care and storage

Keep products and batteries in a clean, dry place and use them indoors as intended. Do not use sprays or cleaning products on the thermostat. Wipe it with a soft, dry cloth, and do not paint over it.

If a product will not be used for an extended period, follow its product instructions for battery removal and storage. Keep spare batteries in their packaging until needed. Store them securely, away from children and from metal objects that could bridge the terminals.

## Disposal and recycling

Do not put batteries in household waste. Take used batteries to an appropriate battery collection or recycling point, following local requirements. Keep used batteries secure until they can be recycled, especially CR2032 coin cells.

Do not put a damaged or leaking battery into ordinary recycling. Handle it carefully and seek guidance from an appropriate local battery collection service. Recycle the product and its batteries separately where required.

## Help and product information

For product-specific battery replacement instructions or help with a battery-related issue, use Help > Contact support in the Dormouse app. When seeking help, identify the product and describe the displayed code or the battery issue.
