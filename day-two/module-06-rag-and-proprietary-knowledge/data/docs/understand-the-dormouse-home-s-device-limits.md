# Understand the Dormouse home's device limits

A Dormouse home can have up to four Dormouse Thermostats. Each thermostat controls its own zone, with its own schedule. For example, one thermostat can control downstairs radiators and another can control upstairs radiators.

You can add thermostats, Radiator Valves and Room Sensors in the app or web dashboard. Go to Settings > Home > Add a device.

## Thermostats per home

A home can have up to four Dormouse Thermostats. Each thermostat controls one zone. The thermostats let you set different schedules and temperatures for those zones.

Adding a thermostat does not increase the number of devices allowed in another zone. Radiator Valves and Room Sensors have separate limits for each zone.

## Radiator Valves per zone

Up to ten Dormouse Radiator Valves can join a zone. Each valve controls the temperature of its own radiator. The valves communicate with the thermostat for their zone over a low-power radio link.

A valve fits M30 x 1.5 valve bodies. Adapters for other common valve types are included with the valve. Each valve runs on two AA batteries, which last about two years.

## Room Sensors per zone

Up to three Dormouse Room Sensors can join a zone. A sensor measures temperature and humidity in another room of that zone. It communicates with the zone's thermostat over a low-power radio link.

Each sensor runs on one CR2032 coin cell, which lasts about a year. Sensors do not connect directly to Wi-Fi.

## How the limits work together

The device limits apply separately:

| Device | Maximum |
|---|---:|
| Dormouse Thermostats per home | 4 |
| Dormouse Radiator Valves per zone | 10 |
| Dormouse Room Sensors per zone | 3 |

For example, a home with two thermostats can have up to ten Radiator Valves and three Room Sensors in each zone. The limits are per zone, not shared across the whole home.

## Connecting devices

The thermostat uses Wi-Fi to connect to your home network and Dormouse account. Radiator Valves and Room Sensors communicate with the thermostat in their zone using a low-power 868 MHz radio link. They do not use Wi-Fi themselves.

To add a device, go to Settings > Home > Add a device in the app or web dashboard, then follow the instructions. Each valve or sensor must be added to the zone it will serve.

## If a zone reaches its limit

A zone cannot have more than ten Radiator Valves or three Room Sensors. To add another device of that type, remove one from the zone first. A home cannot have more than four thermostats; adding another requires removing a thermostat from the home first.

Only the home owner can add or remove devices. Members can change temperatures, schedules and away mode, but cannot manage devices.
