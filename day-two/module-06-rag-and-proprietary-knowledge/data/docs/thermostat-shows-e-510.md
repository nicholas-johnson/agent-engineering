# Thermostat Shows E-510

## What E-510 means

E-510 means that a firmware update failed. Your thermostat keeps its current firmware and tries the update again the next night. You do not need to start the update yourself.

## What happens to your heating

The thermostat continues using its current firmware. The failed update does not remove your schedules or change your settings. While the thermostat is installing an update, it shows UPDATING, restarts and pauses the heating for about two minutes.

## What to do now

There is nothing you need to do for E-510. Leave the thermostat powered and let it try again the next night. Firmware updates install automatically between 02:00 and 04:00 local time and cannot be postponed.

## If E-510 appears again

The thermostat will try the update again the following night. If the code continues to appear, contact Dormouse support in the app under Help > Contact support. Include the error code and, if you know it, your thermostat's firmware version. To check the version, press the dial to open the menu and choose Menu > About.

## Related update behaviour

During a successful update, the display shows UPDATING and the thermostat restarts when installation is complete. The update takes about two minutes, and heating pauses until it has finished. If an update fails, the thermostat keeps its current firmware and tries again the next night.
