# Android app: Home screen guide

## About this guide

This guide explains the home screen controls for checking the room and target temperatures and changing the target temperature. The Dormouse app is available for Android and iOS, and you can also use the web dashboard. Most settings can be changed in any of them.

The instructions here apply to the Android app. The screen layout may differ between the app and the web dashboard, but the temperature controls work in the same way.

## Room temperature

The room temperature is shown in large numbers on the thermostat display. In the app, check the home screen to see the temperature reported for the selected home or zone.

A home can have up to four Dormouse Thermostats, each controlling its own zone. If your home has more than one thermostat, make sure you are viewing the zone whose temperature you want to check.

A Dormouse Room Sensor can join a zone and measure the temperature in another room in that zone. The sensor also measures humidity. Up to three Room Sensors can be added to a zone.

## Target temperature

The target temperature is the temperature the thermostat is set to reach. The thermostat display shows it underneath the room temperature. In the app, use the home screen temperature control to view the target for the selected zone.

You can set the target temperature from 5 to 30 degrees, in half-degree steps. The thermostat heats to the target according to its heating controls and schedule.

## Changing the target temperature

Use the temperature control on the home screen to change the target for the selected zone. You can also turn the thermostat dial to adjust the target temperature.

On the thermostat, the target temperature gets larger on the display while you turn the dial. Press the dial to confirm the change. In the app, use the on-screen control to choose the temperature you want.

A change made by hand lasts until the next schedule period starts. If you want to set temperatures for different times of day, change the schedule in the app or web dashboard.

## Choosing a zone

Each Dormouse Thermostat controls its own zone and has its own schedule. For example, one thermostat can control downstairs radiators and another can control upstairs radiators.

Select the zone you want before checking or changing its target temperature. A temperature change applies to the thermostat for that zone, not to every thermostat in the home.

Dormouse Radiator Valves can join a zone and heat individual radiators to their own temperatures. Valves communicate with the zone's thermostat over a low-power radio link. They do not connect to Wi-Fi.

## Temperature display and control

The thermostat display shows the room temperature in large numbers and the target temperature underneath. When you turn the dial, the target temperature becomes larger so you can see the value you are changing.

After 20 seconds without a touch, the display dims. This does not change the room temperature or target temperature.

The thermostat's round colour display is 2.4 inches across. It is powered by mains electricity through its wall plate. The target can be adjusted in half-degree steps within the 5 to 30 degree range.

## Schedules and manual changes

A schedule sets the target temperature at different times of day. Each day can have up to six periods. Each period has a start time and target temperature, and continues until the next period begins.

A manual temperature change lasts until the next schedule period starts. To change the temperatures or times used in a schedule, open the Schedule tab in the app or web dashboard and choose a day. You can use Copy day to apply the same periods to other days.

On the thermostat, Menu > Schedule shows today's periods. You can view them there, but you can only change them in the app or web dashboard.

## Other temperature controls

The boost button on the thermostat starts a one-hour boost at the boost temperature. The default boost temperature is 21 degrees. To change it, go to Settings > Heating > Boost temperature. Press the boost button again to cancel a running boost.

Away mode keeps the home at its away temperature until you turn it off. The default away temperature is 16 degrees, and you can change it in Settings > Away > Away temperature. Away mode can be turned on in the app or from Menu > Away on the thermostat.

Frost protection is always on. If the room falls below 5 degrees, the thermostat turns the heating on, even when heating is off or away mode is on.

## When temperatures are not updating

The thermostat can keep running its schedules if it cannot reach the Dormouse cloud. Changes made in the app wait until the thermostat reconnects. Check that your router is connected to the internet. The thermostat requires a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network and cannot join a 5 GHz network.

If the thermostat shows an error code, the app shows the same code. For E-110, check your internet connection. For other codes, follow the instructions in the thermostat user guide.

To contact support, open the app and go to Help > Contact support.
