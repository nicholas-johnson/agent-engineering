# Stop geofencing for the home

## What geofencing does

Geofencing uses the location of household members' phones to turn Away mode on or off automatically. Away mode turns on when every household member who shares their location is more than 1 km from home. It turns off when the first of them comes back within 1 km.

Each member chooses whether to share their location in the app. You can stop geofencing for the whole home without changing those individual choices.

## Turn off geofencing

1. Open the Dormouse app or web dashboard.
2. Go to Settings > Away > Geofencing.
3. Turn geofencing off for the home.

Once geofencing is off, it no longer automatically changes Away mode based on household members' locations.

## Away mode after geofencing is off

Turning off geofencing does not turn off Away mode. If Away mode is already on, it stays on until you turn it off. You can turn Away mode on or off in the app, or choose Menu > Away on the thermostat.

Away mode keeps the home at the away temperature until you turn it off. The away temperature is 16 degrees unless you change it in Settings > Away > Away temperature.

## Location sharing and privacy

Household members choose individually whether to share their location in the app. To stop geofencing for the entire home, use Settings > Away > Geofencing. This stops geofencing from using household members' locations to switch Away mode on or off.

Phone locations are never stored. The system stores only whether each member is home or away.
