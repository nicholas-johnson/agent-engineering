# Use a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network with your thermostat

## Check the Wi-Fi requirement

The Dormouse Thermostat connects to a Wi-Fi network on the 2.4 GHz band. It cannot join a 5 GHz network.

Check that your router offers a 2.4 GHz network before pairing the thermostat. If your router offers both bands, make sure you select the 2.4 GHz network in the Dormouse app during setup.

## Pair the thermostat

Pairing connects the thermostat to your Wi-Fi network and your Dormouse account.

1. Hold the thermostat dial down for 5 seconds, until the display shows PAIR.
2. In the Dormouse app, choose Add thermostat and follow the steps.
3. Keep your phone within 5 metres of the thermostat until the app says pairing has finished.

Pairing must finish within 3 minutes of PAIR appearing. If it takes longer, the display shows E-104. Start pairing again by holding the dial down for 5 seconds.

## If the 2.4 GHz network is not found

If the thermostat cannot find your network, the display shows E-101. Check that your router is switched on and offers a 2.4 GHz network.

The thermostat cannot connect to a 5 GHz network. If your router offers both bands, check that the 2.4 GHz band is enabled and select that network in the app.

## If you change your Wi-Fi details

If you change the Wi-Fi network name or password, pair the thermostat again so it can connect using the new details.

To start pairing, hold the dial down for 5 seconds, until the display shows PAIR. Then follow the steps in the Dormouse app and keep your phone within 5 metres until pairing finishes.

## Wi-Fi and other Dormouse devices

The thermostat uses Wi-Fi. Dormouse Radiator Valves and Dormouse Room Sensors communicate with the thermostat in their zone over a low-power radio link; they do not use Wi-Fi.

A home can have up to four Dormouse Thermostats, each controlling its own zone.
