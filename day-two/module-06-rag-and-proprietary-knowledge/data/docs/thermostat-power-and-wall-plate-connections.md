# Thermostat Power and Wall-Plate Connections

## Purpose and scope

This guide describes the Dormouse Thermostat's power arrangement and sets out checks for safe installation. The thermostat is powered from the mains through its wall plate. Its backup batteries help preserve the clock and schedules during a power cut; they do not power the heating.

The thermostat must be wired to a compatible gas boiler by a qualified installer. Do not attempt wiring if you are not qualified to work on mains-powered heating equipment. The information here does not replace the boiler manufacturer's instructions, site-specific wiring information or applicable electrical and gas safety requirements.

## Thermostat power arrangement

The Dormouse Thermostat receives its operating power through the wall plate. It is intended for indoor use, between 0 and 40 degrees. The thermostat measures temperature to an accuracy of 0.3 degrees.

Two AA backup batteries fit behind the front cover. They keep the clock and schedules during a power cut for up to 48 hours. The thermostat cannot switch the heating on while mains power is off, even if the backup batteries are working.

Replace the batteries once a year, or when the display shows E-401. E-401 indicates that the backup batteries are low; replace them within a month. To access them, pull the bottom of the front cover gently towards you.

## Wall-plate installation boundary

The wall plate is the mains power connection point for the thermostat. The thermostat must be installed and wired to the boiler by a qualified installer. Use the installation information supplied for the boiler and the particular site to identify and verify the correct conductors and terminals.

This guide does not specify terminal assignments, conductor colours, cable sizes, protective devices, isolation arrangements or wiring diagrams. Do not infer these from the thermostat's appearance or from another installation. If the correct connection information is unavailable or the wiring is unclear, stop and obtain qualified advice before proceeding.

## Safe connection checks

Before making or inspecting a connection, the installer should:

1. Confirm that the boiler and thermostat are suitable for the intended installation, and consult the boiler's own installation instructions.
2. Identify the mains supply and the correct wall-plate and boiler connections from the applicable installation information.
3. Isolate the relevant supply using the site's appropriate isolation method, and verify that the circuit is safe to work on before touching conductors.
4. Check that the wall plate and wiring are secure and undamaged, and that connections match the verified installation information.
5. Confirm that the installation is complete and safe before restoring power.

Do not work on exposed live conductors. Do not guess at a connection, alter boiler wiring without the appropriate competence, or rely on the thermostat's backup batteries as an alternative to safe isolation.

## Power-cut behaviour

During a mains power cut, the backup batteries preserve the clock and schedules for up to 48 hours. They do not allow the thermostat to operate the boiler, so heating cannot be switched on while mains power is unavailable.

When mains power is interrupted, the display shows E-402. The thermostat is running on its backup batteries. Restore the mains supply safely and investigate the cause of the interruption using the appropriate site and boiler procedures. If the power remains off, do not assume that a lit display means the heating is available.

## Display indications related to power

Use the display to distinguish power-related conditions:

| Display indication | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| E-402 | Mains power is interrupted; the thermostat is using its backup batteries. | Check the supply and installation safely. Heating cannot be switched on while mains power is off. |
| E-401 | Backup batteries are low. | Replace the two AA batteries within a month. |
| Flame | The thermostat is asking the boiler for heat. | If heating does not respond, check the boiler and its status using the boiler's instructions. |

An error code may also appear in the app. E-305 means the boiler is not responding: check that it is switched on, that its pressure is between 1 and 1.5 bar, and then check its own display for a fault. E-312 means the boiler reports a fault; consult the boiler manual or a registered gas engineer.

## Restoring operation and checking the system

After a safe installation or restoration of mains power, check that the thermostat starts normally and that the display does not show a power-related error. Confirm that the boiler responds as expected using the boiler's own operating and safety instructions. Do not bypass a fault indication or continue testing if a connection appears damaged, uncertain or unsafe.

If E-402 remains after the supply has been restored, or the thermostat does not behave as expected, stop and have the supply and wiring checked by a qualified installer. For thermostat support, use Help > Contact support in the app.

## Backup-battery care

The batteries are a backup for the clock and schedules, not a substitute for the wall-plate mains supply. Replace both AA batteries once a year, or when E-401 appears. Use the correct battery type and fit the batteries as indicated in the compartment. Refit the front cover securely.

If the thermostat has been without mains power for an extended period, remember that the batteries preserve the clock and schedules for up to 48 hours only. The thermostat cannot control the heating without mains power, regardless of the battery condition.

## Installation limits and handover

The thermostat is an indoor device for use between 0 and 40 degrees. Keep its front cover and wall plate free of damage, and do not paint over the thermostat. Clean it only with a soft, dry cloth; do not use sprays or cleaning products.

At handover, explain that mains power reaches the thermostat through its wall plate, while the two AA batteries provide limited backup for the clock and schedules. Make clear that heating is unavailable during a mains power cut. Leave the installation in a safe condition and provide the user with the relevant operating information for the thermostat and boiler.
