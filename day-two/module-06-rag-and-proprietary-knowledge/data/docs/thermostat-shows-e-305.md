# Thermostat Shows E-305

## What E-305 means

E-305 means the thermostat cannot get a response from the boiler. The thermostat may be asking for heat, but the boiler is not responding as expected.

Check the boiler in this order:

1. Confirm that it is switched on.
2. Check that its pressure is between 1 and 1.5 bar.
3. Check the boiler's own display for a fault.

## Check that the boiler is switched on

Look at the boiler and confirm that its power is on. If it has been switched off, switch it on and allow it time to start.

The thermostat cannot make the boiler respond if the boiler itself is off. After checking the power, see whether E-305 clears. If it remains, continue to the pressure check.

## Check the boiler pressure

Read the pressure on the boiler's gauge or display. It should be between 1 and 1.5 bar.

If the pressure is outside this range, follow the instructions in the boiler's own manual. Do not use the thermostat to change boiler pressure. Once the pressure is in range, check whether E-305 clears.

## Check the boiler's display

Look at the boiler's own display for a fault or error code. The boiler may have detected a problem that the thermostat cannot diagnose.

Use the boiler's manual to look up any fault shown. If the boiler reports a fault, follow the manual's guidance or call a registered gas engineer.

## Check whether E-305 clears

After checking the boiler's power, pressure and display, allow the thermostat and boiler to respond. If the thermostat still shows E-305, check the app as well: it shows the same error code as the thermostat.

Do not treat E-305 as a boiler fault code. It means the boiler is not responding to the thermostat; the boiler's own display may provide separate information about the cause.

## If the problem continues

If the boiler is switched on, its pressure is between 1 and 1.5 bar, and its display shows no fault, but E-305 remains, contact Dormouse support in the app under Help > Contact support.

If the boiler displays a fault, consult its manual or contact a registered gas engineer.
