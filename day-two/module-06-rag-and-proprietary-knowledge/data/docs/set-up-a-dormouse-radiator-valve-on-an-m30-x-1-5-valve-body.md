# Set up a Dormouse Radiator Valve on an M30 x 1.5 valve body

## What you need

A Dormouse Radiator Valve controls the temperature of an individual radiator in a Dormouse zone. It fits M30 x 1.5 valve bodies. Adapters are included in the box for other common valve types.

Before you begin, make sure the radiator is in a zone controlled by a Dormouse Thermostat. A home can have up to four thermostats, and each thermostat controls its own zone.

## How the valve connects

The Radiator Valve communicates with the thermostat for its zone over a low-power 868 MHz radio link. It does not connect to Wi-Fi. The thermostat connects to Wi-Fi and manages the zone.

Up to ten Dormouse Radiator Valves can join one zone. Each valve controls its own radiator, so radiators in the same zone can heat to their own temperatures.

## Fit the valve

1. Check that the radiator has an M30 x 1.5 valve body.
2. Fit the Dormouse Radiator Valve to the valve body.
3. If the valve body is a different common type, use the matching adapter supplied in the box.

If the valve body is not M30 x 1.5 and you are unsure which supplied adapter to use, ask a qualified heating professional to identify the valve body before fitting.

## Add the valve to a zone

Once the valve is fitted, add it to the zone it will control:

1. In the app or web dashboard, go to Settings > Home > Add a device.
2. Follow the instructions to add the Radiator Valve.
3. Select the zone controlled by the thermostat that the valve should communicate with.

A Room Sensor can also join a zone. Each zone can have up to three Room Sensors and up to ten Radiator Valves.

## Power and battery life

The Radiator Valve runs on two AA batteries. They last about two years. Replace the batteries when they need changing.

The thermostat's backup batteries are separate from the Radiator Valve's batteries. The thermostat uses two AA backup batteries to keep its clock and schedules during a mains power cut; it cannot switch the heating on while mains power is off.

## Check the zone

After adding the valve, check in the app or web dashboard that it appears in the intended zone. Use the zone's thermostat and schedule to manage heating for that zone. The valve then controls its own radiator within the zone.

If the valve is in the wrong zone, use Settings > Home > Add a device to manage the home's devices.

## Compatibility at a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Valve body fit | M30 x 1.5 |
| Other common valve types | Adapters included in the box |
| Connection | Low-power 868 MHz radio link to the zone's thermostat |
| Wi-Fi | Used by the thermostat, not the Radiator Valve |
| Batteries | Two AA batteries; about two years' life |
| Zone capacity | Up to ten Radiator Valves |
