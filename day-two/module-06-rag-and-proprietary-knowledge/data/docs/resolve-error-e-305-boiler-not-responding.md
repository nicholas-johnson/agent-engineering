# Resolve error E-305: Boiler not responding

## What E-305 means

E-305 means the Dormouse Thermostat cannot get a response from the boiler. The thermostat display and app show the same error code.

Check the boiler itself before taking further action:

1. Confirm that the boiler is switched on.
2. Check that its pressure is between 1 and 1.5 bar.
3. Look at the boiler's display for a fault.

## Check that the boiler is switched on

Check the boiler's controls and display to confirm it has power and is switched on. If it is off, use the boiler's own controls to switch it on, following the boiler's instructions.

Then check whether the boiler responds to the thermostat. If E-305 remains, continue with the pressure check.

## Check the boiler pressure

Read the pressure from the boiler's pressure gauge or display. The pressure should be between 1 and 1.5 bar.

If the reading is outside this range, follow the instructions in the boiler's manual. Do not adjust the boiler unless you know how to do so safely. If you are unsure, contact a registered gas engineer.

## Check the boiler display for a fault

Look at the boiler's display for a fault code or message. If it reports a fault, consult the boiler's manual for what that code means. You can also call a registered gas engineer.

## If E-305 remains

If the boiler is switched on, its pressure is between 1 and 1.5 bar, and its display shows no fault, but E-305 remains, contact Dormouse support in the app under Help > Contact support.

If the boiler displays a fault, resolve it using the boiler's manual or with help from a registered gas engineer before checking the thermostat error again.
