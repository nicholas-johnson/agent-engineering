# Contact Dormouse support in the app

## Open Contact support

You can contact Dormouse support from the Dormouse app:

1. Open the app and sign in.
2. Open **Help**.
3. Choose **Contact support**.

The app is available for Android and iOS. Use the same app account you use for your thermostat.

## Before you contact support

If your thermostat is showing an error code, note the code before opening the support screen. The app shows the same code as the thermostat, and may also notify you about it.

You can find guidance for known codes in the thermostat's user guide under **Error codes**. For example, E-104 means pairing timed out, while E-401 means the backup batteries are low. Following the recommended steps may resolve the issue without further help.

## Describe the issue

In Contact support, explain what is happening and include any relevant details, such as:

- The error code shown on the thermostat or in the app.
- What you were doing when the issue started.
- Any steps you have already tried.

For heating problems, include whether the thermostat can change the target temperature and whether the heating responds. For connection problems, say whether the thermostat shows the Wi-Fi symbol with a cross.

## Find your thermostat's firmware version

If support needs to know which firmware your thermostat has, check it on the thermostat:

1. Press the dial to open the menu.
2. Choose **About**.

The guide covers firmware 4.1. Providing the version shown can help support understand which features and instructions apply.

## Keep the app and thermostat details together

If your home has more than one Dormouse Thermostat, identify which thermostat or zone is affected. Each thermostat controls its own zone, with its own schedule. Include the zone or thermostat details that will help support distinguish it from the others.

You can also mention whether the issue affects a Dormouse Room Sensor or Dormouse Radiator Valve in that zone.

## Allow notifications

The app can notify you when the thermostat shows an error code or its backup batteries are low. Each household member chooses their own notification preferences in **Settings > Notifications**.

Notifications can make it easier to notice an issue, but you can still open **Help > Contact support** in the app when you need assistance.

## Household access

A home can have one owner and up to eight members. Members can change the temperature, schedules and away mode. Only the owner can add or remove devices, invite members or remove them.

If you are a member and need help with a device or household change that only the owner can make, contact the owner. The owner can contact support through the app as described above.

## Support and privacy

Contact support in the app under **Help > Contact support**. Do not include passwords or two-step verification codes in your description.

Phone locations used for geofencing are never stored. Dormouse stores only whether each member is home or away.
