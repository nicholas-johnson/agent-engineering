# Web dashboard: Home screen guide

## What the home screen shows

The home screen gives you a view of the temperatures and heating in your home. Each Dormouse Thermostat controls its own zone, with its own schedule. A home can have up to four thermostats.

The thermostat display shows the room temperature in large numbers and the target temperature underneath. The target temperature is the temperature the thermostat is set to reach. In the dashboard, select the relevant zone to view its room and target temperatures.

A Dormouse Room Sensor can measure temperature and humidity in another room in a zone. Up to three Room Sensors can join a zone. A Dormouse Radiator Valve can control the temperature at its own radiator. These devices communicate with their zone's thermostat over a low-power radio link; only the thermostat uses Wi-Fi.

## View a zone's temperatures

1. Open the home screen in the web dashboard.
2. Select the zone you want to view.
3. Read the room temperature and target temperature shown for that zone.

If your home has more than one thermostat, select each zone in turn to see its temperatures. Each thermostat has its own schedule, so the target temperature may differ between zones.

The thermostat's round display also shows the room temperature and target temperature. When you turn its dial, the target temperature gets larger while you change it. After 20 seconds without a touch, the display dims.

## Change the target temperature

Select a zone on the home screen and use its temperature control to change the target temperature. The thermostat can be set from 5 to 30 degrees, in half-degree steps. You can also change the target temperature by turning the thermostat's dial.

A change made by hand lasts until the next schedule period starts. The schedule then sets the target temperature for the new period. Each day can have up to six periods, each with a start time and target temperature.

To change schedule periods, open the Schedule tab in the web dashboard, choose a day and edit its periods. To use the same periods on other days, choose Copy day. The thermostat's Schedule menu shows today's periods, but changes must be made in the app or web dashboard.

## Understand heating activity

The thermostat asks the boiler for heat when the home needs heating. The flame symbol on the thermostat display means it is asking the boiler for heat.

Other display symbols indicate specific heating states:

| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Sunrise | Warm Wake |
| Snowflake | Frost protection is heating the home |
| Upward arrow | A boost is running |
| House with an arrow | Away mode is on |

Frost protection is always on. If the room falls below 5 degrees, the thermostat turns the heating on, even when heating is off or away mode is on.

## Boost

A boost heats the home at the boost temperature for one hour. The default boost temperature is 21 degrees. To change it, go to Settings > Heating > Boost temperature.

You can start a boost with the boost button below the thermostat's dial. The display shows an upward arrow while the boost is running. Press the button again to cancel it.

## Away mode

Away mode keeps the home at the away temperature until you turn it off. The away temperature is 16 degrees unless you change it in Settings > Away > Away temperature.

Turn away mode on by tapping Away in the app or choosing Menu > Away on the thermostat. You can also use geofencing, which turns away mode on when all household members who share their location are more than 1 km from home. It turns away mode off when the first of them comes back within 1 km. Each member chooses whether to share their location in the app. To stop geofencing for the whole home, go to Settings > Away > Geofencing.

## When the dashboard and thermostat differ

Most settings can be changed in the app and web dashboard. If the thermostat cannot reach the Dormouse cloud, it keeps running its schedules, but changes made in the app or dashboard wait until it reconnects. The display shows E-110 when the cloud cannot be reached. Check that your router is connected to the internet.

The thermostat needs a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network; it cannot join a 5 GHz network. If the display shows a Wi-Fi symbol with a cross, the thermostat is not connected to Wi-Fi.

## Get help

If you see an error code on the home screen, the thermostat display shows the same code. For example, E-217 means the temperature sensor is disconnected. The thermostat turns the heating off to be safe; contact support to arrange a replacement.

For other codes, follow the action in the error code table in the Dormouse Thermostat User Guide. Contact Dormouse support in the app under Help > Contact support.
