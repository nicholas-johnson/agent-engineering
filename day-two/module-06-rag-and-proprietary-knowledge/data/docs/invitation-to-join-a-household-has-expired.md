# Invitation to Join a Household Has Expired

## What the message means

A household invitation works for 7 days. After that, its link expires and cannot be used to join the home.

## Check the invitation age

Check when the invitation was sent. If it was sent more than 7 days ago, it has expired.

## Ask the owner to send a new invitation

Only the home owner can invite members. The owner needs to send a new invitation:

1. Open Settings > Household > Invite.
2. Enter your email address and send the invitation.
3. Open the new invitation and follow its link to join.

The new invitation works for 7 days from when it is sent.
