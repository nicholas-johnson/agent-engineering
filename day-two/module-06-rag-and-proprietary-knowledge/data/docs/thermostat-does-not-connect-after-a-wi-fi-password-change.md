# Thermostat Does Not Connect After a Wi-Fi Password Change

## Check whether the network details changed

If your thermostat stopped connecting after a router change, check whether the Wi-Fi network name or password has changed. The thermostat needs the current details to connect.

The thermostat can join a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network. It cannot join a 5 GHz network. Check that your router is switched on and that a 2.4 GHz network is available.

If the network name or password has changed, pair the thermostat again.

## Get ready to pair

Before you start, make sure:

- Your router is on and offers a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network.
- You have the Dormouse app on a phone running Android 12 or later, or iOS 17 or later.
- You can sign in to your Dormouse account.
- Your phone can stay within 5 metres of the thermostat until pairing is complete.

Pairing connects the thermostat to Wi-Fi and to your Dormouse account. It must finish within 3 minutes of the thermostat showing PAIR.

## Pair the thermostat again

1. Hold the thermostat dial down for 5 seconds, until the display shows PAIR.
2. In the app, choose Add thermostat and follow the steps.
3. Keep your phone within 5 metres of the thermostat until the app says pairing has finished.

If pairing takes longer than 3 minutes, the display shows E-104. Start again by holding the dial down for 5 seconds, then follow the app steps promptly.

## Check the connection

When the app says pairing has finished, the thermostat is connected to your Wi-Fi and Dormouse account. If the thermostat still cannot connect, check that the router is on and the available network is 2.4 GHz, not 5 GHz.

If the display shows E-101, the thermostat cannot find the Wi-Fi network. Check that the network is available and that its details are correct, then pair the thermostat again.

If it shows E-110, the thermostat cannot reach the Dormouse cloud. Check that your router is connected to the internet. The thermostat continues to run its schedules while disconnected, but changes made in the app wait until it reconnects.

## Get help

If the thermostat still does not connect after you have checked the network and paired it again, contact Dormouse support in the app under Help > Contact support.
