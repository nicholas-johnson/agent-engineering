# Replace the Dormouse Radiator Valve batteries

## When to replace the batteries

The Dormouse Radiator Valve runs on two AA batteries. They typically last about two years. Replace both batteries when they run low, or if the valve stops working.

## What you need

- Two AA batteries
- A dry place to set the valve while you change the batteries

## Before you begin

Make sure you can reach the valve safely. If you have several valves, replace the batteries in one valve at a time so you can keep track of which radiator each valve controls.

## Replace the batteries

1. Remove the valve from the radiator, following the instructions supplied with the valve.
2. Open the battery compartment.
3. Remove both old batteries.
4. Insert two new AA batteries, matching the polarity marks in the compartment.
5. Close the battery compartment.
6. Refit the valve to the radiator, following its instructions.

## After replacing the batteries

Allow the valve to reconnect to its zone's thermostat. The valve communicates with the thermostat over a low-power radio link; it does not use Wi-Fi.

If the valve does not resume working, check that both batteries are fitted in the correct direction and that the battery compartment is closed securely.
