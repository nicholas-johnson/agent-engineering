# Understand where the thermostat can be used

## Indoor operating range

The Dormouse Thermostat is designed for indoor use between 0 and 40 degrees. Use it in a room where the surrounding temperature stays within this range.

The range describes the conditions the thermostat is designed to operate in. It is not the target-temperature range: you can set a target temperature from 5 to 30 degrees, in half-degree steps.

## Choosing a location

Choose an indoor position where the thermostat is likely to experience temperatures within its operating range. Consider the room conditions throughout the year, not only when you install it.

The thermostat is powered by mains electricity through its wall plate. A qualified installer must wire it to the boiler.

## If the room is outside the range

The thermostat is not designed for use where the indoor temperature is below 0 degrees or above 40 degrees. Select another indoor position that stays within its operating range.

## Target temperature and frost protection

The target temperature can be set from 5 to 30 degrees. Turning the dial changes the target temperature; a manual change lasts until the next schedule period starts.

Frost protection is always on. If the room falls below 5 degrees, the thermostat turns the heating on, even when the heating is off or Away mode is on. This protection does not change the thermostat's indoor operating range: the thermostat is designed for use between 0 and 40 degrees.

## Related specifications

The thermostat is 84 mm across and 24 mm deep, with a 2.4-inch round colour display. Its temperature measurement accuracy is 0.3 degrees.

For care, wipe the thermostat with a soft, dry cloth. Do not use sprays or cleaning products, and do not paint over it.
