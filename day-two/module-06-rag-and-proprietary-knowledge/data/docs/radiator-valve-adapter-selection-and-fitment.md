# Radiator Valve Adapter Selection and Fitment

## Purpose and scope

The Dormouse Radiator Valve fits M30 x 1.5 valve bodies. Adapters supplied in the box allow it to fit other common valve bodies.

This guide describes how to identify whether an adapter is needed and how to fit the valve using an appropriate supplied adapter. It does not identify specific alternative valve-body types or provide a compatibility list. If you cannot confirm that a supplied adapter matches the valve body, do not force the fit.

The Dormouse Radiator Valve controls an individual radiator within a zone. It communicates with the zone's Dormouse Thermostat over a low-power 868 MHz radio link. The valve does not connect to Wi-Fi.

## Before you begin

Check that the radiator valve body is suitable for fitting a Dormouse Radiator Valve. The standard fit is for an M30 x 1.5 valve body. For other common valve bodies, check the adapters supplied in the box and use one only when it is a suitable match.

Work carefully around the existing valve body. Do not use force to make the Dormouse Radiator Valve or an adapter fit. Stop if the parts do not engage correctly or cannot be secured properly.

The valve runs on two AA batteries, which typically last about two years. Have the correct batteries ready for setup.

## Identify the valve-body fit

Inspect the existing valve body and compare its connection with the Dormouse Radiator Valve and supplied adapters.

1. If the valve body has an M30 x 1.5 connection, fit the Dormouse Radiator Valve directly without an adapter.
2. If it does not have an M30 x 1.5 connection, check whether one of the supplied adapters is made for that valve body.
3. Select an adapter only when its fit is clear. Do not assume that an adapter is suitable because it looks similar.
4. If none of the supplied adapters clearly matches, do not install the valve using an improvised or forced connection.

The supplied adapters are for other common valve bodies. No specific alternative body types or adapter markings are defined in this guide. If you are unsure which adapter applies, stop and seek advice from Dormouse support in the app under Help > Contact support.

## Fit the valve directly to an M30 x 1.5 body

Use this method only when the valve body has an M30 x 1.5 connection.

1. Check that the connection is clean and unobstructed.
2. Position the Dormouse Radiator Valve squarely on the valve body.
3. Secure it without forcing the connection.
4. Check that the valve sits correctly and is held securely before proceeding with setup.

If the valve does not engage or sit securely, remove it and check the connection again. Do not continue by applying extra force.

## Fit a supplied adapter

Use an adapter only when it clearly matches the valve body and is needed for the connection.

1. Confirm that the adapter is one of those supplied with the Dormouse Radiator Valve.
2. Check that it matches the valve body's connection. Do not modify the adapter.
3. Fit the adapter squarely to the valve body and secure it without force.
4. Position the Dormouse Radiator Valve on the fitted adapter.
5. Secure the valve and check that the assembly sits correctly and remains stable.

Do not stack adapters, use parts that were not supplied for the valve, or alter the valve body or adapter to make them fit. If the adapter or valve cannot be secured correctly, remove the parts and stop the installation.

## Check the completed fit

After fitting, check that the Dormouse Radiator Valve is held securely and aligned correctly on the valve body, either directly or through the selected adapter. The assembly must not be loose or forced into position.

If anything appears damaged, does not fit correctly, or cannot be secured, do not use the valve in that condition. Remove the valve and adapter, if fitted, and check that the correct connection has been identified. Contact Dormouse support in the app under Help > Contact support if you cannot confirm the fit.

## Power and zone communication

The Dormouse Radiator Valve runs on two AA batteries. Once the valve is fitted, use its battery compartment and setup instructions supplied with the product to install the batteries and complete setup.

A valve joins a zone and communicates with that zone's Dormouse Thermostat over a low-power 868 MHz radio link. The thermostat, not the valve, connects to Wi-Fi. Add a valve to the home in Settings > Home > Add a device, then follow the setup instructions in the app or web dashboard.

Each Dormouse Radiator Valve in a zone heats its own radiator to its own temperature. A zone can include up to ten Dormouse Radiator Valves. Make sure the valve is added to the intended zone during setup.

## Troubleshooting fitment

| Issue | Action |
|---|---|
| The valve body is M30 x 1.5, but the valve does not fit securely | Remove the valve and check that it is positioned squarely. Do not force it. |
| The valve body is not M30 x 1.5 | Check whether a supplied adapter clearly matches. Do not guess or modify parts. |
| No supplied adapter clearly matches | Stop the installation and contact Dormouse support in the app under Help > Contact support. |
| The fitted adapter or valve is loose or misaligned | Remove the parts and check the match and positioning before trying again. Do not operate the valve in an insecure fit. |
| The valve is fitted but does not appear in the intended zone | Add the valve through Settings > Home > Add a device and check the zone selected during setup. |

## Product care

The Dormouse Radiator Valve is powered by two AA batteries, which typically last about two years. Replace batteries as needed, following the instructions supplied with the valve.

The thermostat controls the zone and connects to the home's Wi-Fi network. Valves and sensors communicate with their zone's thermostat using the low-power 868 MHz radio link. A valve does not need its own Wi-Fi connection.
