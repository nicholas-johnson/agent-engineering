# Room Sensor Mounting and Battery Installation

## About this guide

This guide covers indoor placement of a Dormouse Room Sensor and fitting its CR2032 coin cell. The sensor measures temperature and humidity in a room and joins a zone controlled by a Dormouse Thermostat.

A zone can have up to three Dormouse Room Sensors. Each sensor communicates with its zone's thermostat over a low-power 868 MHz radio link. The sensor does not use Wi-Fi; only the thermostat connects to Wi-Fi.

## Before you begin

Check that you have:

- A Dormouse Room Sensor.
- One CR2032 coin cell for the sensor.
- A zone with a Dormouse Thermostat.

Choose a location indoors where the sensor can measure the room conditions you want the zone to respond to. The thermostat and sensor must be within range of each other for their radio link to operate. The sensor is intended for indoor use.

## Choose a mounting position

Mount the sensor in the room whose temperature and humidity you want it to measure. Avoid placing it where the reading is unlikely to represent the room, such as directly beside a source of heat or in a draught.

Choose a position where the sensor can communicate with the thermostat for its zone over the 868 MHz radio link. The sensor does not need to be placed near a Wi-Fi router.

The sensor measures temperature and humidity. Its readings are available through the Dormouse system when it has joined a zone. The thermostat and any other devices in that zone continue to operate as part of the same zone.

## Fit the CR2032 coin cell

The Dormouse Room Sensor uses one CR2032 coin cell. The cell lasts about a year under typical use.

1. Open the sensor's battery compartment.
2. Insert one CR2032 coin cell, following the polarity markings in the compartment.
3. Close the battery compartment securely.

Use a CR2032 cell only. Do not force the cell into place. If the compartment does not close easily, remove the cell and check that it is seated in the orientation shown by the markings.

## Add the sensor to a zone

After fitting the battery, add the sensor to the zone it will serve. In the app or web dashboard, go to Settings > Home > Add a device and follow the instructions.

A sensor joins one zone and reports conditions from its room to that zone's system. Each zone can include up to three Dormouse Room Sensors and up to ten Dormouse Radiator Valves. A home can have up to four Dormouse Thermostats, each controlling its own zone.

## Check operation

Once the sensor has been added, check that it appears in the intended zone in the app or web dashboard. Allow the system to establish its low-power radio link with the zone's thermostat.

If the sensor does not appear or its readings are unavailable, check that:

- The CR2032 coin cell is installed in the marked orientation.
- The battery compartment is closed securely.
- The sensor has been added to the correct zone.
- The sensor is positioned where it can communicate with that zone's thermostat.

The thermostat, rather than the sensor, connects to Wi-Fi. A Wi-Fi connection for the sensor is not required.

## Battery care

The sensor's CR2032 coin cell lasts about a year. Replace it when the sensor needs a new battery, using the same fitting steps and a fresh CR2032 cell.

Dispose of used coin cells safely and in accordance with local battery-disposal requirements. Keep coin cells out of reach of children. Do not use a cell that is damaged or leaking.

## Cleaning and support

Keep the sensor clean and dry. For the thermostat, the user guide specifies wiping with a soft, dry cloth and avoiding sprays and cleaning products. Do not paint over the thermostat.

For help with a sensor or its installation, contact Dormouse support in the app under Help > Contact support.
