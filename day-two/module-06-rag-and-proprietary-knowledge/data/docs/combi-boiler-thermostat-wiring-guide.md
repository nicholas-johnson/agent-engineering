# Combi Boiler Thermostat Wiring Guide

## Scope

This guide covers wiring considerations for connecting a Dormouse Thermostat to a combi boiler. The thermostat is designed to work with gas combi boilers, as well as system and conventional boilers. It must be wired to the boiler by a qualified installer.

The exact wiring arrangement depends on the boiler and the thermostat installation. Check the boiler's installation instructions and the requirements for the specific installation before making any connection. This guide does not provide terminal numbers, wiring diagrams or instructions for opening or modifying a boiler.

## Before you begin

Confirm that the installation meets the thermostat's requirements:

- The boiler is a gas combi boiler.
- The thermostat is to be wired to the boiler by a qualified installer.
- The home has a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network available for pairing. The thermostat cannot join a 5 GHz network.
- The Dormouse app is available on a phone running Android 12 or later, or iOS 17 or later.
- The owner has a Dormouse account, or is ready to create one in the app.

The thermostat's Wi-Fi connection is used to connect it to the Dormouse app and account. It does not replace the wired connection between the thermostat and boiler.

## Compatibility and installation planning

Dormouse Thermostats support gas combi, system and conventional boilers. Confirm that the boiler is suitable for use with the thermostat before proceeding. Follow the boiler manufacturer's instructions and use an installer qualified to carry out the work.

A home can have up to four Dormouse Thermostats. Each thermostat controls its own zone and has its own schedule. If the home uses multiple thermostats, plan which zone the thermostat being installed will control.

The thermostat is intended for indoor use between 0 and 40 degrees. It is powered by mains through its wall plate. Its two AA backup batteries keep the clock and schedules during a power cut for up to 48 hours, but the thermostat cannot switch the heating on while mains power is off.

## Wiring considerations

The thermostat must be wired to the boiler by a qualified installer. Use the boiler's installation instructions to identify the correct connection method and terminals for that boiler. Do not assume that terminal names or wiring arrangements are the same across different boiler models.

Before wiring, establish how the boiler is intended to be controlled and confirm that the proposed thermostat connection is compatible with the boiler. If the boiler's documentation does not clearly identify the appropriate connection, stop and consult a qualified installer.

The thermostat is a heating control. It does not replace the boiler's own safety controls or instructions. Keep the boiler's controls and safety functions in accordance with the manufacturer's requirements.

## Power and backup batteries

The thermostat receives mains power through its wall plate. It also has two AA backup batteries behind the front cover. These batteries preserve the clock and schedules during a power cut for up to 48 hours; they do not provide power to run the heating.

Replace the batteries once a year, or when the display shows E-401. When E-401 appears, replace the two AA batteries within a month. To reach them, gently pull the bottom of the front cover towards you.

If the display shows E-402, mains power has been interrupted. The thermostat is running on its backup batteries and cannot switch the heating on until mains power is restored.

## Pairing after installation

Pairing connects the thermostat to the home's Wi-Fi and the Dormouse account. Complete the wired installation before pairing.

1. Hold the thermostat dial down for 5 seconds, until the display shows PAIR.
2. In the Dormouse app, choose Add thermostat and follow the steps.
3. Keep the phone within 5 metres of the thermostat until the app confirms that pairing is complete.

Pairing must finish within 3 minutes of PAIR appearing. If it does not, the thermostat shows E-104. Hold the dial down for 5 seconds to start pairing again.

The thermostat requires a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network. If it shows E-101, check that the router is on and offers a 2.4 GHz network.

## Checks after wiring

After the installer has completed the wiring and the thermostat has been powered, confirm that the thermostat starts normally and can be paired. Use the thermostat display and app to check for errors.

If the boiler does not respond to a heating request and the display shows E-305, check that the boiler is switched on and its pressure is between 1 and 1.5 bar. Then check the boiler's own display for a fault.

If E-312 appears, the boiler has reported a fault of its own. Look up that fault in the boiler's manual or call a registered gas engineer. If E-217 appears, the thermostat turns the heating off to be safe; contact Dormouse support to arrange a replacement.

## Operation and heating controls

Turn the thermostat dial to change the target temperature, or use the app. The target temperature can be set from 5 to 30 degrees in half-degree steps. A manual temperature change lasts until the next schedule period starts.

A boost runs the heating at the boost temperature for one hour. Press the boost button to start it; press the button again to cancel it. The boost temperature is 21 degrees unless changed in Settings > Heating > Boost temperature.

Frost protection is always on and cannot be turned off. If the room falls below 5 degrees, the thermostat turns on the heating even when the heating is off or away mode is on.

## Faults and support

The thermostat display and app show the same error code. Use the code's guidance in the user guide to check the next step. For example, E-110 means the thermostat cannot reach the Dormouse cloud; the thermostat keeps running its schedules, while app changes wait until it reconnects.

For boiler faults, follow the boiler manufacturer's instructions. Do not treat a thermostat error code as a substitute for investigating a boiler fault safely.

To contact Dormouse support, use Help > Contact support in the app.
