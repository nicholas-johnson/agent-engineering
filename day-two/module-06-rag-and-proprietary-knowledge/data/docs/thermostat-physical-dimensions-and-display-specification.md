# Thermostat Physical Dimensions and Display Specification

## Product

The Dormouse Thermostat is an indoor heating control for a gas boiler. It connects to Wi-Fi and can be controlled through the Dormouse app or web dashboard. The thermostat has a round colour display, a rotary dial and a boost button.

This specification documents the thermostat's physical dimensions and display. It applies to the Dormouse Thermostat with firmware 4.1.

## Physical dimensions

| Parameter | Specification |
|---|---:|
| Diameter | 84 mm |
| Depth | 24 mm |

The thermostat is circular when viewed from the front. Its depth is measured from the front of the unit to the rear. The thermostat is powered by mains electricity through its wall plate.

## Display

| Parameter | Specification |
|---|---|
| Form | Round colour display |
| Size | 2.4 inches |

The display presents the room temperature in large numbers and the target temperature underneath. When the target temperature is being changed with the dial, the target temperature appears larger. The display dims after 20 seconds without a touch.

## Display indications

The display can show operating symbols and status information alongside temperature readings.

| Symbol or indication | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Flame | The thermostat is asking the boiler for heat. |
| Sunrise | Warm Wake is heating ahead of the next schedule period. |
| Snowflake | Frost protection is heating the home. |
| House with an arrow | Away mode is on. |
| Upward arrow | A boost is running. |
| Wi-Fi symbol with a cross | The thermostat is not connected to Wi-Fi. |
| Battery | The backup batteries are low. |
| Error code | The thermostat has detected a fault or other error condition. |
| UPDATING | A firmware update is installing. |

The symbols indicate thermostat status; they do not change the physical dimensions or display size.

## Controls and display interaction

The dial is used to adjust the target temperature. Pressing the dial confirms a selection or opens the menu when the display shows the room temperature. The menu contains Schedule, Away, Settings and About.

The button below the dial starts a boost. While the boost is running, an upward arrow appears on the display. Pressing the boost button again cancels it.

## Installation and power

The thermostat must be wired to a compatible gas boiler by a qualified installer. It is powered from mains electricity through its wall plate. The thermostat requires a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network for connection; it cannot join a 5 GHz network.

Two AA backup batteries are located behind the front cover. They preserve the clock and schedules during a power cut for up to 48 hours. They do not allow the thermostat to switch the heating on while mains power is off. Replace the batteries once a year, or when the display shows E-401.

## Operating environment

The thermostat is for indoor use between 0 and 40 degrees. The product's temperature measurement accuracy is 0.3 degrees.

Keep the thermostat indoors and use it within the stated temperature range. For cleaning, wipe it with a soft, dry cloth. Do not use sprays or cleaning products, and do not paint over the thermostat.

## Identification and firmware

The thermostat's firmware version can be checked by pressing the dial to open the menu and choosing Menu > About. This specification describes firmware 4.1.

Hardware revisions are identified by their serial-number prefixes:

| Hardware revision | Serial-number prefix |
|---|---|
| A | DMA |
| B | DMB |
| C | DMC |

The display and physical dimensions specified here are 2.4 inches round, 84 mm across and 24 mm deep.

## Related technical information

The thermostat supports homes with up to four Dormouse Thermostats, each controlling its own zone. A Dormouse Room Sensor can join a zone, as can up to ten Dormouse Radiator Valves.

For error-code meanings and actions, refer to the Dormouse Thermostat User Guide. To contact support, use Help > Contact support in the app.
