# Thermostat Won't Join Wi-Fi

Use this guide when the thermostat cannot connect to your Wi-Fi network. Work through the checks in order. You need a Wi-Fi network on 2.4 GHz; the thermostat cannot join a 5 GHz network.

## Check the router is on

Make sure your router is switched on and its power cable is connected. Check that its Wi-Fi network is available to other devices in your home.

If the router is off, turn it on and wait for it to finish starting up. Then check the thermostat again. A Wi-Fi symbol with a cross on the display means the thermostat is not connected to Wi-Fi.

## Check that your network offers 2.4 GHz

The thermostat connects to a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network. It cannot join a 5 GHz network.

Check your router's network settings or information to confirm that 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi is enabled. If your router offers separate network names for 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz, use the 2.4 GHz network when pairing.

If the router offers both bands under one network name, make sure it makes a 2.4 GHz network available for the thermostat to join. Contact your router provider if you need help checking its settings.

## Check the error code

If the display shows an error code, use it to identify the problem:

| Code | Meaning | What to check |
|------|---------|----------------|
| E-101 | Wi-Fi network not found | Check that the router is on and offers a 2.4 GHz network. |
| E-104 | Pairing timed out | Pairing did not finish within 3 minutes. Start again and keep your phone within 5 metres of the thermostat. |
| E-110 | Cannot reach the Dormouse cloud | Check that the router is connected to the internet. The thermostat may be connected to Wi-Fi but unable to reach the cloud. |

The thermostat and app show the same error code. If the display shows a different code, see the Error codes section in the user guide.

## Check whether the network details changed

If you have changed your Wi-Fi network's name or password, pair the thermostat again. Pairing connects it to Wi-Fi and to your Dormouse account.

Before you start, confirm that the router is on and offers a 2.4 GHz network. Keep your phone within 5 metres of the thermostat for the whole process.

## Pair the thermostat again

1. Hold the thermostat dial down for 5 seconds, until the display shows PAIR.
2. In the Dormouse app, choose Add thermostat and follow the steps.
3. Keep your phone within 5 metres of the thermostat until the app says pairing has finished.

Pairing must finish within 3 minutes of PAIR appearing. If it takes longer, the display shows E-104. Start pairing again by holding the dial down for 5 seconds.

## If the thermostat still cannot connect

Check once more that the router is on, that a 2.4 GHz network is available, and that you are using the current network details. If E-110 appears, check that the router is connected to the internet. While the thermostat cannot reach the Dormouse cloud, it keeps running its schedules, but changes made in the app wait until it reconnects.

If the thermostat continues to show an error, contact Dormouse support in the app under Help > Contact support. Include the error code shown on the thermostat.
