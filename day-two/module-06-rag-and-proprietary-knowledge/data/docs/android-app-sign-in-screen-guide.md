# Android app: Sign-in screen guide

## About this guide

This guide explains how to sign in to the Dormouse app on Android, what the password requirements are, and how to reset a forgotten password. The same email address and password are used to sign in to the iOS app and the web dashboard.

## Before you sign in

You need a Dormouse account to use the app. If you do not have one, you can create an account in the app.

Your password must contain at least 10 characters. Keep your sign-in details private.

## Sign in

1. Open the Dormouse app.
2. Enter the email address for your Dormouse account.
3. Enter your password.
4. Choose the sign-in option on the screen.

Use the same account details to sign in to the iOS app or the web dashboard.

## Two-step verification

If two-step verification is turned on, signing in on a new phone or browser also requires a 6-digit code. The code is sent by email, or provided by an authenticator app if you have set one up.

To turn on two-step verification, go to Settings > Account > Two-step verification.

## Reset your password

If you cannot remember your password:

1. On the sign-in screen, choose Forgot password?
2. Enter the email address for your Dormouse account.
3. Follow the link sent to that address to reset your password.

The reset link works for 30 minutes and can be used only once. If it expires, request a new link from the sign-in screen.

## Sign-in problems

Check that you entered the email address and password for your Dormouse account. Remember that passwords must have at least 10 characters.

If you have requested a password-reset link, use it within 30 minutes. A link that has expired or has already been used will not work; request another one.

## Account security

Two-step verification adds a 6-digit code when you sign in on a new phone or browser. You can receive the code by email or use an authenticator app if you have set one up.

If you need help with the app, go to Help > Contact support.
