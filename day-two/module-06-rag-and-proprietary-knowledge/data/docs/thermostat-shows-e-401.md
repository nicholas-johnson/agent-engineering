# Thermostat Shows E-401

## What E-401 means

E-401 means the thermostat's backup batteries are low. Replace both AA batteries within a month of the warning.

The batteries help keep the clock and schedules during a power cut. They do not power the heating: while mains power is off, the thermostat cannot switch the heating on.

## Locate the backup batteries

The two AA batteries are behind the thermostat's front cover. To reach them, pull the bottom of the cover gently towards you.

## Replace both batteries

1. Pull the bottom of the front cover gently towards you.
2. Remove both AA batteries.
3. Fit two replacement AA batteries.
4. Refit the front cover.

Replace both batteries when E-401 appears; do not wait for the warning to change.

## Check the display

After replacing the batteries, check whether E-401 is still shown. If it is, contact Dormouse support in the app under Help > Contact support.

## Prevent future battery warnings

Replace the backup batteries once a year. E-401 appears a month before they run out, giving you time to replace them.
