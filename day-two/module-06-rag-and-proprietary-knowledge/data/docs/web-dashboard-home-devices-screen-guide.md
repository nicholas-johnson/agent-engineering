# Web dashboard: Home devices screen guide

## Purpose

The Home devices screen lists the thermostats, room sensors and radiator valves in your home. Use it to add a device to a zone.

A home can have up to four Dormouse Thermostats. Each thermostat controls its own zone, with its own schedule. A Dormouse Room Sensor can join a zone to measure temperature in another room. Up to ten Dormouse Radiator Valves can join a zone, so each radiator in that zone heats to its own temperature.

The same device-management settings are available in the Dormouse app for Android and iOS. Menu paths in this guide are the same in the apps and the web dashboard.

## Open Home devices

1. Sign in to the web dashboard with your email address and password.
2. Go to Settings > Home.
3. Open the devices area to view the devices in your home or add one.

You need to be the home owner to add or remove devices. Household members can change the temperature, schedules and away mode, but only the owner can manage devices.

## Devices and zones

A zone is controlled by a Dormouse Thermostat and has its own schedule. For example, one thermostat can control downstairs radiators and another can control upstairs radiators.

A room sensor or radiator valve joins a zone. Sensors and valves communicate with their zone's thermostat over a low-power 868 MHz radio link. Only the thermostat uses Wi-Fi.

Before adding a device, decide which zone it should join. A room sensor measures temperature in another room of that zone. A radiator valve lets its radiator heat to its own temperature within the zone.

## Add a thermostat

1. Go to Settings > Home > Add a device.
2. Choose the option to add a Dormouse Thermostat.
3. Follow the on-screen instructions to pair it with your Wi-Fi and Dormouse account.

Pairing requires a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network; the thermostat cannot join a 5 GHz network. Keep your phone within 5 metres of the thermostat while pairing. Pairing must finish within 3 minutes of the thermostat showing PAIR. If it times out, the thermostat shows E-104; hold the dial down for 5 seconds to start pairing again.

A thermostat must be wired to the boiler by a qualified installer. Each thermostat controls its own zone. A home can have up to four thermostats.

## Add a room sensor

1. Go to Settings > Home > Add a device.
2. Choose the option to add a Dormouse Room Sensor.
3. Follow the on-screen instructions to join it to a zone.

A Dormouse Room Sensor measures temperature and humidity. Up to three room sensors can join a zone. Each sensor uses one CR2032 coin cell, which lasts about a year.

The sensor communicates with the zone's thermostat over a low-power 868 MHz radio link. It does not use Wi-Fi.

## Add a radiator valve

1. Go to Settings > Home > Add a device.
2. Choose the option to add a Dormouse Radiator Valve.
3. Follow the on-screen instructions to join it to a zone.

Up to ten radiator valves can join a zone. Each valve lets its radiator heat to its own temperature. The valve communicates with the zone's thermostat over a low-power 868 MHz radio link; it does not use Wi-Fi.

The Dormouse Radiator Valve fits M30 x 1.5 valve bodies. Adapters for other common valves are included in the box. The valve runs on two AA batteries, which last about two years.

## Check the device list

After adding a device, check that it appears in the Home devices list and is assigned to the intended zone. The list helps you review which thermostats, sensors and valves belong to your home.

If a device is not listed as expected, check that you completed the on-screen setup steps. For a thermostat, also check that it has joined a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network and that pairing finished within the required time.

## Device management and support

Only the home owner can add or remove devices. If you are a member rather than the owner, ask the owner to make the change.

For help with setup or device errors, contact Dormouse support in the app under Help > Contact support. The thermostat's display and the app show the same error code when something is wrong. For example, E-104 means thermostat pairing timed out.
