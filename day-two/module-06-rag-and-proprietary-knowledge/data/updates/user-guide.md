# Dormouse Thermostat User Guide

## About this guide

This guide covers the Dormouse Thermostat with firmware 4.2. To see which firmware your thermostat has, press the dial to open the menu and choose Menu > About.

The thermostat works with the Dormouse app for Android and iOS, and with the web dashboard. Most settings can be changed in any of them. Menu paths in this guide, such as Settings > Heating, are the same in the app and the web dashboard.

## The thermostat at a glance

### The display

The round display shows the room temperature in large numbers and the target temperature underneath. When you turn the dial, the target temperature gets larger while you change it. After 20 seconds without a touch, the display dims.

### The dial

Turn the dial to change the target temperature. Press it to confirm, or to open the menu when the display shows the room temperature. The menu has four items: Schedule, Away, Settings and About. Turn the dial to move between them and press to choose.

### The boost button

The button below the dial starts a boost. See Boost under Heating.

### Symbols on the display

| Symbol | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Flame | The thermostat is asking the boiler for heat. |
| Sunrise | Warm Wake is heating ahead of the next schedule period. |
| Snowflake | Frost protection is heating the home. |
| House with an arrow | Away mode is on. |
| Upward arrow | A boost is running. |
| Wi-Fi symbol with a cross | The thermostat is not connected to Wi-Fi. |
| Battery | The backup batteries are low. |

## Setting up

### What you need

- A gas boiler: combi, system or conventional. The thermostat must be wired to the boiler by a qualified installer.
- A Wi-Fi network on 2.4 GHz. The thermostat cannot join a 5 GHz network.
- The Dormouse app on a phone with Android 12 or later, or iOS 17 or later.
- A Dormouse account. You can create one in the app.

### Pairing the thermostat

Pairing connects the thermostat to your Wi-Fi and to your Dormouse account.

1. Hold the dial down for 5 seconds, until the display shows PAIR.
2. In the app, choose Add thermostat and follow the steps.
3. Keep your phone within 5 metres of the thermostat until the app says it has finished.

Pairing must finish within 3 minutes of the display showing PAIR. If it takes longer, the display shows E-104 and you need to start again.

### Backup batteries

Two AA batteries behind the front cover keep the clock and your schedules during a power cut, for up to 48 hours. The thermostat cannot switch the heating on while the mains power is off. To reach the batteries, pull the bottom of the front cover gently towards you.

Replace the batteries once a year, or when the display shows E-401.

## Your account

### Signing in

Sign in to the app and the web dashboard with your email address and password. A password must have at least 10 characters.

### Two-step verification

Two-step verification asks for a 6-digit code as well as your password when you sign in on a new phone or browser. The code comes by email, or from an authenticator app if you set one up. To turn it on, go to Settings > Account > Two-step verification.

### Resetting your password

On the sign-in screen, choose Forgot password? and enter your email address. We send a link to reset your password. The link works for 30 minutes and can only be used once. If it has expired, ask for a new one.

## Heating

### Target temperature

The target temperature can be set from 5 to 30 degrees, in half-degree steps. Turn the dial, or use the app. A change you make by hand lasts until the next schedule period starts.

### Boost

Press the boost button to heat the home at the boost temperature for one hour. The display shows an upward arrow while a boost is running. Press the button again to cancel the boost.

The boost temperature is 21 degrees unless you change it. To change it, go to Settings > Heating > Boost temperature.

### Frost protection

Frost protection stops your pipes freezing. If the room falls below 5 degrees, the thermostat turns the heating on, even when the heating is off or away mode is on. The display shows a snowflake while frost protection is heating. Frost protection is always on and cannot be turned off.

## Schedules

### Setting a schedule

A schedule tells the thermostat what temperature you want at different times of day. Each day can have up to six periods. A period has a start time and a target temperature, and lasts until the next period starts.

To set a schedule, open the Schedule tab in the app or the web dashboard and choose a day. To use the same periods on other days, choose Copy day. On the thermostat, Menu > Schedule shows today's periods, but you can only change them in the app or the web dashboard.

### Warm Wake

Warm Wake makes sure the home has already reached the target temperature when a schedule period starts, rather than starting to warm up at that time. It learns how long your home takes to heat up, and by default starts the heating up to 60 minutes early. It uses the last 14 days of heating and the outside temperature for your postcode.

You can let Warm Wake start up to 30, 60 or 90 minutes early. To choose, or to turn Warm Wake off, go to Settings > Schedule > Warm Wake. When Warm Wake is off, the heating starts at the time each period starts.

While Warm Wake is heating early, the display shows the sunrise symbol.

## Away mode

### Turning away mode on

Away mode keeps the home at the away temperature until you turn it off. The away temperature is 16 degrees unless you change it in Settings > Away > Away temperature. To turn away mode on, tap Away in the app, or choose Menu > Away on the thermostat.

### Geofencing

Geofencing turns away mode on and off for you, using the location of each household member's phone. Away mode turns on when every member who shares their location is more than 1 km from home, and turns off when the first of them comes back within 1 km.

Each member chooses whether to share their location in the app. To stop geofencing for the whole home, go to Settings > Away > Geofencing.

## Zones

A home can have up to four Dormouse Thermostats, and each one controls its own zone with its own schedule. For example, one thermostat can run the downstairs radiators and another the upstairs ones.

A Dormouse Room Sensor can join a zone to measure the temperature in another room of that zone. Up to ten Dormouse Radiator Valves can join a zone, so that each radiator in it heats to its own temperature.

To add a thermostat, sensor or valve, go to Settings > Home > Add a device.

## Household sharing

A home has one owner and up to eight members. Members can change the temperature, the schedules and away mode. Only the owner can add or remove devices, invite members or remove them.

To invite someone, go to Settings > Household > Invite and enter their email address. They get an email with a link to join. The invitation works for 7 days. After that, the owner needs to send a new one.

## Notifications

The app can notify you when:

- the thermostat shows an error code
- the backup batteries are low
- the heating has been on for 2 hours while everyone is away
- a firmware update has been installed
- your monthly energy report is ready

Each member chooses their own notifications in Settings > Notifications.

## Energy reports

An energy report arrives on the 1st of each month, in the app's Energy tab. It shows how many hours the heating was on each day, compared with the month before, and an estimate of the gas used in kWh.

The estimate depends on the size of your boiler. To enter it, go to Settings > Home > Boiler size.

## Firmware updates

The thermostat updates its own firmware. Updates are installed automatically between 02:00 and 04:00 local time, and cannot be postponed. While an update installs, the display shows UPDATING and the thermostat restarts. This takes about two minutes, and the heating pauses until it has finished.

If an update fails, the display shows E-510. The thermostat keeps its current firmware and tries again the next night.

## Error codes

When something is wrong, the display shows an error code. The app shows the same code and can notify you about it.

| Code | Meaning | What to do |
|------|---------|------------|
| E-101 | Wi-Fi network not found | Check your router is on and offers a 2.4 GHz network. If you have changed the network's name or password, pair the thermostat again. |
| E-104 | Pairing timed out | Pairing must finish within 3 minutes. Hold the dial for 5 seconds to start again, and keep your phone within 5 metres of the thermostat. |
| E-110 | Cannot reach the Dormouse cloud | Check your router is connected to the internet. The thermostat keeps running your schedules, and changes made in the app wait until it reconnects. |
| E-217 | Temperature sensor disconnected | The thermostat turns the heating off to be safe. Contact Dormouse support to arrange a replacement. |
| E-221 | Humidity sensor fault | The heating keeps working, but humidity readings disappear from the app. Contact Dormouse support if the code stays for more than a day. |
| E-305 | Boiler not responding | Check the boiler is switched on and its pressure is between 1 and 1.5 bar. Then check the boiler's own display for a fault. |
| E-312 | Boiler reports a fault | The boiler has found a fault of its own. Look up the fault in the boiler's manual, or call a registered gas engineer. |
| E-401 | Backup batteries low | Replace the two AA batteries behind the front cover within a month. |
| E-402 | Mains power interrupted | The thermostat is running on its backup batteries. It keeps the clock and your schedules for up to 48 hours, but cannot switch the heating on. |
| E-510 | Firmware update failed | Nothing to do. The thermostat keeps its current firmware and tries again the next night. |

## Factory reset

A factory reset removes the Wi-Fi details, the schedules and the link to your account. Do it before you give the thermostat to someone else or move house without it.

Hold the dial and the boost button down together for 10 seconds, until the display shows RESET. The thermostat restarts as if it were new, and you can pair it again.

## Cleaning and care

Wipe the thermostat with a soft, dry cloth. Do not use sprays or cleaning products, and do not paint over it.
