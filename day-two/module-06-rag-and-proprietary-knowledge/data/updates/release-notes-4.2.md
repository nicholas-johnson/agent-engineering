# Firmware 4.2 release notes

Firmware 4.2 is being installed on every Dormouse Thermostat overnight, between 02:00 and 04:00, from 16 November 2026.

## Warm Wake starts less early

Warm Wake now starts the heating up to 60 minutes early by default, instead of 90 minutes. Many homes reached their target temperature well before the schedule period started, which used more gas than needed.

## Choose how early Warm Wake starts

You can now let Warm Wake start up to 30, 60 or 90 minutes early. If you prefer the old behaviour, choose 90 minutes.

## Warm Wake has moved

The Warm Wake setting has moved from Settings > Heating to Settings > Schedule > Warm Wake, next to the other schedule settings.

## Fixes

- The sunrise symbol no longer stays on the display after a schedule period has started.
- Energy reports now arrive on the 1st of the month even if the thermostat was offline at midnight.
