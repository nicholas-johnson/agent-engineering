# Event RSVP

Build the site for an event: a wedding, a reunion or a small conference. Guests open a personal link, say whether they're coming, choose a meal and add dietary needs; the organiser sees the numbers and exports a list for the caterer. It's a form, a database and an export, plus personal data that needs looking after.

**Build it in:** Lovable. **Time:** an evening. **Needs:** a Lovable account.

## Start in Plan mode

> Build an RSVP site for a wedding on 6 June. The couple add guests and send each household a personal link, with no sign-in for guests. Each guest says whether they're coming, chooses one of three meals, and adds any dietary needs. The couple see the totals, chase people who haven't answered, and export a spreadsheet for the caterer.

Before you approve the plan, look for how a personal link works. What stops someone guessing another household's link?

## Then, one change at a time

- "Add a page with the day's timetable, travel and where to stay."
- "Children get a children's menu, and don't count towards the wine order."
- "Guests can bring a plus-one only if their invitation says so."
- "Send a reminder to everyone who hasn't answered a month before."

## Watch for

- **Personal data.** Names, addresses and dietary needs, which can say something about someone's health or religion. Ask in Chat mode who can see each of them.
- **The links.** Open one guest's link, then change a letter in it. What happens?
- **The export.** Open the spreadsheet. Is it what a caterer actually needs, or what the model thought one would?

## Try this

1. Make it a conference instead: talks, a schedule, and each guest picks the sessions they'll attend.
2. Add the couple's own words, their story and how they write, to the Instructions, and ask it to rewrite every page in that voice.
3. Run the deep security scan and fix what it finds.
4. Ask it to delete all guest data automatically a month after the event. How does it do that, and how would you check it worked?
5. Use the custom domain setting (on a paid plan) to put it on a real address, and check what the quick scan says when you publish.

## Questions

For discussion. There are no right answers.

- The guest list is personal data. Who is responsible for it: the couple, Lovable, or the person who built the site?
- A guessable link is convenient and slightly unsafe. Where's the line for a wedding? For a conference with paid tickets?
- Would you tell guests the site was built with AI? Does it matter?
