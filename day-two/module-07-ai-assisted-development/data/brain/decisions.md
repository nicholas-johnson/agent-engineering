# Decisions

Product decisions we have made, newest first, and why. Check here before proposing something that goes against one.

## 2026-05: Settings about when the heating runs live under Schedule

Customers look under Schedule when the heating comes on at a time they didn't expect. Any setting that changes when the heating runs belongs in Settings > Schedule, next to the schedule itself. Settings > Heating is for how warm, not when.

## 2026-02: Give customers a choice of three, not a number

When customers can tune a setting, offer three named choices with a sensible default, rather than a number to type. Fewer choices means fewer support contacts, and every choice can be tested.

## 2025-11: Defaults should use less gas, not more

When we are unsure, the default is the one that uses less gas. Customers who want more comfort can choose it.

## 2025-06: No savings figures in marketing

We don't quote percentage savings in marketing or sales material. Point customers to their own monthly energy report instead.

## 2024-09: The app and the web dashboard match

Every setting has the same name, in the same place, in the app and the web dashboard.
