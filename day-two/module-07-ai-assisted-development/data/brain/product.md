# Product

Dormouse makes a smart thermostat for homes with a gas boiler.

## What we sell

- **Dormouse Thermostat**: a round thermostat on the wall, with a dial, a display and a boost button. It is wired to the boiler by an installer.
- **Dormouse Room Sensor**: measures the temperature in another room of the same zone.
- **Dormouse Radiator Valve**: lets each radiator in a zone heat to its own temperature.
- **The Dormouse app** for Android and iOS, and **the web dashboard**. Both are free, and have the same settings in the same places.

The current firmware is 4.1. Firmware updates install themselves overnight, between 02:00 and 04:00.

## Features

| Feature | What it does | Where customers change it |
|---------|--------------|---------------------------|
| Schedule | Up to six periods a day, each with a start time and a target temperature | The Schedule tab |
| Warm Wake | Starts the heating early, by up to 90 minutes, so the home is already warm when a schedule period starts. Learns how long the home takes to heat up. The display shows a sunrise symbol while it heats. | Settings > Heating > Warm Wake |
| Boost | Heats to the boost temperature (21 degrees unless changed) for one hour | The boost button; Settings > Heating > Boost temperature |
| Frost protection | Heats the home if it falls below 5 degrees. Always on. | Cannot be changed |
| Away mode | Holds the away temperature (16 degrees unless changed) | Settings > Away > Away temperature |
| Geofencing | Turns away mode on when everyone is more than 1 km from home | Settings > Away > Geofencing |
| Energy reports | Monthly, in the app's Energy tab: hours of heating and an estimate of gas used | Settings > Home > Boiler size |

## The settings menu

The settings menu is the same in the app and the web dashboard:

- **Heating**: Boost temperature, Warm Wake
- **Schedule**: Week starts on
- **Away**: Away temperature, Geofencing
- **Home**: Add a device, Boiler size
- **Household**: Invite, Members
- **Notifications**
- **Account**: Two-step verification, Password
