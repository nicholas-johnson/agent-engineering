# Voice

How Dormouse writes, everywhere: the app, the help centre, emails, social posts and sales material.

## How we sound

- Plain and calm, like a helpful neighbour who knows about heating.
- Short sentences. One idea each.
- Say what something does for the customer, not how clever it is.
- British English: colour, organise, programme (but "program" for software).
- Be exact: "up to 60 minutes early", not "a little early".
- Never promise a saving we can't show. Point to the Energy tab instead.

## Words and formats

- Feature names are capitalised: Warm Wake, Boost. Everything else is in sentence case, including headings and buttons.
- Temperatures are "21 degrees" in customer text, never with a symbol.
- Times use the 24-hour clock: 06:30.
- Menu paths use ">": Settings > Schedule > Warm Wake.

## Words we don't use

- revolutionary
- game-changer
- seamless
- effortless
- cutting-edge
- unlock
- smart AI
- supercharge
