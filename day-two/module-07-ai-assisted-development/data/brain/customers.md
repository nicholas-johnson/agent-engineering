# Customers

About 240,000 homes in the UK and Ireland use a Dormouse Thermostat. Most bought it to spend less on gas without thinking about the heating.

## Who they are

- **Homeowners who want to set it and forget it.** They set a schedule once and expect it to just work. They rarely open the settings. This is most of our customers.
- **People watching their bills.** They read the monthly energy report and notice when the heating runs more than they expect.
- **Shift workers and people at home in the day.** Their schedules change often, and they care about the heating coming on exactly when they set it.
- **Landlords and letting agents.** They install Dormouse in rented homes. The tenant uses the app; the landlord wants fewer call-outs.

## What they tell us

- They judge the thermostat by whether the home is warm when they want it, and whether the heating is on when they don't.
- They don't read the user guide. They find out what a feature does when it surprises them.
- When something surprises them, they assume it is a fault, and contact support or leave a review.

## How they reach us

Support chat in the app, email, app store reviews, and a short survey after each support conversation.
