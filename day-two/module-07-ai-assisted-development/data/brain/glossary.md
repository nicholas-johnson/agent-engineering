# Glossary

- **Schedule period**: a time of day with a target temperature. It lasts until the next period starts. Up to six a day.
- **Target temperature**: the temperature the thermostat heats the home to.
- **Warm Wake**: starts the heating before a schedule period, so the home is warm when the period starts. Customers often call it "heating coming on early" and don't know its name.
- **Heat-up rate**: how many degrees an hour the home warms up, learned from the last 14 days of heating and the outside temperature. Warm Wake uses it to work out how early to start.
- **Boost**: one hour of heating at the boost temperature, from the button on the thermostat.
- **Away mode**: holds the away temperature until it is turned off.
- **Geofencing**: turns away mode on and off from household members' phone locations.
- **Zone**: the radiators one thermostat controls. A home can have up to four.
- **Hub**: we don't have one. The thermostat connects to Wi-Fi directly. Competitors' hubs are a common question.
