# Warm Wake

Warm Wake makes sure the home has already reached the target temperature when a schedule period starts, rather than starting to warm up at that time. It learns how long your home takes to heat up, and starts the heating early by up to 90 minutes. It uses the last 14 days of heating and the outside temperature for your postcode.

While Warm Wake is heating early, the display shows the sunrise symbol.

Warm Wake is on for every new thermostat. To turn it off, go to Settings > Heating > Warm Wake. When Warm Wake is off, the heating starts at the time each period starts.
