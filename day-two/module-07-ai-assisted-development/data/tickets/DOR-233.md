# DOR-233: Let customers choose how long Boost lasts

- **Type:** Feature
- **Status:** Ready for development
- **Reporter:** Product

## Problem

Boost always heats for one hour. Customers who only want to take the chill off a room for a few minutes leave it running, and customers working from home on a cold day press it again every hour.

## What we're building

1. A new setting, **Boost length**, with three choices: 30 minutes, 1 hour or 2 hours.
2. The default is **1 hour**, so nothing changes for customers who don't choose.
3. It sits under Settings > Heating, below Boost temperature.

## Acceptance criteria

- Boost length accepts 30, 60 or 120 minutes, and rejects any other value.
- A new customer, and an existing customer who has never changed it, gets 60 minutes.
- The settings page shows Boost length under Heating, below Boost temperature, with the three choices named 30 minutes, 1 hour and 2 hours.
- The text on the settings page follows the brand voice.

## Out of scope

- The boost button on the thermostat itself.
