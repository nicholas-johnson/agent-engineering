# DOR-231: Let customers choose how early Warm Wake starts

- **Type:** Feature
- **Firmware:** 4.2
- **Status:** Ready for development
- **Reporter:** Product

## Problem

Warm Wake starts the heating up to 90 minutes before a schedule period. For many homes that is far more than they need: they are warm well before the period starts, and use more gas than they have to. Customers who don't know about Warm Wake think the schedule is broken.

In September and October, 14 of 30 pieces of feedback were about the heating coming on early. Most didn't know it was Warm Wake. Two wanted it to start less early rather than not at all, and one customer who turned it off found the house too cold.

## What we're building

1. A new setting, **How early**, with three choices: up to 30, 60 or 90 minutes. Warm Wake never starts the heating earlier than the chosen number of minutes.
2. The default is **60 minutes**, for new and existing customers. Most homes heat up in well under an hour, and it uses less gas than 90.
3. Warm Wake, and the new How early setting, move from Settings > Heating to **Settings > Schedule**, because customers look there when the heating comes on at an unexpected time.
4. How early is shown only while Warm Wake is on.

## Acceptance criteria

- With How early set to 30, 60 or 90, the heating never starts more than that many minutes before the period.
- Any other value for How early is rejected.
- A new customer, and an existing customer who has never changed it, gets 60 minutes.
- The settings page shows Warm Wake and How early under Schedule, and not under Heating.
- How early appears only while Warm Wake is on.
- The text on the settings page follows the brand voice.

## Out of scope

- How the heat-up rate is learned.
- The sunrise symbol fix (DOR-219).
