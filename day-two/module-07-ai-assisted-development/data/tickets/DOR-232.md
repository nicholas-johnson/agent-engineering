# DOR-232: Let the week start on Saturday

- **Type:** Feature
- **Status:** Ready for development
- **Reporter:** Product

## Problem

Week starts on offers Monday or Sunday. Customers whose working week runs from Saturday, such as people who work weekends and have Thursday and Friday off, have asked for Saturday too: they plan their heating a week at a time, and the Schedule tab starts on the wrong day for them.

## What we're building

Week starts on gets a third choice: **Saturday**.

## Acceptance criteria

- Week starts on accepts Monday, Sunday or Saturday, and rejects any other value.
- The settings page offers all three, in that order, with the saved one selected.
- Monday stays the default.

## Out of scope

- How the Schedule tab itself is drawn.
