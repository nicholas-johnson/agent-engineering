# DOR-234: Warm Wake starts 90 minutes early in homes that heat up quickly

- **Type:** Bug
- **Status:** Ready for development
- **Reporter:** Support

## What customers see

Customers in small homes say the heating comes on 90 minutes before every schedule period, although their home warms up in under half an hour. In larger homes it seems to be right. Warm Wake is meant to start only as early as the home needs, up to 90 minutes.

## To reproduce

From the project folder:

```bash
python -m dormouse homes/flat-3b.json
```

Flat 3B's last three heat-ups gained 2.5, 3 and 2 degrees in 30, 35 and 25 minutes. To warm from 18 to 20 degrees it needs about 24 minutes, so for a 07:00 period the heating should come on at about 06:36. Warm Wake switches it on at 05:30.

`homes/house-14.json` is a larger home, where 05:00 for a 06:30 period looks right.

## Acceptance criteria

- Flat 3B's heating comes on at 06:36 for its 07:00 period.
- 14 Mill Lane's heating still comes on at 05:00.
- A test covers the cause, so it can't come back.
