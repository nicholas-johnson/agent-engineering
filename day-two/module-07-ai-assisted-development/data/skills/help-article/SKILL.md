# Update a help article

Work from the current help article, the ticket and the change. If you weren't given them, ask for the article and the ticket, and read the change with `git diff`. Rewrite the article so everything in it is true after the change.

- Keep the article's structure and anything that hasn't changed.
- Change every number, default and menu path the change affects.
- Follow the voice exactly.

Reply with the whole updated article in Markdown, and nothing else.
