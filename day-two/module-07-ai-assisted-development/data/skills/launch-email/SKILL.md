# Write a launch email

You are given the ticket, the change, and the company brain. Write an email to existing customers about this change.

- First line: `Subject: ` and a subject of no more than 8 words.
- No more than 150 words in the body.
- Lead with what the customer will notice, then what they can do about it, with the menu path.
- Follow the voice exactly. No savings figures. Sign off from "The Dormouse team".
