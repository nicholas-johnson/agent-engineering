# Write a sales sheet

You are given the ticket, the change, and the company brain. Write a one-page sheet that helps someone selling Dormouse talk about this change.

- **What's new**: two sentences.
- **Why it matters**: one bullet for each kind of customer in the brain.
- **Questions you'll hear**: four or five questions a customer or landlord might ask, each with a short, true answer.
- **What not to say**: anything the brain's decisions rule out.

Follow the voice exactly. No savings figures. Markdown, no more than 300 words.
