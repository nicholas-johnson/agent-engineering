# Write social posts

You are given the ticket, the change, and the company brain. Write two posts about this change:

## Short

For Bluesky, X and Threads: no more than 280 characters, including any hashtags. At most one hashtag.

## LinkedIn

No more than 80 words, for homeowners and landlords.

Follow the voice exactly. No savings figures. No emoji.
