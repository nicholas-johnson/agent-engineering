# E-110: Cannot reach the Dormouse cloud

## What E-110 means

E-110 means the thermostat cannot reach the Dormouse cloud. The thermostat shows the error code on its display, and the app shows the same code. You may also receive a notification if you have enabled error-code notifications.

This error does not stop the thermostat from running your schedules. However, changes you make in the app wait until the thermostat reconnects.

## What can cause E-110

E-110 can appear when the router is not connected to the internet, or when the thermostat cannot communicate with the Dormouse cloud. The code identifies the connection problem; it does not confirm which part of the connection is at fault.

The thermostat uses Wi-Fi to connect to the cloud. It must use a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network and cannot join a 5 GHz network.

## Before you begin

Check whether other devices on your home network can connect to the internet. If they cannot, the problem may be with the router or its internet connection.

Keep in mind that the thermostat continues running its schedules while it cannot reach the cloud. App changes will not take effect on the thermostat until it reconnects.

## Step 1: Check the router and internet connection

Check that the router is switched on and connected to the internet. If the router indicates that its internet connection is down, follow its usual recovery steps.

Once the router is back online, give the thermostat time to reconnect. Check whether E-110 clears from the display and the app.

## Step 2: Check the Wi-Fi network

Make sure the router offers a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network. The thermostat cannot connect to a 5 GHz network.

If you have changed the Wi-Fi network name or password, the thermostat may need to be paired again. Pairing connects it to Wi-Fi and your Dormouse account. Start pairing by holding the dial down for 5 seconds, until the display shows PAIR. Then choose Add thermostat in the app and follow the steps.

Keep your phone within 5 metres of the thermostat while pairing. Pairing must finish within 3 minutes of PAIR appearing. If it takes longer, the display shows E-104 and you need to start again.

## Step 3: Check the thermostat and app

Check the thermostat display for the current error code. The app shows the same code when the thermostat reports an error.

If the thermostat has reconnected, allow time for changes made in the app while it was offline to reach it. Until then, the thermostat continues to run its schedules.

## If E-110 remains

If the router is connected to the internet and the thermostat still shows E-110, contact Dormouse support in the app under Help > Contact support. Include the error code and say what you have already checked.

## What to expect while disconnected

While E-110 is present, the thermostat keeps running its schedules. Changes made in the app wait until it reconnects to the Dormouse cloud. When the connection is restored, check the thermostat and app to confirm the error has cleared and the thermostat is receiving changes again.
