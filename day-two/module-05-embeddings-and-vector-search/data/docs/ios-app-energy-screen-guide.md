# iOS app: Energy screen guide

## About the Energy screen

The Energy screen shows your monthly energy report. The report compares the heating hours recorded for each day with the previous month and estimates the gas used, in kWh.

A report is ready on the 1st of each month and appears in the app's Energy tab. The estimate depends on your boiler size.

## Open the report

1. Open the Dormouse app on your iPhone.
2. Tap the Energy tab.
3. View the report for the month shown.

The report is available in the app when it is ready. It is not a real-time measure of gas use.

## Daily heating hours

The report shows how many hours the heating was on each day of the month. Use the daily figures to see how heating use varied over the month.

These figures are hours of heating operation. They do not show the temperature in your home or the amount of heat delivered to each room.

## Compare with the previous month

The report compares daily heating hours with the month before. Use the comparison to review how the recorded heating hours changed from one month to the next.

The report covers a calendar month and arrives on the 1st of the following month. A report is not available until it has been prepared.

## Estimated gas use

The report includes an estimate of gas used, shown in kWh. It is an estimate, not a direct gas-meter reading. Its accuracy depends on the boiler size saved for your home.

To enter or update the boiler size, go to **Settings > Home > Boiler size**. Check that the size is correct if you want the estimate to reflect your boiler.

## Heating history

Dormouse keeps heating history for two years. This history supports the monthly reports and their comparisons. Reports older than two years are not retained as heating history.

## Notifications

Each household member chooses their own app notifications. You can manage yours in **Settings > Notifications**. The app can notify you when your monthly energy report is ready.

Notifications are optional. You can still open the Energy tab to view a report when it is available.

## Getting help

If the Energy screen does not show a report when expected, or its information does not look right, contact Dormouse support in the app under **Help > Contact support**.
