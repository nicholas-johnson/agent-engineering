# Radiator Valve Compatibility Policy

## Purpose and scope

This policy explains which radiator valve bodies the Dormouse Radiator Valve is designed to fit and how the supplied adapters support other common valve types. It applies to Dormouse Radiator Valves used with a Dormouse Thermostat.

Each Radiator Valve joins a zone and heats its radiator to its own temperature. Valves communicate with the thermostat for their zone over a low-power 868 MHz radio link. Only the thermostat uses Wi-Fi.

## Standard compatibility

The Dormouse Radiator Valve fits radiator valve bodies with an M30 x 1.5 connection. This is the valve's standard fit.

Before installation, check the connection on the radiator valve body. Do not assume that a body has an M30 x 1.5 connection based on the radiator or its appearance. If you are unsure which connection it has, ask a qualified heating professional to identify it.

## Compatibility using supplied adapters

Adapters are included in the box for other common valve types. An adapter can provide a compatible connection between the Radiator Valve and a valve body that does not have the standard M30 x 1.5 connection.

Use an adapter only when it is suitable for the valve body. The fact that adapters are supplied does not mean that every valve body is compatible. If you cannot identify the connection or determine which supplied adapter is appropriate, do not force the Radiator Valve or an adapter onto the body. Ask a qualified heating professional for advice.

## Checking before fitting

Check the valve body and the available parts before starting:

1. Identify the connection on the radiator valve body.
2. If it is M30 x 1.5, use the standard connection.
3. If it is another common valve type, check whether a supplied adapter is suitable.
4. If you cannot confirm a suitable fit, stop and seek advice from a qualified heating professional.

A suitable connection must be established before the Radiator Valve is used. Do not rely on force to make parts fit.

## Installation and use

The Radiator Valve is designed to work as part of a Dormouse zone. Up to ten Dormouse Radiator Valves can join a zone, allowing each radiator in that zone to heat to its own temperature. Add a valve to a zone through Settings > Home > Add a device.

Radiator Valves communicate with their zone's thermostat over a low-power 868 MHz radio link. A Radiator Valve does not use Wi-Fi. For the thermostat's Wi-Fi requirements and other setup instructions, follow the Dormouse Thermostat user guide.

## When compatibility is uncertain

Stop fitting the valve if the connection does not match, the adapter does not sit correctly, or the parts appear damaged. Do not modify the valve body, adapter or Radiator Valve to make them fit. Contact Dormouse support in the app under Help > Contact support, or ask a qualified heating professional to assess the valve body.

## Product details

The Dormouse Radiator Valve fits M30 x 1.5 valve bodies and is supplied with adapters for other common valve types. It runs on two AA batteries, which last about two years. These product details do not establish compatibility with a particular valve body; check the connection and use only a suitable supplied adapter.
