# Understand the Dormouse product warranty

## Warranty period

Dormouse products have a two-year warranty. The warranty period is two years from the product's purchase date.

## Products covered

The Dormouse product range includes the Dormouse Thermostat, Dormouse Radiator Valve and Dormouse Room Sensor. Each product has a different power source and installation setup, but the warranty period for Dormouse products is two years.

## Keep your proof of purchase

Keep your proof of purchase so you can confirm when the two-year warranty period began. The purchase date is needed to establish whether a product is still within its warranty period.

## If a product needs help

If you need help with a Dormouse product, contact Dormouse support in the app under Help > Contact support. Include the product details and proof of purchase so support can identify the product and check its warranty period.

## Warranty and product care

Follow the care instructions for your product. For the Dormouse Thermostat, wipe it with a soft, dry cloth. Do not use sprays or cleaning products, and do not paint over it.

## Other time limits

The two-year warranty is separate from other time limits that apply to Dormouse services and features. Returns are available within 30 days. Heating history is kept for two years.
