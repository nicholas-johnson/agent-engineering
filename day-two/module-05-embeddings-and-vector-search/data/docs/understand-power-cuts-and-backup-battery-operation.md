# Understand power cuts and backup battery operation

## What happens when mains power is interrupted

The thermostat uses mains power to operate the heating. If mains power is interrupted, it switches to its two AA backup batteries. The batteries keep the clock and your schedules running for up to 48 hours, but the thermostat cannot switch the heating on while mains power is off.

The thermostat may show E-402 when mains power is interrupted. The app shows the same error code and can notify you if you have enabled notifications for errors.

## What the backup batteries keep running

While the thermostat is on backup batteries, it keeps its clock and schedules for up to 48 hours. This helps preserve your schedule information during a power cut.

Backup batteries do not provide enough power to operate the heating. The thermostat cannot switch the heating on until mains power returns, even if a schedule calls for heat or the room gets cold enough for frost protection.

## What happens to heating

Heating pauses while mains power is off. The thermostat cannot operate the boiler on backup batteries.

Frost protection normally turns on the heating if the room falls below 5 degrees, even when heating is off or away mode is on. However, frost protection cannot switch the heating on during a mains power interruption because the thermostat has no mains power.

When mains power returns, the thermostat can operate the heating again. If it shows an error code, check the code in the app or in the error codes section of this guide.

## What E-402 means

E-402 means mains power is interrupted and the thermostat is running on its backup batteries. The thermostat keeps the clock and schedules for up to 48 hours, but cannot switch the heating on.

The code is not a low-battery warning. E-401 means the backup batteries are low and need replacing. If the display shows E-401, replace both AA batteries within a month.

## Checking the batteries

Two AA batteries behind the front cover provide backup power. To reach them, pull the bottom of the front cover gently towards you.

Replace the batteries once a year, or when the display shows E-401. Use two AA batteries. The batteries help preserve the clock and schedules during a power cut, but do not keep the heating running.

## When mains power returns

Once mains power is restored, the thermostat can switch the heating on again. Check that the thermostat is operating as expected and that any error code has cleared.

If the thermostat continues to show E-402 after power has returned, check that mains power is reaching the thermostat. If it continues to show another error, follow the instructions for that code.

## If the power cut lasts more than 48 hours

The backup batteries keep the clock and schedules for up to 48 hours. The guide does not specify what happens to clock or schedule information if the interruption lasts longer.

The thermostat still cannot switch the heating on while mains power is off. Restore mains power to resume normal heating operation.

## Getting help

For help with a persistent error or a thermostat that does not resume normal operation after mains power returns, contact support in the app under Help > Contact support.
