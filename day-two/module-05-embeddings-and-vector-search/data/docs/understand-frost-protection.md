# Understand frost protection

Frost protection helps stop your pipes freezing. It is always on and cannot be turned off.

## When frost protection heats

If the room temperature falls below 5 degrees, the thermostat turns the heating on. It does this even when the heating is off or away mode is on.

## What you will see

A snowflake appears on the thermostat display while frost protection is heating. When the room is no longer below 5 degrees, frost protection stops heating.

## How it differs from your settings

Frost protection can heat the home regardless of the heating being off or away mode being on. It does not turn those settings off; it provides heat when the room falls below 5 degrees.
