# Check your thermostat firmware version

## Find the firmware version

1. Press the thermostat dial to open the menu.
2. Turn the dial to **About**.
3. Press the dial to choose it. The thermostat displays its firmware version.

## If the display is dim

The display dims after 20 seconds without a touch. Turn the dial to make the target temperature larger, then press it to open the menu. This changes the target temperature. A manual temperature change lasts until the next schedule period starts.
