# iOS app: Away screen guide

## What the Away screen does

Use the Away screen in the Dormouse app to turn away mode on or off and to change the away temperature. Away mode keeps your home at that temperature until you turn it off.

The Away screen is available in the iOS app. You can also turn away mode on from the thermostat by choosing Menu > Away. To change the away temperature, use Settings > Away > Away temperature in the app or web dashboard.

## Turn away mode on

1. Open the Dormouse app and select Away.
2. Turn away mode on.

The home stays at the away temperature until you turn away mode off. The default away temperature is 16 degrees. If you have changed it, the home uses your chosen temperature instead.

When away mode is on, the thermostat display shows a house with an arrow.

## Turn away mode off

Open the Away screen and turn away mode off. The home then follows its schedule, unless another heating action is in effect.

Frost protection remains active when away mode is on or off. If the room falls below 5 degrees, the thermostat turns the heating on to help stop pipes freezing.

## Change the away temperature

1. In the app, open Settings > Away > Away temperature.
2. Choose a temperature.
3. Save the change if the app asks you to.

The away temperature is 16 degrees unless you change it. The thermostat can be set from 5 to 30 degrees in half-degree steps. The temperature you choose is used whenever away mode is on.

## Automatic control with geofencing

Geofencing can turn away mode on and off using the location of household members' phones. Each member chooses whether to share their location in the app.

When every member who shares their location is more than 1 km from home, geofencing turns away mode on. When the first of them comes back within 1 km, it turns away mode off.

To stop geofencing for the whole home, open Settings > Away > Geofencing and turn it off. You can still control away mode manually from the Away screen.

## Away mode and household members

A home can have one owner and up to eight members. Members can change the temperature, schedules and away mode. Only the owner can invite or remove members and add or remove devices.

Each member chooses their own notification preferences in Settings > Notifications. The app can notify you when the heating has been on for 2 hours while everyone is away.

## If the screen does not reflect a change

If the thermostat cannot reach the Dormouse cloud, changes made in the app wait until it reconnects. The thermostat keeps running its schedules while disconnected. Check that your router is connected to the internet if the thermostat shows E-110.

For other thermostat error codes, check the code in the app or on the thermostat display and follow the guidance in the user guide.

## Related controls

Away mode is separate from a manual target-temperature change. A temperature you set by hand lasts until the next schedule period starts. Away mode instead keeps the home at the away temperature until it is turned off, including when the schedule would otherwise call for a different temperature. Frost protection can still turn the heating on if the room falls below 5 degrees.
