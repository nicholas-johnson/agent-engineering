# Start or cancel a boost

A boost heats your home at the boost temperature for one hour. You can start it with the boost button below the thermostat's dial, then cancel it early by pressing the button again.

## Start a boost

Press the boost button once. The thermostat starts heating at the boost temperature, and an upward arrow appears on the display while the boost is running.

The boost temperature is 21 degrees by default. You can change it in Settings > Heating > Boost temperature.

## Cancel a boost

Press the boost button again while the boost is running. The thermostat cancels the boost.

## What happens when a boost ends

A boost runs for one hour unless you cancel it sooner. After it ends, the thermostat returns to its usual heating control. A manual change to the target temperature lasts until the next schedule period starts.
