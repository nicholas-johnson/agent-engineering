# Set up and use the Dormouse Radiator Valve

## About the valve

The Dormouse Radiator Valve controls the temperature of an individual radiator. It joins a zone controlled by a Dormouse Thermostat, so each radiator in that zone can heat to its own temperature.

The valve communicates with the thermostat for its zone over a low-power 868 MHz radio link. It does not connect to Wi-Fi. The thermostat uses Wi-Fi to connect to the Dormouse app and web dashboard.

## Check that it fits your radiator

The valve fits radiator valve bodies with an M30 x 1.5 connection. Adapters are included in the box for other common valve types.

Before fitting, check the connection on your existing radiator valve body. If it is not M30 x 1.5, identify whether one of the included adapters fits. Do not force the valve onto a connection that does not match.

## Prepare the zone

The Radiator Valve must join a zone that has a Dormouse Thermostat. Each thermostat controls its own zone, and up to ten Dormouse Radiator Valves can join that zone.

If you have more than one thermostat, check which one controls the radiator's zone. A valve communicates with its zone's thermostat, not directly with the app or web dashboard.

To add a valve to a zone, use the app or web dashboard and go to Settings > Home > Add a device. Follow the instructions shown there.

## Power the valve

The Dormouse Radiator Valve runs on two AA batteries. The batteries last about two years.

The thermostat's backup batteries and the valve's batteries have different roles: the thermostat's two AA batteries keep its clock and schedules during a power cut, while the valve's two AA batteries power the valve. The thermostat cannot switch the heating on while mains power is off.

## Add the valve to your home

1. Make sure the zone has a Dormouse Thermostat.
2. Open the app or web dashboard.
3. Go to Settings > Home > Add a device.
4. Follow the on-screen instructions to add the Radiator Valve to the intended zone.

Once added, the valve communicates with that zone's thermostat over its low-power radio link. Only the thermostat uses Wi-Fi.

## Use the valve in a zone

A zone can include a thermostat, a Dormouse Room Sensor and up to ten Dormouse Radiator Valves. Each valve controls its own radiator temperature.

The thermostat provides the schedule for its zone. The valves let individual radiators in that zone heat to their own temperatures. For example, one thermostat can control a zone with several radiators, each using its own valve.

## Care and setup notes

The valve uses two AA batteries, which last about two years. The thermostat's display and menus show the thermostat's status; the valve itself communicates with the thermostat assigned to its zone.

If you need to add or manage devices in a zone, go to Settings > Home > Add a device in the app or web dashboard. For help with your Dormouse product, open Help > Contact support in the app.
