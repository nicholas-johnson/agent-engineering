# iOS app: Add thermostat screen guide

## Purpose

Use the Add thermostat screen to connect a Dormouse Thermostat to your Wi-Fi network and Dormouse account. Pairing is completed in the Dormouse app. The thermostat requires a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network; it cannot join a 5 GHz network.

Before starting, make sure the thermostat is wired to the boiler by a qualified installer, and that you have a Dormouse account. The app requires iOS 17 or later.

## Before you begin

Check that your router is on and that its Wi-Fi network is available. If you have changed the network name or password since the thermostat was last paired, you will need to pair the thermostat again.

Have your phone ready and stay close to the thermostat during setup. Keep the phone within 5 metres until the app confirms that pairing is complete.

Pairing must finish within 3 minutes of the thermostat displaying PAIR. Start the app steps only when you are ready to continue through setup.

## Start pairing on the thermostat

1. Hold down the thermostat dial for 5 seconds.
2. Release it when the display shows PAIR.
3. Continue in the Dormouse app.

The 3-minute pairing window begins when PAIR appears on the thermostat display. If it expires before setup is complete, the display shows E-104. Start pairing again by holding the dial down for 5 seconds.

## Open Add thermostat

In the Dormouse app, choose **Add thermostat** and follow the on-screen steps. Keep the app open and your phone within 5 metres of the thermostat while it works.

The app guides you through connecting the thermostat to Wi-Fi and linking it to your Dormouse account. Use a 2.4 GHz network. A 5 GHz network is not supported.

## Complete pairing

Wait for the app to say that pairing has finished before moving your phone away from the thermostat. Once pairing is complete, the thermostat is connected to your Wi-Fi and your Dormouse account.

If the display shows E-104, pairing timed out. Hold the dial down for 5 seconds to show PAIR again, then repeat the app steps. Keep your phone within 5 metres and complete the process within 3 minutes of PAIR appearing.

## If the network is not found

If the thermostat cannot find the Wi-Fi network, it shows E-101. Check that the router is on and that it offers a 2.4 GHz network. The thermostat cannot join a 5 GHz network.

If the Wi-Fi network name or password has changed, pair the thermostat again using **Add thermostat** in the app. Start pairing on the thermostat by holding the dial for 5 seconds, until PAIR appears, and keep within the 3-minute time limit.

## After setup

The thermostat shows room and target temperatures on its display. You can use the Dormouse app to change most settings; menu paths in the app and web dashboard use the same names.

To check the thermostat firmware, press the dial to open the menu and choose **Menu > About**. This guide covers firmware 4.1.
