# Thermostat Backup Battery Access and Replacement

## Purpose

This guide explains how to access and replace the two AA backup batteries in a Dormouse Thermostat. The batteries are for backup: they keep the clock and schedules during a power cut for up to 48 hours. They do not power the heating. The thermostat cannot switch the heating on while mains power is off.

The thermostat is powered by mains through its wall plate. Replacing the backup batteries does not replace or disconnect that mains supply.

## When to replace the batteries

Replace the batteries once a year, or when the display shows E-401. E-401 means the backup batteries are low. Replace them within a month of this code appearing.

The app can also notify you when the backup batteries are low. The display shows a battery symbol when they are low.

## Before you start

You will need two AA batteries. Do not treat these as the thermostat's mains supply: they only provide backup for the clock and schedules during a power cut.

If the display shows E-402, mains power has been interrupted and the thermostat is running on its backup batteries. It can keep the clock and schedules for up to 48 hours, but it cannot switch the heating on. Replacing the batteries does not restore mains power or enable heating while the mains supply is off.

## Access the battery compartment

1. Locate the front cover of the thermostat.
2. Gently pull the bottom of the front cover towards you to reach the batteries.
3. Remove the two existing AA batteries.

Take care when opening the cover. Do not confuse the battery compartment with the thermostat's mains connection at the wall plate.

## Replace the batteries

1. Fit two AA batteries in the battery compartment, following the polarity markings inside.
2. Refit the front cover.

The thermostat uses these batteries to keep the clock and schedules during a power cut. They do not provide power to operate the heating.

## After replacement

Check the display. If E-401 remains, confirm that both batteries are correctly fitted and replace them with two new AA batteries if needed.

If the display shows E-402, check that the thermostat's mains supply has been restored. The backup batteries cannot switch on the heating. If the mains supply is off, the thermostat continues to keep the clock and schedules for up to 48 hours only.

## Battery and power indicators

| Display indication | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Battery symbol or E-401 | Backup batteries are low | Replace the two AA batteries. E-401 calls for replacement within a month. |
| E-402 | Mains power is interrupted; the thermostat is running on backup batteries | Restore mains power. The thermostat cannot switch the heating on while mains power is off. |

## Ongoing care

Replace the backup batteries once a year, even if no low-battery indication has appeared. If the display shows E-401, replace them within a month.

The backup batteries keep the clock and schedules for up to 48 hours during a power cut. They are not a substitute for the thermostat's mains supply.
