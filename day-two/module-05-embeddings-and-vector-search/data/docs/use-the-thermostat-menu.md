# Use the thermostat menu

The thermostat menu gives you access to Schedule, Away, Settings and About. Use the dial to move through the menu and choose an item. The same menu paths shown here apply in the Dormouse app and web dashboard unless stated otherwise.

## Open the menu

Press the dial when the display shows the room temperature. The four menu items appear:

- Schedule
- Away
- Settings
- About

Turn the dial to move between items. Press the dial to choose the highlighted item.

If the display is showing a target temperature because you have turned the dial, press it to confirm the temperature change. When the display shows the room temperature, pressing the dial opens the menu.

## View today's schedule

Choose Menu > Schedule to see today's schedule periods on the thermostat. Each period has a start time and a target temperature. It lasts until the next period starts.

You can view today's periods on the thermostat, but you cannot change them there. To set or edit schedule periods, open the Schedule tab in the app or web dashboard and choose a day. Each day can have up to six periods. To use the same periods on other days, choose Copy day.

A manual temperature change lasts until the next schedule period starts.

## Turn Away mode on

Choose Menu > Away to turn Away mode on. It keeps the home at the away temperature until you turn Away mode off. The away temperature is 16 degrees unless you change it in Settings > Away > Away temperature.

You can also turn Away mode on by tapping Away in the app.

When Away mode is on, the display shows a house with an arrow. Frost protection remains active: if the room falls below 5 degrees, the thermostat turns the heating on even when Away mode is on.

## Use Settings

Choose Menu > Settings to access thermostat settings. Turn the dial to move through the available items, then press it to choose one.

For example, the boost temperature can be changed in Settings > Heating > Boost temperature. It is 21 degrees unless you change it. You can also change the away temperature in Settings > Away > Away temperature.

The thermostat menu is for navigating and choosing settings. Schedule periods can only be changed in the app or web dashboard.

## View About

Choose Menu > About to view information about the thermostat, including its firmware version. The firmware version helps identify which instructions apply to your device.

This help guide covers firmware 4.1. If you need to check the version, press the dial to open the menu, then choose Menu > About.

## Return to the temperature display

After choosing a menu item, follow the display prompts and use the dial to navigate or confirm. When you are finished, return to the room-temperature display. Turn the dial there to adjust the target temperature.

The display dims after 20 seconds without a touch. A dimmed display does not change your schedule or settings.
