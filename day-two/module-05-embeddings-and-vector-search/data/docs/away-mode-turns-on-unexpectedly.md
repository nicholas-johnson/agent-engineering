# Away Mode Turns On Unexpectedly

Away mode can turn on automatically if geofencing is enabled. Geofencing uses the location of household members who share their location. Away mode turns on when every member sharing their location is more than 1 km from home.

Use these checks to find out whether location sharing or the home's geofencing setting is causing the change.

## Check the home's geofencing setting

In the app or web dashboard, go to Settings > Away > Geofencing. Check whether geofencing is on.

If you do not want geofencing to control away mode, turn it off for the whole home in this setting. Away mode can still be turned on manually in the app or by choosing Menu > Away on the thermostat.

## Check each household member's location-sharing choice

Each household member chooses whether to share their location in the app. Check the location-sharing choice for every member of the home. Geofencing uses the location of members who share their location.

If all members who share their location are more than 1 km from home, geofencing turns away mode on. If a member who shares their location comes back within 1 km, geofencing turns away mode off.

## Check who can change household settings

A home can have one owner and up to eight members. Members can change away mode, but only the owner can add or remove devices, invite members or remove them.

If the household membership has changed, the owner can review it under Settings > Household. A member's location-sharing choice is managed by that member in the app.

## Turn away mode off

To turn away mode off, use the Away control in the app. Away mode keeps the home at the away temperature until you turn it off, unless geofencing turns it off when a sharing member returns within 1 km.

The away temperature is 16 degrees unless it has been changed in Settings > Away > Away temperature. Changing the away temperature does not turn geofencing on or off.

## If away mode keeps turning on

Check that geofencing is off if you do not want automatic changes. If it is on, review the location-sharing choices of household members and remember that geofencing turns away mode on when every sharing member is more than 1 km from home.

If the thermostat shows the house-with-an-arrow symbol, away mode is on. Frost protection remains active in away mode and turns the heating on if the room falls below 5 degrees.
