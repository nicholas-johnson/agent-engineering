# Thermostat Shows E-402

## What E-402 means

E-402 means the thermostat's mains power has been interrupted. It is running on its two backup AA batteries.

The batteries keep the clock and your schedules for up to 48 hours during a power cut. They do not power the heating: while mains power is off, the thermostat cannot switch the heating on.

## Check the mains supply

Check whether the home has lost power. If other electrical equipment is also off, the interruption may affect the wider home. Restore mains power or wait for the supply to return.

If the rest of the home has power, check that the thermostat's wall plate is receiving mains power. The thermostat is powered through its wall plate. If you cannot safely identify or restore the supply, contact a qualified electrician.

## Understand what continues during an interruption

The backup batteries preserve the thermostat's clock and schedules for up to 48 hours. They do not keep the thermostat operating the heating, so heating cannot be switched on during the interruption.

Once mains power returns, the thermostat can resume normal operation. If power remains off for longer than the backup period, the clock and schedules may not be preserved.

## Check the display after power returns

When mains power is restored, check the display to see whether E-402 has cleared. The app also shows the thermostat's error code.

If the display continues to show E-402, check the mains supply again. If the supply is restored but the error remains, contact Dormouse support in the app under Help > Contact support.

## If the interruption lasts a long time

The backup batteries can preserve the clock and schedules for up to 48 hours, but they cannot provide heating during a mains interruption. If the supply is expected to remain off, make plans for the home accordingly.

Do not rely on the backup batteries to run the thermostat's heating. They are for preserving the clock and schedules, not for switching the boiler on.
