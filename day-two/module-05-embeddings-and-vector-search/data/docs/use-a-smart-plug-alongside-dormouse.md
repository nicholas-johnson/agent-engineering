# Use a Smart Plug Alongside Dormouse

## Purpose

A smart plug can be used in a separate home automation alongside a Dormouse Thermostat. For example, an automation might switch a lamp or another suitable plug-in device when a chosen event occurs.

The smart plug does not connect to, control or replace the thermostat's boiler controls. The thermostat must remain wired to the boiler by a qualified installer. It manages heating through its own controls, schedules and settings.

## Keep heating controls separate

Do not use a smart plug to interrupt power to the thermostat or its boiler controls. The thermostat is powered by mains through its wall plate, and it needs that power to operate normally. Switching off its supply can stop it controlling the heating.

A smart plug is not a substitute for the thermostat's heating functions. Use the thermostat to set target temperatures, schedules, boost and away mode. Frost protection is always on and cannot be turned off.

## What a smart plug can do

A smart plug can switch a compatible plug-in device on or off as part of a separate automation. The automation and the Dormouse heating controls have distinct roles:

| System | Role |
|---|---|
| Dormouse Thermostat | Controls the heating and communicates with the boiler through its installed wiring. |
| Smart plug | Switches a compatible plug-in device according to its own controls or automation. |

Do not assume that changing the state of a smart plug changes the thermostat's target temperature, schedule or heating mode.

## Plan the automation

Decide which plug-in device the smart plug will operate and what the automation should do. Check that the device is suitable for use with a smart plug and follow its instructions.

Keep the automation independent of the thermostat's boiler supply and wiring. If the automation uses information from another system, check that system's instructions for how its events and actions work. Dormouse does not provide smart-plug setup steps in this guide.

## Set up the smart plug separately

Set up the smart plug using the instructions supplied with it. Configure its automation in the system that manages the smart plug. The thermostat does not pair with a smart plug through its own pairing process.

Dormouse pairing connects the thermostat to Wi-Fi and to your Dormouse account. To pair the thermostat, hold the dial down for 5 seconds until the display shows PAIR, then follow the app's steps. Pairing must finish within 3 minutes, and your phone must stay within 5 metres of the thermostat until the app says it has finished.

## Use Dormouse for heating

Use the thermostat, Dormouse app or web dashboard to manage heating. You can set the target temperature from 5 to 30 degrees in half-degree steps. A manual temperature change lasts until the next schedule period starts.

Schedules are changed in the app or web dashboard. Each day can have up to six periods. You can also use the thermostat menu to view today's periods, or use the thermostat or app to turn away mode on.

The thermostat's boost button starts a one-hour boost at the boost temperature. Use the thermostat or app for heating actions rather than switching a smart plug.

## Check power and operation

Leave the thermostat's mains supply available. Its backup batteries keep the clock and schedules during a power cut for up to 48 hours, but the thermostat cannot switch the heating on while mains power is off. The batteries do not make a smart plug a suitable way to control the boiler.

If the thermostat displays an error, check the code on the thermostat or in the app and follow the guidance in the user guide. For example, E-402 means mains power is interrupted; the thermostat is running on its backup batteries and cannot switch the heating on.

## Troubleshooting the separate automation

If the smart plug does not switch its device as expected, check the smart plug and automation using their own instructions. Confirm that the plug-in device is connected to the smart plug and that the automation is enabled.

If the heating does not respond as expected, check the Dormouse controls independently:

- Check the target temperature and the current schedule period.
- Check whether away mode or a boost is active.
- Check the thermostat display for an error code.
- Confirm that the thermostat has mains power.

Do not change the thermostat's boiler wiring or use a smart plug to switch it. Contact Dormouse support in the app under Help > Contact support if you need help with the thermostat.
