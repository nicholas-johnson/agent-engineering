# Radiator Valve Installation Across Multiple Radiators

## Scope

This guide covers planning, fitting and commissioning Dormouse Radiator Valves on multiple radiators in one zone. A zone is controlled by a Dormouse Thermostat and can include up to ten Dormouse Radiator Valves. Each valve controls the temperature of its own radiator.

Valves communicate with the thermostat for their zone over a low-power 868 MHz radio link. Only the thermostat uses Wi-Fi. The valves do not connect directly to Wi-Fi.

This guide is for installers and confident customers. The thermostat must be wired to the boiler by a qualified installer. Follow the boiler and heating-system manufacturers' instructions when working on those systems.

## Plan the zone

Confirm which radiators belong to the zone and identify the thermostat that controls it. Each home can have up to four Dormouse Thermostats, each controlling its own zone and schedule. A valve must be added to the correct zone; it cannot be used to control a radiator in a different zone.

Count the radiators to be controlled by valves before installation. A zone supports up to ten Dormouse Radiator Valves. Do not plan more than ten valves for one zone.

Check that each radiator has a compatible valve body. Dormouse Radiator Valves fit M30 x 1.5 valve bodies. Adapters for other common valve types are supplied in the box. Confirm the valve body type and choose the appropriate adapter before fitting.

Allow for battery access at each valve. Each valve runs on two AA batteries, which last about two years.

## Check the system and equipment

Before starting, confirm that the zone's thermostat is installed and available to add devices through the Dormouse app or web dashboard. Most settings can be changed in either. The menu paths in this guide are the same in both.

Gather the Dormouse Radiator Valves, two AA batteries for each valve, and any required adapter from the supplied set. Use only the supplied adapters for compatible valve bodies.

The valves communicate with their zone thermostat over a low-power 868 MHz radio link. Keep the thermostat and valves in their intended installed positions when checking communication. A valve's radio link is to the thermostat for its zone, not to the home Wi-Fi network.

## Fit each valve

Fit one valve to each radiator that is to have individual temperature control. Use the adapter supplied for the valve body where needed, and follow the valve's fitting instructions. Do not force a connection that does not match the valve body.

Install two AA batteries in each valve as instructed for the device. Check that each valve is securely fitted and that its batteries are in place before adding it to the zone.

Repeat the fitting and battery checks for every radiator in the planned group. Keep a record of which physical radiator each valve is fitted to. This makes it easier to identify and check individual devices during commissioning and later schedule or temperature changes.

## Add valves to the zone

Use the app or web dashboard to add the valves to the intended zone:

1. Open Settings > Home > Add a device.
2. Follow the on-screen steps to add a Dormouse Radiator Valve.
3. Select the zone controlled by the correct thermostat, if prompted.
4. Repeat for each valve, confirming the zone as you go.

Check the zone's valve count as devices are added. A zone can have up to ten Dormouse Radiator Valves. If the zone already has ten, do not add another valve to it.

The thermostat is the device that joins the home Wi-Fi. The valves communicate with their zone's thermostat over the 868 MHz radio link; they do not need to join Wi-Fi separately.

## Commission the installation

After adding the valves, check that each one appears in the intended zone and corresponds to the radiator recorded during fitting. Confirm that the zone contains no more than ten valves.

Use the app or web dashboard to check the zone's schedule and make any required schedule changes. Each thermostat controls its own zone and has its own schedule. A schedule period has a start time and target temperature; it lasts until the next period begins. On the thermostat, Menu > Schedule shows today's periods, but schedules can only be changed in the app or web dashboard.

Check the zone's operation against its schedule and the temperature settings selected for the home. A manual temperature change made at the thermostat lasts until the next schedule period starts. The Dormouse Radiator Valve allows each radiator in the zone to heat to its own temperature.

## Checks after commissioning

Review the finished installation against this checklist:

| Check | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Zone assignment | Every valve is assigned to the thermostat that controls its zone. |
| Valve count | No more than ten valves are assigned to the zone. |
| Valve fit | Each valve fits its radiator valve body, using a supplied adapter where needed. |
| Power | Each valve has two AA batteries installed. |
| Device record | Each valve is identified with its corresponding radiator. |
| Schedule | The zone's schedule is available in the app or web dashboard. |

If a device does not appear in the intended zone, revisit Settings > Home > Add a device and check the device assignment. Confirm that the thermostat for the zone is the one being used for setup.

## Maintenance and support

Dormouse Radiator Valves run on two AA batteries, which last about two years. Include battery checks in routine maintenance, and replace batteries when needed. Keep each valve accessible so its batteries can be reached.

For help with installation or a device that cannot be added to its zone, contact Dormouse support in the app under Help > Contact support.
