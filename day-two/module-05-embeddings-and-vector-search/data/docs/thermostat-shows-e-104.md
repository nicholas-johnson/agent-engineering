# Thermostat Shows E-104

## What E-104 means

E-104 means pairing timed out. Pairing connects the thermostat to your Wi-Fi network and Dormouse account. It must finish within 3 minutes of the display showing PAIR. If it takes longer, the thermostat shows E-104 and pairing needs to be started again.

## Before you start

Have the Dormouse app open on your phone and make sure you can sign in to your Dormouse account. Keep the phone within 5 metres of the thermostat throughout pairing. Use a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network; the thermostat cannot join a 5 GHz network.

## Start pairing again

1. Hold the thermostat dial down for 5 seconds, until the display shows PAIR.
2. In the Dormouse app, choose Add thermostat and follow the setup steps.
3. Keep your phone within 5 metres of the thermostat.
4. Finish the app setup within 3 minutes of the display showing PAIR.

If setup takes longer than 3 minutes, the display shows E-104. Start again from step 1.

## If pairing times out again

Check that you begin the app setup as soon as PAIR appears, and keep the phone nearby until the app says setup is finished. If the app does not finish within 3 minutes, restart pairing using the steps above.

The thermostat must connect to a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network. If your network name or password has changed, pair the thermostat again using the current network details.

## After pairing

When the app says setup has finished, pairing is complete. The thermostat is connected to your Wi-Fi network and Dormouse account. If E-104 appears again, restart pairing and make sure the app setup finishes within 3 minutes while your phone stays within 5 metres of the thermostat.
