# Thermostat Shows the Low-Battery Symbol

## What the symbol means

The battery symbol on the Dormouse Thermostat display means the backup batteries are low. These batteries keep the clock and your schedules during a power cut, for up to 48 hours. They do not power the heating: if mains power is off, the thermostat cannot switch the heating on.

Check the display for an error code as well. If it shows E-401, the backup batteries are low and should be replaced within a month. If the battery symbol appears without E-401, use the steps below to check the code and the batteries.

## Check for E-401

Look at the thermostat display for E-401. The app shows the same error code and can notify you about it.

If the display is dim, touch the dial or turn it to wake the display. The display dims after 20 seconds without a touch.

- **E-401 is shown:** Replace the two AA backup batteries within a month.
- **E-401 is not shown:** The battery symbol still indicates low backup batteries. Replace the batteries, then check whether the symbol remains.
- **E-402 is shown:** Mains power has been interrupted. The thermostat is running on its backup batteries and cannot switch the heating on until mains power is restored.

## Before replacing the batteries

The batteries are behind the front cover. You will need two AA batteries.

Replace the batteries once a year, or when the display shows E-401. The thermostat's backup batteries keep the clock and schedules during a power cut for up to 48 hours, but they do not allow the thermostat to switch on the heating while mains power is off.

## Replace the two AA batteries

1. Gently pull the bottom of the front cover towards you to reach the batteries.
2. Remove the two old AA batteries.
3. Fit two replacement AA batteries in their place.
4. Refit the front cover.

Do not use sprays or cleaning products on the thermostat. If you need to clean it, wipe it with a soft, dry cloth.

## Check the display after replacement

After replacing the batteries, check the display for the battery symbol and any error code. If E-401 was showing, check that it no longer appears. The app also shows error codes displayed by the thermostat.

If the battery symbol or E-401 remains, check that both batteries have been replaced with two AA batteries and are fitted in place. If the issue continues, contact Dormouse support in the app under Help > Contact support.

## If the thermostat shows E-402

E-402 means the mains power has been interrupted. The thermostat runs on its backup batteries, keeping the clock and schedules for up to 48 hours, but it cannot switch the heating on while mains power is off.

Check that mains power has been restored. Replacing the backup batteries does not restore mains power or allow the thermostat to heat the home while mains power remains off.
