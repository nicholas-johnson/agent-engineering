# Enter your boiler size for energy reports

## Why enter your boiler size

Dormouse uses the boiler size you enter to estimate the gas used for heating. The estimate appears in your monthly energy report in the app's Energy tab.

The report also shows how many hours the heating was on each day, compared with the month before. Reports arrive on the 1st of each month.

## Enter or update the boiler size

You can enter or change the boiler size in the app or the web dashboard:

1. Open **Settings > Home > Boiler size**.
2. Enter your boiler's size.
3. Save the change.

The setting applies to the home. You can update it later if the boiler changes or you need to correct the information.

## Finding your boiler's size

Use the size shown for your boiler in its documentation or on its information label. If you cannot identify it, check the boiler's manual or contact a registered gas engineer. Do not guess if you are unsure.

## About the estimate

The gas-use figure is an estimate, and its accuracy depends on the boiler size entered. It is not a direct measurement of gas consumption.

Heating history is kept for two years. The monthly report is available in the app's Energy tab.
