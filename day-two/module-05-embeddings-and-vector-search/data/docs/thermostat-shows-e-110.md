# Thermostat Shows E-110

## What E-110 means

E-110 means the thermostat cannot reach the Dormouse cloud. The thermostat may still be connected to your Wi-Fi, but it cannot communicate with the service used by the app and web dashboard.

While it is offline, the thermostat keeps running its schedules. Changes you make in the app wait until the thermostat reconnects, so they may not take effect straight away. The thermostat does not stop heating just because E-110 appears.

## Check the router's internet connection

Check that your router is switched on and connected to the internet. If other devices in your home cannot reach the internet either, the problem may be with the router's connection rather than the thermostat.

If the router appears to be working, check whether its internet connection has been interrupted. Restore the connection if needed, then give the thermostat time to reconnect.

## Check the thermostat's connection

Look at the thermostat display for the Wi-Fi symbol with a cross. This symbol means the thermostat is not connected to Wi-Fi. E-110, however, specifically means the thermostat cannot reach the Dormouse cloud.

The thermostat needs a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network. It cannot join a 5 GHz network. If you have changed your Wi-Fi network's name or password, pair the thermostat again using the app.

## Allow time for reconnection

When the internet connection is restored, the thermostat can reconnect to the Dormouse cloud. Until it does, it continues to run its schedules, but changes made in the app wait.

Do not rely on an app change having taken effect while E-110 is showing. Check the thermostat after it reconnects to confirm the current target temperature or settings.

## If E-110 remains

If your router is connected to the internet but the thermostat still shows E-110, contact Dormouse support in the app under Help > Contact support. Include the E-110 code and say whether the thermostat shows the Wi-Fi symbol with a cross.
