# Thermostat Placement and Room Temperature Sensing

## Purpose

This guide covers indoor placement of the Dormouse Thermostat and explains its temperature measurement accuracy and operating range. Choose a suitable position before installation so the thermostat can measure the room where it is installed.

The thermostat is designed for indoor use between 0 and 40 degrees. Its temperature measurement is accurate to 0.3 degrees.

## Choose an indoor position

Install the thermostat indoors, in a position that represents the temperature of the area its zone controls. Avoid placing it where local conditions are likely to differ from the rest of that area. A position exposed to unusual heat, cold or airflow may not reflect the temperature people experience in the room.

Consider the thermostat's operating range when choosing the location. The surrounding temperature must remain between 0 and 40 degrees.

## Measurement accuracy

The Dormouse Thermostat measures temperature with an accuracy of 0.3 degrees. Accuracy describes how close a measurement is to the actual temperature; it does not mean that every point in a room has the same temperature.

Room temperature can vary with position and local conditions. For consistent control, place the thermostat where it can measure the area it is intended to control, rather than a locally warmer or cooler spot.

## Thermostat dimensions and display

The thermostat is 84 mm across and 24 mm deep. Its 2.4-inch round colour display shows the room temperature in large numbers and the target temperature underneath. When the dial is turned, the target temperature becomes larger while it is being changed.

The thermostat is powered by mains through its wall plate. It also has two AA backup batteries behind the front cover. These keep the clock and schedules during a power cut for up to 48 hours, but the thermostat cannot switch the heating on while mains power is off.

## Room Sensor and zone sensing

A Dormouse Room Sensor measures temperature and humidity in another room of the same zone. It uses one CR2032 coin cell, which lasts about a year. Up to three Room Sensors can join a zone.

To add a sensor to a zone, go to Settings > Home > Add a device. The Room Sensor communicates with the zone's thermostat over a low-power 868 MHz radio link. Only the thermostat uses Wi-Fi.

A Room Sensor provides readings from its own room; it does not change the thermostat's indoor operating range of 0 to 40 degrees.

## Installation and connection

The thermostat must be wired to the boiler by a qualified installer. It connects to a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network; it cannot join a 5 GHz network. Room Sensors and Radiator Valves communicate with their zone's thermostat over the 868 MHz radio link.

A home can have up to four Dormouse Thermostats, each controlling its own zone with its own schedule. A Room Sensor can join a zone to measure conditions in another room of that zone.

## Check operation after installation

After installation, the display shows the room temperature and target temperature. Turn the dial to change the target temperature. The target can be set from 5 to 30 degrees in half-degree steps. A hand-set change lasts until the next schedule period starts.

The thermostat asks the boiler for heat when needed. The flame symbol indicates that it is asking the boiler for heat. If the room falls below 5 degrees, frost protection turns the heating on, even when heating is off or Away mode is on.

## Care and support

Wipe the thermostat with a soft, dry cloth. Do not use sprays or cleaning products, and do not paint over it.

For installation or product support, contact Dormouse in the app under Help > Contact support.
