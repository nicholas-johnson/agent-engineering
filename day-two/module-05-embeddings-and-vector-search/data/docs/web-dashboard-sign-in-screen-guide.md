# Web dashboard: Sign-in screen guide

## About this guide

This guide explains how to sign in to the Dormouse web dashboard with your email address and password, and how to request a password-reset link if you cannot sign in.

The same account is used for the Dormouse app on Android and iOS and for the web dashboard. You can use your account to access your home's thermostat settings and other features.

## What you need to sign in

To sign in, you need the email address and password for your Dormouse account. Your password must have at least 10 characters.

If you are signing in on a new browser, and two-step verification is turned on for your account, you also need a 6-digit verification code. The code is sent by email, or provided by an authenticator app if you have set one up.

## Sign in

1. Open the Dormouse web dashboard.
2. Enter the email address for your Dormouse account.
3. Enter your password.
4. If asked, enter the 6-digit code from your email or authenticator app.
5. Continue to your account.

Two-step verification is required on a new browser when it is enabled for your account. To turn it on or check its status in the app or web dashboard, go to Settings > Account > Two-step verification.

## Request a password-reset link

If you have forgotten your password, use the password-reset option on the sign-in screen:

1. Choose **Forgot password?**
2. Enter the email address for your Dormouse account.
3. Submit the request.

Dormouse sends a password-reset link to that email address. Open the link and follow the instructions to set a new password.

## Password-reset link expiry

A password-reset link works for 30 minutes and can only be used once. If it has expired, request another link from the sign-in screen. Use the latest link you requested; an earlier link cannot be used again once it has been used.

## After resetting your password

Return to the sign-in screen and sign in with your email address and new password. If two-step verification is enabled and you are signing in on a new browser, enter the 6-digit code when prompted.

## Account access across Dormouse

Your Dormouse account works with the app for Android and iOS and with the web dashboard. Sign in using the email address and password for the account in each. Account settings, including two-step verification, can be changed in the app or web dashboard.

## Protect your account

Keep your password private. Choose a password with at least 10 characters. If you use two-step verification, keep access to the email account or authenticator app that provides your code so you can complete sign-in on a new browser.
