# Android app: Notifications screen guide

## What the Notifications screen does

The Notifications screen lets each household member choose which Dormouse app notifications they receive. You can select notifications for thermostat errors, low backup batteries, heating while everyone is away, installed firmware updates and monthly energy reports.

Notification choices are personal to each member. Changing your choices does not change what other members receive.

This guide covers the Android app. The same notification settings are available in the iOS app and web dashboard.

## Open Notifications

1. Open the Dormouse app and sign in.
2. Open **Settings**.
3. Tap **Notifications**.

The screen lists the notification types available for your account. Use the controls beside each type to choose whether you want to receive it. Your choices apply to your own account.

## Thermostat errors

Choose whether the app notifies you when a thermostat shows an error code. The app can show the same error code as the thermostat display.

An error notification can help you identify what needs attention. The response depends on the code. For example, E-401 means the backup batteries are low, while E-110 means the thermostat cannot reach the Dormouse cloud. Check the code in the thermostat guide for its meaning and recommended action.

## Low backup batteries

Choose whether to receive a notification when the thermostat's backup batteries are low. The thermostat also shows E-401 on its display when the batteries need replacing.

Replace the two AA batteries behind the front cover within a month of E-401 appearing. The backup batteries keep the clock and schedules during a power cut, for up to 48 hours. They do not let the thermostat switch the heating on while mains power is off.

## Heating while everyone is away

Choose whether to receive a notification when the heating has been on for 2 hours while everyone is away.

This notification concerns heating activity while the home is away. Away mode keeps the home at its configured away temperature until it is turned off. Frost protection can still turn the heating on if the room falls below 5 degrees, even when away mode is on.

## Firmware updates

Choose whether to receive a notification when a firmware update has been installed.

The thermostat installs updates automatically between 02:00 and 04:00 local time. During installation, its display shows UPDATING and the thermostat restarts. The process takes about two minutes, and the heating pauses until it has finished.

If an update fails, the thermostat shows E-510, keeps its current firmware and tries again the next night. Error-code notifications are controlled separately from firmware-update notifications.

## Monthly energy reports

Choose whether to receive a notification when your monthly energy report is ready. Reports arrive on the 1st of each month in the app's Energy tab.

A report shows how many hours the heating was on each day, compared with the month before, and an estimate of gas used in kWh. The estimate depends on the size of your boiler. Set the boiler size in **Settings > Home > Boiler size**.

## Review your choices

You can return to **Settings > Notifications** at any time to review or change your notification choices. Each household member manages their own settings, so other members should make their choices in their own accounts.

Notifications are one way to stay informed about activity and issues. You can also check the thermostat display and the app for current status. The app shows the same error code as the thermostat when an error occurs.
