# Understand heating history retention

## What heating history is

Heating history records how long your heating was on. It helps you review your home's heating activity over time.

Your Dormouse Thermostat can have its own schedule and control its own zone. A home can have up to four thermostats, each controlling a separate zone.

## How long history is kept

Heating history is kept for 2 years. Information older than that is not retained as heating history.

## What heating history supports

The monthly energy report uses heating activity to show how many hours the heating was on each day. It compares those daily hours with the previous month and includes an estimate of gas used in kWh.

The report is available in the app's Energy tab on the 1st of each month. The gas-use estimate depends on your boiler size. To enter or change the boiler size, go to Settings > Home > Boiler size.

## Heating history and energy estimates

The report's gas-use figure is an estimate, not a direct measurement of gas consumption. Entering your boiler size helps the report estimate gas use. The report also shows heating hours, so you can compare how long the heating was on from one day to another and with the previous month.

## Who can see heating information

Household members can change the temperature, schedules and away mode. Each member chooses their own notifications in Settings > Notifications. The guide does not specify separate access controls for heating history.

## Heating history during connection issues

If the thermostat cannot reach the Dormouse cloud, it continues to run its schedules. Changes made in the app wait until the thermostat reconnects. If the display shows E-110, check that your router is connected to the internet.

The guide does not specify whether heating history is available in the app while the thermostat is disconnected.

## Related heating information

A schedule sets the target temperature at different times of day. Each day can have up to six periods, and a period lasts until the next one starts. A manual change to the target temperature lasts until the next schedule period starts.

To view today's periods on the thermostat, choose Menu > Schedule. Schedules can only be changed in the app or the web dashboard.
