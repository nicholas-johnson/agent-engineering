# Understand the thermostat's indoor placement limits

## Where the thermostat can be used

The Dormouse Thermostat is intended for indoor use, where the surrounding temperature is between 0 and 40 degrees. These are the limits for the conditions around the thermostat itself.

The thermostat is not intended for outdoor use. Place it indoors in a location that stays within the specified temperature range.

## What the temperature range means

The 0 to 40 degrees range describes the conditions in which the thermostat is designed to operate. It is separate from the target temperature you choose for your home.

You can set a target temperature from 5 to 30 degrees, in half-degree steps. The thermostat measures the room temperature and uses the target temperature as the heating goal. Setting a target within that range does not change the thermostat's indoor-use limits.

## Choosing a location

Before installing the thermostat, consider whether the location is likely to remain between 0 and 40 degrees. A suitable indoor location should not be exposed to conditions that take it outside that range.

The thermostat must be wired to the boiler by a qualified installer. If you are unsure whether a proposed position is suitable, discuss the location with the installer before fitting the thermostat.

## The thermostat's room reading

The display shows the room temperature in large numbers, with the target temperature underneath. The thermostat measures temperature at its own location, so its reading is the temperature where the thermostat is installed.

A Dormouse Room Sensor can join a zone to measure the temperature in another room in that zone. Up to three Room Sensors can join a zone. Sensors and radiator valves communicate with their zone's thermostat over a low-power radio link; only the thermostat uses Wi-Fi.

## Keep the operating limits in mind

The thermostat's specified indoor-use range is 0 to 40 degrees. If a location may fall below 0 degrees or rise above 40 degrees, choose another indoor position within the range.

Frost protection is a heating feature, not a way to extend the thermostat's operating range. If the room falls below 5 degrees, the thermostat turns the heating on to help stop pipes freezing. Frost protection does not change the 0 to 40 degrees indoor-use limit.

## Before installation

Use this checklist when deciding where the thermostat should go:

1. Choose an indoor location.
2. Check that the location is expected to remain between 0 and 40 degrees.
3. Have a qualified installer wire the thermostat to the boiler.
4. If you need a temperature reading in another room of the same zone, consider adding a Dormouse Room Sensor.

The thermostat is 84 mm across and 24 mm deep. Its round colour display measures 2.4 inches. These dimensions can help you check that the chosen position has enough space.

## If the location is outside the range

Do not install the thermostat in a location that is expected to be colder than 0 degrees or warmer than 40 degrees. Move it to a suitable indoor location before use.

If you are unsure about placement or installation, contact Dormouse support in the app under Help > Contact support.
