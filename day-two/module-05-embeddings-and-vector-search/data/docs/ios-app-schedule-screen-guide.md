# iOS app: Schedule screen guide

## About this guide

This guide explains how to use the Schedule screen in the Dormouse iOS app. You can choose a day, set its schedule periods and copy those periods to other days.

The schedule controls what temperature the thermostat targets at different times of day. Each day can have up to six periods. A period has a start time and a target temperature, and lasts until the next period starts.

## Open the Schedule screen

Open the Schedule tab in the Dormouse app to view and edit your schedule. Choose a day to see its periods.

You can also set schedules in the web dashboard. On the thermostat, Menu > Schedule shows today's periods, but you can only change them in the app or the web dashboard.

## Choose a day

Select the day you want to work on. The screen shows the periods assigned to that day. Review the displayed day before making changes, especially if you have copied periods between days.

Each day has its own schedule. You can set different periods for different days, or use Copy day to apply one day's periods to other days.

## Understand schedule periods

A period consists of a start time and a target temperature. It applies from its start time until the next period starts. The last period continues until the next day's schedule begins.

You can set up to six periods per day. Choose start times and temperatures that represent the changes you want through the day. For example, a day could have one target temperature in the morning and another later in the day.

## Set up periods

1. Open the Schedule tab.
2. Choose the day you want to edit.
3. Set the start time and target temperature for each period.
4. Add or adjust periods as needed, up to six for the day.
5. Review the day's periods to check their times and temperatures.

The schedule uses the target temperatures you set for each period. When a period starts, it becomes the current schedule target. A temperature change made by hand lasts until the next schedule period starts.

## Copy one day's periods

Use Copy day when you want other days to follow the same periods.

1. Choose the day whose periods you want to reuse.
2. Check that its start times and target temperatures are correct.
3. Choose Copy day.
4. Select the other days that should use those periods.

Copy day is useful when several days share a routine. Choose and review the destination days carefully so that you only apply the copied periods where you want them.

## Review your schedule

After editing or copying, choose each relevant day and check its periods. Confirm that the start times and target temperatures match the routine you want for that day. Each day can have up to six periods.

Warm Wake may start the heating before a schedule period. The sunrise symbol appears on the thermostat display while Warm Wake is heating ahead of a period.
