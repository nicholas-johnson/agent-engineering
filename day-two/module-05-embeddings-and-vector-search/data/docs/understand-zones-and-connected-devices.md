# Understand zones and connected devices

## How zones work

A Dormouse home can have up to four Dormouse Thermostats. Each thermostat controls its own zone and runs its own schedule. This lets you set different temperatures and schedules for different parts of your home. For example, one thermostat can control the downstairs radiators and another the upstairs radiators.

A zone is controlled by its thermostat. Room sensors and radiator valves can join that zone to provide additional temperature measurement or control at individual radiators.

## Add a thermostat, sensor or valve

Add devices from the Dormouse app or web dashboard:

1. Go to Settings > Home > Add a device.
2. Follow the instructions to add the thermostat, room sensor or radiator valve to your home.
3. Add a room sensor or radiator valve to the zone you want it to be part of.

Each thermostat controls its own zone. A home can have up to four thermostats, and each zone can have up to ten Dormouse Radiator Valves and three Dormouse Room Sensors.

## Room sensors

A Dormouse Room Sensor measures temperature and humidity in another room of its zone. This can help the zone account for conditions in a room away from its thermostat.

Each Room Sensor uses one CR2032 coin cell, which lasts about a year. Room sensors communicate with the thermostat for their zone using a low-power 868 MHz radio link. They do not connect directly to Wi-Fi.

## Radiator valves

A Dormouse Radiator Valve lets a radiator in a zone heat to its own temperature. Up to ten valves can join a zone, so individual radiators can have their own temperatures while remaining part of that zone.

The valve fits M30 x 1.5 valve bodies. Adapters for other common valve types are included in the box. Each valve runs on two AA batteries, which last about two years.

Valves communicate with their zone's thermostat over a low-power 868 MHz radio link. They do not connect directly to Wi-Fi.

## Connection types

Only the thermostat uses Wi-Fi. Room sensors and radiator valves communicate with their zone's thermostat over the 868 MHz radio link.

The thermostat needs a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network; it cannot join a 5 GHz network. During setup, keep your phone within 5 metres of the thermostat until pairing is complete.

## Schedules and temperature control

Each thermostat runs the schedule for its own zone. A schedule can have up to six periods per day, and periods can be set in the app or web dashboard. On the thermostat, Menu > Schedule shows today's periods, but schedules can only be changed in the app or web dashboard.

The room sensor measures conditions in another room of its zone, while each radiator valve heats its radiator to its own temperature. These devices are assigned to a zone, not used as separate thermostats.

## Check your devices

To add a thermostat, sensor or valve, go to Settings > Home > Add a device in the app or web dashboard. A Room Sensor or Radiator Valve must be added to the intended zone.

If you need help with a device, contact Dormouse support in the app under Help > Contact support.
