# Connect Dormouse to a Voice Assistant

## Before you start

You can connect the Dormouse Thermostat to a voice assistant that supports Dormouse. The connection lets you use voice commands to change the thermostat's target temperature.

You will need:

- A Dormouse Thermostat paired with your Dormouse account.
- Access to the voice assistant's app or setup process.
- The sign-in details for your Dormouse account.

The thermostat must be connected to Wi-Fi to communicate with the Dormouse cloud. It can join a 2.4 GHz network, but not a 5 GHz network.

## Check your thermostat and account

Make sure you can use the Dormouse app or web dashboard and that your thermostat appears in your home. If you have not paired it yet, complete pairing in the Dormouse app first.

The voice assistant connects to your Dormouse account, rather than directly to the thermostat. Changes made through the assistant are sent through the Dormouse cloud. If the thermostat cannot reach the cloud, it keeps running its schedules, but changes made in the app wait until it reconnects.

## Connect the voice assistant

Open the voice assistant's app or setup process and look for its option to add or connect a thermostat service. Choose Dormouse if it is listed, then follow the instructions shown there.

When prompted, sign in with your Dormouse account and approve access to the thermostat information needed for control. The assistant may then ask you to choose or confirm the home and thermostat to connect. Complete the setup in the assistant's app.

The names of screens and steps vary between voice assistants. Follow the instructions in the assistant's app; Dormouse menu paths are not needed to connect the assistant.

## Make the thermostat available

After the connection is complete, use the assistant's app to find or refresh connected devices. Confirm that the Dormouse Thermostat appears and is assigned to the right home or room in that app.

If you have more than one Dormouse Thermostat, check which one the assistant will control before using a voice command. Each thermostat controls its own zone and has its own schedule.

## Change the target temperature by voice

Use the voice assistant's supported thermostat command and say the target temperature you want. For example, ask it to set the thermostat to 20 degrees. The assistant may require you to name the thermostat or room.

The target temperature can be set from 5 to 30 degrees, in half-degree steps. After a manual change, that temperature remains in effect until the next schedule period starts. Check the assistant's app or the thermostat display to confirm the change.

## Check that a command worked

After speaking a command, check the target temperature shown on the thermostat or in the Dormouse app. When you turn the thermostat's dial, the target temperature becomes larger while you change it; the display returns to its usual view after 20 seconds without a touch.

If the target temperature has not changed, confirm that:

- The assistant is connected to the correct Dormouse account and thermostat.
- The thermostat is connected to Wi-Fi.
- The command used a temperature within the supported range.
- The assistant's app shows the Dormouse connection as active.

## If the thermostat is offline

A Wi-Fi symbol with a cross means the thermostat is not connected to Wi-Fi. Check that your router is on and offers a 2.4 GHz network. If the network name or password has changed, pair the thermostat again.

If the display shows E-110, the thermostat cannot reach the Dormouse cloud. Check that your router is connected to the internet. The thermostat continues running its schedules, but changes made in the app wait until it reconnects. Voice commands that need the cloud may not take effect until the connection is restored.

## Disconnect the voice assistant

To stop using voice control, remove or disconnect Dormouse in the voice assistant's app. The steps depend on the assistant. Disconnecting it does not remove the thermostat from your Dormouse account or change its schedules.

For help with your Dormouse thermostat or account, open the Dormouse app and go to Help > Contact support.
