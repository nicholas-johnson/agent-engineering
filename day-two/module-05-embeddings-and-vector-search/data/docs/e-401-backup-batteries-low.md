# E-401: Backup batteries low

## What E-401 means

E-401 means the thermostat's backup batteries are low. The batteries are two AA batteries behind the front cover. They keep the clock and your schedules during a power cut, for up to 48 hours.

E-401 appears about a month before the backup batteries run out. Replace them within a month of seeing the code.

## What causes it

The thermostat displays E-401 when its backup batteries need replacing. The batteries can become low through normal use over time.

These batteries do not power the heating. If mains power is off, the thermostat cannot switch the heating on, even while its backup batteries keep the clock and schedules.

## Before you start

You will need two replacement AA batteries. The batteries are behind the thermostat's front cover. Do not use sprays or cleaning products on the thermostat.

## Replace the batteries

1. Pull the bottom of the front cover gently towards you to reach the batteries.
2. Remove the old batteries.
3. Fit two new AA batteries.
4. Replace the front cover.

Replace the batteries within a month of the display showing E-401.

## After replacing the batteries

Check the display. If E-401 remains, make sure both batteries are fitted correctly and try again with two new AA batteries.

The thermostat uses the backup batteries to keep the clock and schedules during a power cut, for up to 48 hours. It cannot switch the heating on while mains power is off.

## If the code remains

If E-401 stays on the display after you have fitted two new AA batteries, contact Dormouse support in the app under Help > Contact support.

## Avoiding a repeat

Replace the backup batteries once a year, or when the display shows E-401. Regular replacement helps keep the thermostat's clock and schedules during a power cut.
