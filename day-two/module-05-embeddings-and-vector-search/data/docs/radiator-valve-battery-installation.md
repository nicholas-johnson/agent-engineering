# Radiator Valve Battery Installation

## Scope

This guide covers fitting the two AA batteries in a Dormouse Radiator Valve and the expected battery service life. It is intended for installers and confident customers.

The valve uses its batteries for power. It communicates with the thermostat for its zone over a low-power 868 MHz radio link. Only the thermostat uses Wi-Fi.

## Before you begin

You will need two AA batteries. The batteries are supplied separately from the valve. Choose batteries that are suitable for the valve and fit both with the same orientation as the markings inside the battery compartment.

The Dormouse Radiator Valve is designed to fit M30 x 1.5 valve bodies. Adapters for other common valve types are included in the box. This guide covers the batteries only; it does not cover fitting the valve to a radiator.

## Battery service life

Two AA batteries typically last about two years. Actual service life can vary with use and operating conditions.

When replacing the batteries, replace both at the same time. Do not mix old and new batteries, or batteries of different types.

## Fit the batteries

1. Make sure you can reach the battery compartment on the valve.
2. Open the compartment as marked on the valve.
3. Check the polarity markings inside the compartment.
4. Insert two AA batteries, matching each battery's positive and negative ends to the markings.
5. Close the compartment securely.

Do not force the batteries into place. If they do not fit easily, remove them and check their orientation and size.

## Check operation

After closing the compartment, allow the valve to resume operation. Confirm that it can communicate with the thermostat in its zone. The valve communicates with the zone thermostat over a low-power 868 MHz radio link; it does not connect to Wi-Fi.

If the valve does not operate as expected, check that both batteries are seated correctly and that their orientation matches the compartment markings. If necessary, remove and refit both batteries.

## Battery replacement

Replace the two AA batteries together when they are depleted or when the valve no longer operates as expected. Use the same fitting steps in this guide and check the polarity markings before closing the compartment.

The expected service life is about two years. This is an estimate, not a fixed replacement interval: battery life depends on use and operating conditions.
