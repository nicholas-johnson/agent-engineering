# Room Sensor Zone Assignment

## Purpose

This guide explains how to add a Dormouse Room Sensor to a thermostat zone. A sensor measures temperature and humidity in another room in that zone. The thermostat continues to control the zone's heating.

A home can have up to four Dormouse Thermostats. Each thermostat controls its own zone, with its own schedule. Up to three Dormouse Room Sensors can join a zone.

## Before you begin

You need:

- A Dormouse Thermostat already set up in the home.
- A Dormouse Room Sensor.
- Access to the Dormouse app or web dashboard, signed in to the home.

The thermostat must be connected to Wi-Fi for app and dashboard settings to reach it. The Room Sensor does not use Wi-Fi: it communicates with its zone's thermostat over a low-power 868 MHz radio link.

## Choose the zone

Decide which thermostat zone the sensor will join before adding it. A sensor measures conditions in another room of its assigned zone; it does not create a separate zone or have its own schedule.

Check that you have selected the right home and thermostat zone in the app or web dashboard. A home can have no more than three Room Sensors in any one zone.

## Add the sensor

Use the app or web dashboard to assign the Room Sensor:

1. Open Settings > Home > Add a device.
2. Follow the on-screen steps to add the Dormouse Room Sensor.
3. When asked to choose a zone, select the thermostat zone where the sensor will be used.
4. Complete the steps shown in the app or dashboard.

The menu path is the same in the app and the web dashboard. Keep the sensor near the selected zone's thermostat while following the on-screen steps, so the devices can communicate over their radio link.

## Place the sensor

Install the sensor in the room whose temperature and humidity you want it to measure. It reports those readings to the thermostat in its assigned zone over the 868 MHz radio link.

The sensor is battery powered by one CR2032 coin cell. Its battery lasts about a year. The thermostat, not the sensor, uses Wi-Fi.

## Confirm the assignment

After setup, check the app or web dashboard to confirm that the Room Sensor appears in the intended zone and that its readings are available. If the sensor appears under the wrong zone, use Settings > Home > Add a device and follow the available device-management steps to correct the assignment.

A Room Sensor measures temperature and humidity in another room of its zone. It does not replace the thermostat or change the zone's schedule.

## Limits and care

Each zone supports up to three Dormouse Room Sensors and up to ten Dormouse Radiator Valves. A home supports up to four Dormouse Thermostats, each controlling its own zone.

The Room Sensor uses one CR2032 coin cell, which lasts about a year. Replace the cell when needed, following the sensor's battery access and replacement instructions.

For the thermostat, wipe it with a soft, dry cloth. Do not use sprays or cleaning products, and do not paint over it.

## Getting help

If you cannot add the sensor or its readings do not appear, check that it was assigned to the intended zone and that the thermostat is connected to Wi-Fi. The sensor communicates with its zone's thermostat by radio; it does not join the home Wi-Fi network.

Contact Dormouse support in the app under Help > Contact support.
