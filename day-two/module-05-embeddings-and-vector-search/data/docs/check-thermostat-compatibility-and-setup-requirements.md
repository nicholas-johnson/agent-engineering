# Check thermostat compatibility and setup requirements

Check that your heating system, Wi-Fi network, phone and account meet the requirements before you set up a Dormouse Thermostat.

## Check your boiler

The Dormouse Thermostat works with these gas boiler types:

- Combi
- System
- Conventional

The thermostat must be wired to the boiler by a qualified installer. Do not attempt to wire it yourself. If you are unsure which type of boiler you have, check the boiler's documentation or ask a qualified installer.

## Check your Wi-Fi network

The thermostat needs a Wi-Fi network that operates on **2.4 GHz**. It cannot join a 5 GHz network.

Before pairing, check that your router is on and that the 2.4 GHz network is available. If you have changed your Wi-Fi network name or password since the thermostat was paired, pair the thermostat again with the updated network details.

## Check your phone

Install the Dormouse app on a phone running either:

- Android 12 or later
- iOS 17 or later

You will need the app to pair the thermostat with your Wi-Fi and Dormouse account. You can also use the web dashboard for most settings, but pairing is started in the app.

## Set up a Dormouse account

A Dormouse account is required. You can create one in the app. Sign in to the app and web dashboard with your email address and password.

Your password must contain at least 10 characters. You may also choose to turn on two-step verification in Settings > Account > Two-step verification. When enabled, it asks for a 6-digit code as well as your password when you sign in on a new phone or browser.

## Prepare to pair

Pairing connects the thermostat to your Wi-Fi network and Dormouse account. Before you start, make sure:

- The thermostat is wired to the boiler by a qualified installer.
- Your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network is available.
- The Dormouse app is installed and you can sign in to your account.
- Your phone can stay within 5 metres of the thermostat during pairing.

## Pair the thermostat

1. Hold the thermostat dial down for 5 seconds, until the display shows **PAIR**.
2. In the Dormouse app, choose **Add thermostat** and follow the steps.
3. Keep your phone within 5 metres of the thermostat until the app confirms that pairing is finished.

Pairing must finish within 3 minutes of **PAIR** appearing on the display. If it takes longer, the thermostat shows **E-104**. Hold the dial for 5 seconds to start pairing again, then repeat the steps.

## Check whether the setup is supported

Use this checklist before starting:

| Requirement | Supported setup |
|---|---|
| Boiler | Gas combi, system or conventional boiler |
| Installation | Wired to the boiler by a qualified installer |
| Wi-Fi | 2.4 GHz network |
| Phone | Android 12 or later, or iOS 17 or later |
| Account | Dormouse account |
| Pairing distance | Phone within 5 metres of the thermostat |
| Pairing time | Finish within 3 minutes of **PAIR** appearing |

If any requirement is not met, resolve it before pairing. For help, open the Dormouse app and go to Help > Contact support.
