# Accessibility Policy

## Scope

This policy describes accessibility considerations for using Dormouse products and their controls. It applies to the Dormouse Thermostat, Dormouse Radiator Valve, Dormouse Room Sensor, Dormouse app and web dashboard.

The thermostat is designed for indoor use between 0 and 40 degrees. It has an 84 mm round body, a 2.4-inch colour display and a dial with a boost button below it. The display presents room temperature in large numbers and the target temperature underneath.

The app is available for Android 12 or later and iOS 17 or later. The web dashboard is another way to manage most settings. Menu paths in the app and web dashboard are the same.

This policy describes product features and practical ways to use them. It does not claim that every feature is suitable for every user or that the products meet a particular accessibility standard.

## Choosing a control method

You can change most settings in the app, on the web dashboard or on the thermostat itself. Choose the method that is most comfortable and practical for you.

| Control method | What you can do |
|---|---|
| Thermostat dial | Turn it to change the target temperature or move through the menu. Press it to confirm, or to open the menu when the display shows the room temperature. |
| Boost button | Press it to start a one-hour boost. Press it again to cancel the boost. |
| App or web dashboard | Change most settings, including schedules and settings that cannot be edited on the thermostat. |

Turning the dial changes the target temperature. A hand-set temperature lasts until the next schedule period starts. To set a schedule, use the Schedule tab in the app or web dashboard.

If a control method is difficult to use, try another available method. Household members can change the temperature, schedules and away mode. Only the home owner can invite or remove members and add or remove devices.

## Reading the thermostat display

The thermostat uses a round colour display. Room temperature appears in large numbers, with the target temperature underneath. When you turn the dial, the target temperature gets larger while you change it. After 20 seconds without a touch, the display dims.

The display also uses symbols to show the thermostat's status:

| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Flame | The thermostat is asking the boiler for heat. |
| Sunrise | Warm Wake is active. |
| Snowflake | Frost protection is heating the home. |
| House with an arrow | Away mode is on. |
| Upward arrow | A boost is running. |
| Wi-Fi symbol with a cross | The thermostat is not connected to Wi-Fi. |
| Battery | The backup batteries are low. |

The display shows error codes when something is wrong. The app shows the same code and can notify you about it. For example, E-401 means the backup batteries are low; replace the two AA batteries within a month.

## Using the dial and button

Turn the dial to move through the thermostat menu: Schedule, Away, Settings and About. Press the dial to choose an item. When the display shows the room temperature, pressing the dial opens the menu.

The thermostat's Schedule menu shows today's periods, but schedules can only be changed in the app or web dashboard. You can set up to six periods for each day.

The boost button is below the dial. A boost heats the home at the boost temperature for one hour. The boost temperature is 21 degrees unless you change it in Settings > Heating > Boost temperature.

If you find the dial or button difficult to operate, use the app or web dashboard for settings available there. The thermostat can also be operated by another household member with access to the home.

## App, web dashboard and account access

Sign in to the app or web dashboard with your email address and password. Passwords must have at least 10 characters. Two-step verification can be turned on in Settings > Account > Two-step verification. When required on a new phone or browser, it asks for a 6-digit code sent by email or provided by an authenticator app if one is set up.

The app and web dashboard provide alternatives to the thermostat's physical controls for most settings. For example, you can change the target temperature, manage away mode, and edit schedules. A Dormouse account is required to use the app and web dashboard.

A home can have one owner and up to eight members. Members can change the temperature, schedules and away mode. The owner manages invitations and devices.

## Notifications and information

The app can notify each member about thermostat errors, low backup batteries, heating running for two hours while everyone is away, installed firmware updates and monthly energy reports. Each member chooses their notifications in Settings > Notifications.

Energy reports are shown in the app's Energy tab on the 1st of each month. They show heating hours by day compared with the previous month and an estimate of gas used in kWh.

The thermostat's menu includes About. To check the firmware version, press the dial and choose Menu > About. The thermostat updates its own firmware automatically between 02:00 and 04:00 local time. During installation, the display shows UPDATING and heating pauses for about two minutes.

## Room sensors and radiator valves

A Dormouse Room Sensor measures temperature and humidity in another room of its zone. Up to three Room Sensors can join a zone. A Dormouse Radiator Valve lets each radiator in a zone heat to its own temperature; up to ten valves can join a zone.

Sensors and valves communicate with their zone's thermostat over a low-power radio link. They do not use Wi-Fi. Only the thermostat connects to Wi-Fi.

To add a sensor or valve, go to Settings > Home > Add a device. A home can have up to four thermostats, with each thermostat controlling its own zone and schedule.

## Getting help and maintaining access

For help with using Dormouse products, contact support in the app under Help > Contact support. If an error appears, note its code; the app shows the same code as the thermostat and may provide a notification.

Keep the thermostat display clean and readable by wiping it with a soft, dry cloth. Do not use sprays or cleaning products, and do not paint over it.

If you cannot use a particular control or feature, another household member may be able to make the change using the app, web dashboard or thermostat. Use the control method that works best for you and your household.
