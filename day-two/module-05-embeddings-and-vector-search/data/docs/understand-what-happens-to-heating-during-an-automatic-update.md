# Understand what happens to heating during an automatic update

The Dormouse Thermostat installs firmware updates automatically between 02:00 and 04:00 local time. Updates cannot be postponed. While an update installs, heating pauses until the thermostat has finished and restarted.

## What you will see

During installation, the thermostat display shows **UPDATING**. The thermostat then restarts. The whole process takes about two minutes, and heating pauses during this time.

When the thermostat has restarted, it resumes normal operation. Its schedules are retained.

## What to expect from your heating

The thermostat cannot control the heating while the update is installing and the device is restarting. Heating may therefore be paused for about two minutes. The update does not remove your schedules or change your target temperatures.

## If an update fails

If the update fails, the display shows **E-510**. The thermostat keeps its current firmware and tries to install the update again the next night. There is nothing you need to do.

## Getting help

If you see another error code, check its meaning in the thermostat guide. You can also contact Dormouse support in the app under Help > Contact support.
