# Change the target temperature

## Set the temperature on the thermostat

Turn the dial to change the target temperature. As you turn it, the display enlarges the target temperature so you can see the value you are setting.

The target temperature can be set from 5 to 30 degrees, in half-degree steps. When the display shows the temperature you want, press the dial to confirm.

## Set the temperature in the app

You can also change the target temperature in the Dormouse app. Use the temperature control to choose a value from 5 to 30 degrees, in half-degree steps.

Most settings can be changed in the app, on the web dashboard or on the thermostat. The target temperature is available on the thermostat and in the app.

## How a manual change affects your schedule

A temperature change you make by hand lasts until the next schedule period starts. At that time, the thermostat follows the temperature set for the new period.

To set temperatures for different times of day, change your schedule in the Schedule tab of the app or web dashboard. Each day can have up to six periods. On the thermostat, Menu > Schedule shows today's periods, but you cannot change them there.
