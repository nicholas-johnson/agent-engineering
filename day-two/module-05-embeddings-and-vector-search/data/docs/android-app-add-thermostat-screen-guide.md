# Android app: Add thermostat screen guide

## Purpose

The Add thermostat screen connects a Dormouse Thermostat to your Wi-Fi network and Dormouse account. This guide walks through starting pairing, following the setup steps in the app and keeping your phone near the thermostat until pairing finishes.

Pairing requires a Wi-Fi network on 2.4 GHz. The thermostat cannot join a 5 GHz network. You also need the Dormouse app on a phone running Android 12 or later, and a Dormouse account. You can create an account in the app.

The thermostat must be wired to a gas boiler by a qualified installer. Pairing does not replace the installation or connect the thermostat to the boiler.

## Before you start

Check that your phone is connected and ready to use the Dormouse app. Make sure the Wi-Fi network you intend to use is a 2.4 GHz network, and have its network details ready to enter when the app asks.

Keep the phone within 5 metres of the thermostat throughout pairing. The process must finish within 3 minutes of the thermostat displaying PAIR. If it takes longer, the thermostat shows E-104 and you must start again.

If the thermostat is already paired, pairing it again may be needed after changing the Wi-Fi network's name or password. The thermostat's Wi-Fi details are cleared by a factory reset, which also removes its schedules and account link.

## Start pairing on the thermostat

1. Go to the thermostat.
2. Hold the dial down for 5 seconds.
3. Release it when the display shows PAIR.

The thermostat is now ready to pair. Start the app steps promptly so that pairing can finish within the 3-minute limit.

## Follow the app setup steps

1. Open the Dormouse app.
2. Choose **Add thermostat**.
3. Follow the steps shown in the app.
4. Enter or select the requested Wi-Fi details when prompted.
5. Continue through the app until it confirms that pairing has finished.

The app guides you through connecting the thermostat to Wi-Fi and to your Dormouse account. Follow the instructions on the screen; do not leave the process before the app says it has finished.

## Keep your phone nearby

Keep your phone within 5 metres of the thermostat while the app completes setup. Do not move away until the app confirms pairing is complete.

Pairing has a 3-minute time limit, starting when the thermostat first shows PAIR. If the display changes to E-104 before the app finishes, the attempt has timed out. Hold the dial down for 5 seconds to show PAIR again, then restart the Add thermostat steps in the app. Keep the phone within 5 metres for the new attempt.

## Confirm pairing is complete

Wait for the app to say that setup has finished before treating the thermostat as paired. The phone should remain nearby until you see this confirmation.

Once paired, the thermostat is connected to the Wi-Fi network and your Dormouse account. Most settings can then be changed in the app or the web dashboard. Menu paths in the app and web dashboard use the same names, such as Settings > Heating.

## If pairing does not finish

If the thermostat displays E-104, pairing exceeded its 3-minute limit. Hold the dial down for 5 seconds to start again, and keep the phone within 5 metres of the thermostat until the app confirms completion.

If the thermostat displays E-101, it cannot find the Wi-Fi network. Check that the router is on and that the network offers 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. The thermostat cannot join a 5 GHz network. If the network name or password has changed, pair the thermostat again using the current details.

If you need help, contact Dormouse support in the app under Help > Contact support.

## After setup

The thermostat shows the room temperature on its display. Turn the dial to change the target temperature. The app can also be used to change settings and manage schedules.

If you need to pair again, start by holding the dial down for 5 seconds until PAIR appears. Pairing must finish within 3 minutes, and the phone must stay within 5 metres throughout the process.
