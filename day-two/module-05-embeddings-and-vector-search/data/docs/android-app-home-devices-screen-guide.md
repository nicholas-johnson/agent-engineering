# Android app: Home devices screen guide

## Purpose

The Home devices screen lists the Dormouse devices in your home and provides the entry point for adding another one. You can add a Dormouse Thermostat, Dormouse Room Sensor or Dormouse Radiator Valve.

A home can have up to four thermostats. Each thermostat controls its own zone, with its own schedule. A Room Sensor can join a zone to measure the temperature in another room. Up to ten Radiator Valves can join a zone, so each radiator in that zone heats to its own temperature.

The Home devices screen is available in the Dormouse app and web dashboard. In the app, open Settings > Home > Add a device to add a device.

## Before you add a device

Only the home owner can add or remove devices. If you are a household member, ask the owner to make the change.

Before adding a thermostat, make sure it has been wired to the boiler by a qualified installer. The thermostat needs a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network; it cannot join a 5 GHz network. Your phone needs the Dormouse app and an internet connection for pairing.

Room Sensors and Radiator Valves connect to a thermostat in their zone over a low-power 868 MHz radio link. They do not use Wi-Fi. The thermostat is the device that connects to Wi-Fi.

## Open the add-device screen

1. Open the Dormouse app.
2. Go to Settings > Home > Add a device.
3. Choose the type of device you want to add.
4. Follow the instructions shown in the app.

The available device types are Dormouse Thermostat, Dormouse Room Sensor and Dormouse Radiator Valve. Add the device to the home and, where prompted, select the zone it should join.

## Add a Dormouse Thermostat

A thermostat controls the heating in its own zone. A home can have up to four Dormouse Thermostats.

To pair a thermostat:

1. Hold the thermostat dial down for 5 seconds, until the display shows PAIR.
2. In the app, choose Add thermostat and follow the pairing steps.
3. Keep your phone within 5 metres of the thermostat until the app says pairing is finished.

Pairing must finish within 3 minutes of PAIR appearing. If it takes longer, the display shows E-104. Hold the dial down for 5 seconds to start pairing again.

The thermostat needs a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network to pair. If the network name or password has changed, pair the thermostat again with the updated network details.

## Add a Room Sensor

A Dormouse Room Sensor measures temperature and humidity in another room of a zone. Add it to the zone whose thermostat it should work with. Up to three Room Sensors can join a zone.

The sensor uses one CR2032 coin cell, which lasts about a year. It communicates with the zone's thermostat over a low-power 868 MHz radio link, not over Wi-Fi.

Follow the app's instructions to add the sensor and assign it to a zone. To check that it is in the intended zone, review the device listing after setup.

## Add a Radiator Valve

A Dormouse Radiator Valve joins a zone and controls the temperature at its own radiator. Up to ten valves can join a zone.

The valve fits M30 x 1.5 valve bodies. Adapters are included for other common valve types. It runs on two AA batteries, which last about two years. The valve communicates with its zone's thermostat over a low-power 868 MHz radio link; it does not use Wi-Fi.

Follow the app's instructions to add the valve and assign it to a zone. Check the device listing after setup to confirm the valve is assigned to the intended zone.

## Understand zones and device lists

Each thermostat controls one zone and has its own schedule. A Room Sensor can measure conditions in another room of that same zone. Each Radiator Valve in a zone can heat its own radiator to its own temperature.

Use the Home devices screen to review the devices in your home and their zone assignments. If a device is assigned to the wrong zone, use the app's available device controls to make changes. Only the home owner can add or remove devices.

## Troubleshooting

If thermostat pairing times out, the display shows E-104. Start pairing again by holding the dial down for 5 seconds, and keep your phone within 5 metres until the app confirms setup is complete. Pairing must finish within 3 minutes.

If the thermostat cannot find the Wi-Fi network, check that the router is on and that the network offers 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. A thermostat cannot join a 5 GHz network.

Room Sensors and Radiator Valves communicate with their zone's thermostat by radio. Check that each has been assigned to the intended zone and follow the app's setup instructions again if needed.
