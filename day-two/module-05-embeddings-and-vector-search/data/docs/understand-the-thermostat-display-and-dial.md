# Understand the thermostat display and dial

## The display at a glance

The Dormouse Thermostat has a round display. It shows the room temperature in large numbers, with the target temperature underneath.

When you turn the dial to change the target temperature, the target temperature gets larger on the display. This makes it easier to see the value while you adjust it.

## How the display dims

The display dims after 20 seconds without a touch. It still shows the room temperature and target temperature.

## Change the target temperature with the dial

1. Turn the dial to change the target temperature.
2. When the target you want is shown, press the dial to confirm.

You can set the target temperature from 5 to 30 degrees, in half-degree steps. A temperature change you make by hand lasts until the next schedule period starts.

## Open and use the menu

When the display shows the room temperature, press the dial to open the menu. The menu has four items:

- Schedule
- Away
- Settings
- About

Turn the dial to move between the items, then press it to choose one. For example, choose About to see which firmware version the thermostat has.
