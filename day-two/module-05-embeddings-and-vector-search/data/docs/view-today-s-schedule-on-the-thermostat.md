# View today's schedule on the thermostat

## What you can view

The thermostat can show the periods in today's schedule. Each period has a start time and a target temperature. It lasts until the next period starts.

You can view today's periods on the thermostat, but you cannot change the schedule there. To set or edit schedule periods, use the Dormouse app or web dashboard.

## Before you start

The display should show the room temperature. If it has dimmed, touch the display or turn the dial to wake it.

The thermostat menu contains Schedule, Away, Settings and About. You use the dial to move through the menu and select an item.

## Open today's schedule

1. Press the dial to open the menu.
2. Turn the dial until Schedule is selected.
3. Press the dial.

The thermostat shows the periods for today.

## Read the periods

Each period shows a start time and target temperature. The period applies from its start time until the next period begins. The last period continues until the next day's first period starts.

A schedule can have up to six periods per day. The thermostat shows today's periods only; it does not let you choose another day to view.

## Change a schedule

Schedules can only be changed in the app or web dashboard:

1. Open the Schedule tab.
2. Choose a day.
3. Set or edit its periods.

To use the same periods on other days, choose Copy day. Changes made to schedules apply through the app or web dashboard, not through the thermostat's Schedule menu.

## If the displayed temperature differs from the schedule

A temperature change made by hand lasts until the next schedule period starts. Away mode and a running boost can also affect heating. The thermostat's Schedule menu shows the scheduled periods for today; it is not a record of every temperature change made during the day.

## Related controls

Turn the dial to adjust the target temperature shown on the main display. A manual adjustment lasts until the next schedule period starts.

To return to the main display, move out of the menu using the dial.
