# Thermostat Installation Handover and Pairing

## Scope

This guide covers installing and wiring a Dormouse Thermostat, handing it over to the household and pairing it to Wi-Fi and a Dormouse account. It is for qualified installers and confident customers.

The thermostat is designed for a gas boiler: combi, system or conventional. It must be wired to the boiler by a qualified installer. Do not attempt boiler wiring unless you are qualified to do so.

The thermostat connects to a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network. It cannot join a 5 GHz network. Pairing connects the thermostat to both the household's Wi-Fi and its Dormouse account.

## Before installation

Check that the installation has the required equipment and access:

- A compatible gas boiler: combi, system or conventional.
- A suitable place indoors for the thermostat. It is intended for use between 0 and 40 degrees.
- Access to a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network and its credentials.
- A phone with the Dormouse app installed: Android 12 or later, or iOS 17 or later.
- A Dormouse account. The account can be created in the app.

The thermostat is 84 mm across and 24 mm deep. Its round colour display measures 2.4 inches. It is powered by mains through its wall plate. Two AA backup batteries keep the clock and schedules during a power cut for up to 48 hours, but the thermostat cannot switch the heating on while mains power is off.

## Install and wire the thermostat

The thermostat must be wired to the boiler by a qualified installer. Follow the boiler's and installation site's requirements for safe isolation and wiring. This guide does not provide boiler wiring diagrams or terminal instructions.

1. Install and wire the thermostat's wall plate according to the applicable installation requirements.
2. Confirm that the wall plate is supplied with mains power and the boiler wiring has been completed by a qualified installer.
3. Fit the thermostat to the wall plate.
4. Confirm that the display powers on and shows the room temperature.

Do not proceed to pairing if the thermostat does not power on. Check the installation and mains supply before continuing.

## Check the thermostat and controls

The display shows the room temperature in large numbers and the target temperature underneath. After 20 seconds without a touch, the display dims.

Turn the dial to change the target temperature. Press the dial to confirm a change, or to open the menu when the display shows the room temperature. The menu contains Schedule, Away, Settings and About. Turn the dial to move between menu items and press to choose.

To identify the installed firmware, press the dial to open the menu and choose Menu > About. This guide covers firmware 4.1.

## Prepare the household for pairing

Before starting, confirm that the 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network is operating and that the phone can connect to it. If the router offers both 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz networks, make sure the phone and thermostat are set up to use the 2.4 GHz network.

The person pairing the thermostat needs access to the Dormouse account in the app. Keep the phone within 5 metres of the thermostat throughout pairing. The process must finish within 3 minutes of the thermostat displaying PAIR.

If the thermostat was previously paired to a different network, or the network name or password has changed, pair it again using the current Wi-Fi details.

## Pair the thermostat

Complete pairing in the Dormouse app:

1. Hold the thermostat dial down for 5 seconds, until the display shows PAIR.
2. In the app, choose Add thermostat and follow the steps.
3. Keep the phone within 5 metres of the thermostat until the app confirms that pairing has finished.

Pairing connects the thermostat to Wi-Fi and the Dormouse account. Do not leave the pairing process unfinished: if it takes longer than 3 minutes, the display shows E-104. Start again by holding the dial down for 5 seconds.

## Handover checks

After pairing, check that the app shows the thermostat and that its display is operating normally. Confirm that the household can open the thermostat in the app and change the target temperature.

Explain the controls and key display indicators:

- A flame means the thermostat is asking the boiler for heat.
- A house with an arrow means Away mode is on.
- An upward arrow means a boost is running.
- A Wi-Fi symbol with a cross means the thermostat is not connected to Wi-Fi.
- A battery symbol means the backup batteries are low.

The boost button below the dial starts a one-hour boost at the configured boost temperature. The default is 21 degrees. Away mode can be turned on in the app or from Menu > Away on the thermostat.

## Troubleshooting pairing and power

| Display or symptom | Meaning and action |
|---|---|
| E-101 | The Wi-Fi network was not found. Check that the router is on and provides a 2.4 GHz network. If the network name or password has changed, pair again. |
| E-104 | Pairing timed out. Hold the dial for 5 seconds to start again, keep the phone within 5 metres and finish within 3 minutes. |
| E-110 | The thermostat cannot reach the Dormouse cloud. Check that the router is connected to the internet. The thermostat continues running schedules; app changes wait until it reconnects. |
| E-402 | Mains power is interrupted. The thermostat keeps its clock and schedules on backup batteries for up to 48 hours, but cannot switch the heating on. |
| No display | Do not continue pairing. Check the installation and mains supply. |

For other thermostat error codes, consult the user guide. Support can be contacted in the app under Help > Contact support.

## Batteries and handover information

Two AA batteries behind the front cover provide backup for the clock and schedules during a power cut. They do not power the heating. Replace the batteries once a year, or when E-401 appears. E-401 indicates that the batteries should be replaced within a month. To access them, pull the bottom of the front cover gently towards you.

Leave the household with the Wi-Fi details they need and show them how to use the app and thermostat dial. The thermostat's schedules can be viewed on the thermostat at Menu > Schedule, but schedules can only be changed in the app or web dashboard.
