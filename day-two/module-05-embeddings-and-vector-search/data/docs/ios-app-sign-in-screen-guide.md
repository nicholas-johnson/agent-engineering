# iOS app: Sign-in screen guide

## About this guide

This guide explains how to sign in to the Dormouse app on iOS and how to request a password-reset link from its sign-in screen.

Use the Dormouse app on a phone running iOS 17 or later. You can also sign in to the web dashboard with the same email address and password. Most account settings are available in the app and the web dashboard.

## Before you sign in

You need a Dormouse account to use the app. Sign in with the email address and password for that account.

A password must have at least 10 characters. If you cannot remember your password, request a reset link from the sign-in screen rather than repeatedly trying passwords.

## Enter your account details

1. Open the Dormouse app.
2. On the sign-in screen, enter the email address for your Dormouse account.
3. Enter your password.
4. Continue to sign in.

If you sign in on a new phone, you may also be asked for a six-digit verification code. See Two-step verification below.

## Two-step verification

Two-step verification adds a six-digit code when you sign in on a new phone or browser. The code is sent by email, or provided by an authenticator app if you have set one up.

To turn two-step verification on or off, go to Settings > Account > Two-step verification. You can change this setting in the app or on the web dashboard.

## Request a password-reset link

If you have forgotten your password:

1. On the sign-in screen, choose Forgot password?
2. Enter the email address for your Dormouse account.
3. Request the reset link.

We send the link to your email address. Open it to reset your password, then return to the app and sign in with your new password.

## Reset-link expiry

A password-reset link works for 30 minutes and can only be used once. If it has expired, request a new link from the sign-in screen.

If you have already used the link, it cannot be used again. Request another one if you need to reset your password again.

## If you cannot sign in

Check that the email address is the one associated with your Dormouse account and that your password has at least 10 characters. If you have forgotten the password, choose Forgot password? and request a reset link.

If you are signing in on a new phone and two-step verification is on, use the six-digit code sent by email or generated by your authenticator app.

## Account help

For help with the Dormouse app or your account, go to Help > Contact support in the app.
