# Thermostat Warranty Policy

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy describes the two-year warranty for the Dormouse Thermostat. It applies to the thermostat hardware and explains the warranty period, how to request support, and what information to provide.

This policy does not change how the thermostat is installed or operated. The thermostat must be wired to the boiler by a qualified installer. For setup, use and troubleshooting instructions, see the Dormouse Thermostat User Guide for firmware 4.1.

## 2. Warranty period

The Dormouse Thermostat has a two-year warranty. The warranty period begins on the date of purchase.

Keep your proof of purchase so the purchase date can be confirmed if you need to make a warranty request. If you cannot provide proof of purchase, contact Dormouse support for guidance.

## 3. What the warranty covers

The warranty covers faults in the Dormouse Thermostat that arise during normal use within the two-year warranty period.

If you think the thermostat has a fault, contact Dormouse support before attempting a repair or replacing the unit. Support can help identify whether the issue is with the thermostat or another part of the heating system and advise on the next steps.

The thermostat can show error codes when something is wrong. The app shows the same code and can notify you about it. Check the code in the User Guide before contacting support, and include it in your request where possible.

## 4. What to do if there is a problem

Contact Dormouse support in the app under Help > Contact support. Include a clear description of the problem and the steps you have already taken. If the display shows an error code, provide the code.

For example, if the thermostat shows E-217, the User Guide says the temperature sensor is disconnected and the thermostat turns the heating off to be safe. Contact support to arrange a replacement. For other error codes, follow the guidance in the User Guide.

Do not attempt to open, repair or modify the thermostat. If the issue relates to the boiler, follow the boiler's own instructions or contact a registered gas engineer as appropriate.

## 5. Information to include in a request

To help support assess the issue, provide:

- The thermostat's error code, if one is displayed.
- A description of what happened and when it began.
- The steps you have taken so far.
- Proof of purchase showing the purchase date.
- The thermostat's serial number, if requested.

The serial number prefix identifies the hardware revision: DMA indicates revision A, DMB indicates revision B, and DMC indicates revision C. You can find the serial number on the product or its packaging.

## 6. Assessment and resolution

Dormouse support will review the information you provide and advise on the appropriate next step. This may include troubleshooting, arranging a replacement, or another resolution under the warranty.

Follow the instructions provided by support. Do not dispose of or return the thermostat unless support has told you to do so. If a replacement is arranged, support will explain what to do with the affected thermostat.

A warranty request does not replace the need to keep the home safe. For example, if the thermostat reports a boiler fault, consult the boiler's manual or contact a registered gas engineer.

## 7. Care and handling

Use the thermostat as described in the User Guide. Wipe it with a soft, dry cloth. Do not use sprays or cleaning products, and do not paint over it.

The thermostat is intended for indoor use between 0 and 40 degrees. It is powered by mains electricity through its wall plate and must be wired to the boiler by a qualified installer. Do not remove the thermostat from its wall plate or attempt electrical work unless instructed by a qualified professional.

## 8. Returns and other support

The warranty is separate from Dormouse's 30-day returns period. For a warranty issue, contact support in the app under Help > Contact support. Do not treat a return as a substitute for reporting a fault under this policy.

For instructions on pairing, schedules, heating, error codes, firmware updates and factory reset, refer to the Dormouse Thermostat User Guide. A factory reset removes Wi-Fi details, schedules and the link to your account; it does not replace a warranty assessment.
