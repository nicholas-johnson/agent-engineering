# Radiator Valve Zone Assignment

## Purpose

This guide explains how to add a Dormouse Radiator Valve to the correct thermostat zone. Each zone is controlled by its own Dormouse Thermostat. A valve added to a zone heats its radiator to its own temperature as part of that zone.

You can add a valve using the Dormouse app or the web dashboard. Most settings are available in both. The thermostat must be paired with your Dormouse account before you add devices.

## Before you start

Check that the home has a Dormouse Thermostat controlling the zone you want to use. A home can have up to four thermostats, each with its own zone and schedule.

The Dormouse Radiator Valve fits M30 x 1.5 valve bodies. Adapters for other common valve types are supplied in the box. Check that the valve body and the adapter, if needed, are suitable before fitting the radiator valve.

The radiator valve runs on two AA batteries, which typically last about two years. It communicates with the thermostat for its zone over a low-power 868 MHz radio link. The radiator valve does not connect to Wi-Fi; only the thermostat uses Wi-Fi.

## Plan the zone assignment

Decide which thermostat zone the radiator belongs to before adding the valve. For example, a radiator controlled by the downstairs thermostat should be assigned to that thermostat's zone, rather than to the upstairs zone.

A zone can include up to ten Dormouse Radiator Valves. Each valve controls its own radiator temperature. A Dormouse Room Sensor can also join a zone to measure temperature and humidity in another room; a zone can have up to three Room Sensors.

If the home has more than one thermostat, identify the intended zone clearly in the app or web dashboard before starting. Adding a valve to the wrong zone can make it respond to the wrong thermostat's zone settings.

## Fit and power the valve

Fit the Dormouse Radiator Valve to the radiator valve body, using an adapter from the box if required. Follow the fitting instructions supplied with the valve and make sure the connection is secure.

Install the two AA batteries in the valve as indicated in its battery compartment. The valve needs its batteries to communicate with the thermostat and operate the radiator. The thermostat itself is mains-powered through its wall plate.

The thermostat must be within radio communication range of the valve. The valve uses a low-power 868 MHz radio link to communicate with its zone's thermostat. It does not join the home's Wi-Fi network.

## Add the valve to a zone

Use the app or web dashboard while signed in to the Dormouse account for the home:

1. Open Settings > Home > Add a device.
2. Choose the option to add a Dormouse Radiator Valve.
3. Follow the on-screen instructions to put the valve into the required setup state and continue.
4. Select the thermostat zone that controls the radiator.
5. Complete the instructions in the app or dashboard and wait for confirmation that the valve has been added.

Check the selected zone before confirming. If the home has several thermostats, choose the one that controls the room or area where this radiator is installed. Do not choose a thermostat just because it is physically closer unless that is also the correct heating zone.

## Confirm the assignment

After setup, check that the valve appears under the intended zone in the app or web dashboard. Confirm that the zone shows the expected thermostat and that the radiator valve is listed as one of its devices.

The valve should communicate with that zone's thermostat over the 868 MHz radio link. The thermostat controls its own zone, while each radiator valve in the zone heats its radiator to its own temperature.

If the valve is listed under a different zone, use the device and home settings in the app or dashboard to correct the assignment. Follow the displayed instructions and verify the valve is shown under the intended thermostat afterwards.

## If setup does not complete

Check that the valve has two correctly installed AA batteries and that it is fitted to the radiator valve body. Confirm that you selected the correct home and the intended thermostat zone in Settings > Home > Add a device.

Remember that the radiator valve communicates with the thermostat by radio, not Wi-Fi. Check that the thermostat for the intended zone is set up and operating. Only the thermostat connects to Wi-Fi.

If you need help with the setup or device assignment, contact Dormouse support in the app under Help > Contact support.
