# Heating History Data Retention Policy

## Purpose and scope

This policy explains how long Dormouse keeps heating history. It applies to heating history associated with a Dormouse home and its thermostats.

Heating history is information about heating activity, including how many hours the heating was on each day. The Dormouse app presents daily heating hours in the monthly energy report, alongside a comparison with the previous month and an estimate of gas used in kWh.

This policy covers heating history only. It does not set retention periods for other account, device or household information.

## Retention period

Dormouse keeps heating history for two years. The retention period applies to the history used to provide information about heating activity and energy reports.

Heating history is not kept indefinitely. Information older than the two-year period is outside the stated retention period.

## Use in energy reports

An energy report arrives on the 1st of each month in the app's Energy tab. It shows how many hours the heating was on each day, compares those hours with the month before and estimates gas used in kWh.

The estimate depends on the size of the boiler. To enter the boiler size, go to Settings > Home > Boiler size.

Energy reports provide a view of heating activity; the two-year retention period for heating history does not change the report schedule described in the user guide.

## Access and account

The Dormouse app and web dashboard are used to manage a home and its settings. Household members can change the temperature, schedules and away mode. Only the home owner can add or remove devices, invite members or remove them.

This policy does not describe separate retention periods for account details, household membership information or other data. The two-year period stated here is specifically for heating history.

## Location information

Geofencing uses the location of each household member's phone to turn away mode on and off. Phone locations are never stored. Dormouse stores only whether each member is home or away.

This location information is distinct from heating history and is not covered by the two-year heating-history retention period.

## Related information and support

Monthly energy reports are available in the app's Energy tab. Each member can choose their own notifications in Settings > Notifications.

For help with the product or this policy, contact Dormouse support in the app under Help > Contact support.
