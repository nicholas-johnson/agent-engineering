# Understand how boiler size affects energy estimates

## How the estimate works

The monthly energy report includes an estimate of the gas used, shown in kWh. This estimate depends on the size of your boiler.

The thermostat records how many hours the heating was on each day. The energy report uses that information and the boiler size entered for your home to estimate gas use. It also compares the heating hours with the previous month.

The gas-use figure is an estimate, not a direct measurement of the gas your boiler has used. Its accuracy depends in part on whether the boiler size recorded for your home is correct.

## Enter or update your boiler size

You can enter the boiler size in the app or web dashboard:

1. Open Settings > Home > Boiler size.
2. Enter your boiler size and save the change.

If the boiler size is missing or incorrect, update it so future energy estimates use the right information. If you do not know the size, check the information supplied with your boiler or ask a registered gas engineer.

## View your energy report

Energy reports arrive on the 1st of each month in the Energy tab of the app. Each report shows:

- the number of hours the heating was on each day
- a comparison with the previous month
- an estimate of gas used in kWh

Entering the boiler size does not change the reported heating hours. It provides information used for the gas-use estimate.

## What to keep in mind

The report's gas-use figure is an estimate based on the heating data and the boiler size entered for your home. It should not be treated as a meter reading or an exact record of gas consumed. If you change the boiler size, the estimate may differ because the information used to calculate it has changed.
