# Understand thermostat mains power

## How the thermostat gets power

The Dormouse Thermostat is powered from the mains through its wall plate. Its backup batteries do not provide power for normal heating operation.

The thermostat must be wired to the boiler by a qualified installer. If you are setting up a new thermostat or changing its wiring, arrange this through a qualified installer.

## What the backup batteries do

Two AA batteries behind the front cover provide backup during a power cut. They keep the clock and your schedules for up to 48 hours, but they cannot power the heating.

If the mains supply is interrupted, the thermostat cannot switch the heating on. The display shows E-402 while it is running on the backup batteries.

## If mains power is interrupted

When mains power is unavailable:

- The thermostat keeps its clock and schedules for up to 48 hours.
- The thermostat cannot switch the heating on.
- The app may show E-402 for a mains power interruption.

Restore the mains supply to resume normal operation. If the boiler is not responding after power is restored, check the boiler and its display.

## Replacing the backup batteries

The thermostat uses two AA backup batteries. Replace them once a year, or when the display shows E-401. After E-401 appears, replace the batteries within a month.

To reach the batteries, gently pull the bottom of the front cover towards you. Replace both batteries, then refit the cover.

## Checking the error code

The app shows the same error code as the thermostat and can notify you about it. E-402 means mains power has been interrupted and the thermostat is running on its backup batteries. E-401 means the backup batteries are low.

For E-402, restore mains power. For E-401, replace the two AA batteries behind the front cover within a month.
