# Replace the thermostat backup batteries

The Dormouse Thermostat uses two AA backup batteries to keep the clock and schedules during a power cut. They provide backup for up to 48 hours. The thermostat cannot switch the heating on while mains power is off.

Replace the batteries once a year, or when the display shows E-401. E-401 means the backup batteries are low. Replace them within a month of the code appearing.

## Before you start

You will need two AA batteries. Replace both batteries together.

If the thermostat shows E-402, mains power is interrupted and the thermostat is running on its backup batteries. It can keep the clock and schedules for up to 48 hours, but it cannot switch the heating on until mains power is restored.

## Reach the batteries

The batteries are behind the thermostat's front cover.

1. Gently pull the bottom of the front cover towards you.
2. Remove the two AA batteries.

## Replace the batteries

1. Fit two new AA batteries in the battery compartment, following the polarity markings.
2. Refit the front cover.

## After replacement

The E-401 code indicates that the batteries are low and should be replaced within a month. If you see E-401, replace the batteries as soon as you can rather than waiting until the deadline.

## Battery care

Replace the backup batteries once a year, even if E-401 has not appeared. The batteries help preserve the clock and schedules during a power cut, but they do not power the heating.
