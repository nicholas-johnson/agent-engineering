# Understand automatic firmware updates

## How updates work

The Dormouse Thermostat installs firmware updates automatically. You do not need to start an update or choose when it installs.

Updates are installed between 02:00 and 04:00 local time. They cannot be postponed. The thermostat restarts as part of the installation, and heating pauses until the update has finished.

## What UPDATING means

While an update is installing, the thermostat display shows **UPDATING**. This means the installation is in progress and the thermostat is restarting.

The process takes about two minutes. During this time, the thermostat cannot control the heating. Heating resumes when the update has finished and the thermostat has restarted.

## If an update fails

If an update fails, the display shows **E-510**. The thermostat keeps its current firmware and tries the update again the next night. There is nothing you need to do.

## Check your firmware version

To see which firmware is installed, press the dial to open the menu and choose **Menu > About**. The thermostat guide covers firmware 4.1.
