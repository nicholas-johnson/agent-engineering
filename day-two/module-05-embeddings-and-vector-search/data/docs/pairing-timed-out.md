# Pairing Timed Out

The display shows E-104 when pairing has not finished within three minutes of the thermostat showing PAIR. Pairing connects the thermostat to your Wi-Fi and Dormouse account. Work through these checks in order.

## Check the three-minute pairing window

Start the pairing steps in the app as soon as the thermostat displays PAIR. The app must finish pairing within three minutes of that display appearing. If the time runs out, the thermostat shows E-104 and pairing needs to be started again.

## Restart pairing

1. Hold the thermostat dial down for 5 seconds, until the display shows PAIR.
2. In the app, choose Add thermostat and follow the steps.
3. Continue until the app says pairing has finished.

If E-104 appears again, restart pairing and make sure you complete the app steps within three minutes.

## Keep your phone close

Keep your phone within 5 metres of the thermostat until the app says pairing has finished. If the phone is farther away, bring it closer before starting again.

## If pairing still times out

Start the pairing steps again from the beginning: hold the dial for 5 seconds until PAIR appears, then choose Add thermostat in the app. Keep your phone within 5 metres and finish within three minutes.

For help, contact support in the app under Help > Contact support.
