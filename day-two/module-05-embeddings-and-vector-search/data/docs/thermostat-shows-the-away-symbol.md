# Thermostat Shows the Away Symbol

The house-with-an-arrow symbol means away mode is on. Away mode keeps your home at the away temperature until it is turned off. The default away temperature is 16 degrees, but it may have been changed.

Follow these steps to check why away mode is on and turn it off.

## 1. Confirm that away mode is active

Check the thermostat display for the house-with-an-arrow symbol. You can also check the Away status in the Dormouse app.

If the symbol is not showing and the app does not show away mode as active, away mode is not the cause of the current temperature setting.

## 2. Check whether away mode was turned on manually

Away mode can be turned on from the app or from the thermostat.

- In the app, tap Away to turn away mode off.
- On the thermostat, press the dial to open the menu, choose Away, and follow the on-screen control to turn it off.

When away mode is off, the away symbol should disappear. The thermostat then follows its schedule, unless another setting, such as a manual temperature change or boost, is affecting the heating.

## 3. Check whether geofencing turned it on

Geofencing can turn away mode on when every household member who shares their location is more than 1 km from home. It turns away mode off when the first of them comes back within 1 km.

In the app, check the Away status and whether geofencing is enabled. Each household member chooses whether to share their location in the app. If location sharing and geofencing are enabled, away mode may have been turned on automatically.

## 4. Turn off geofencing if needed

To stop geofencing from controlling away mode for the whole home, go to **Settings > Away > Geofencing** and turn it off.

Turning off geofencing does not itself turn away mode off. If away mode is still active, turn it off in the app by tapping Away, or on the thermostat by choosing Menu > Away.

## 5. Check the away temperature

If away mode remains on, the home is held at the away temperature. It is 16 degrees by default, but you can change it at **Settings > Away > Away temperature**.

Changing the away temperature does not turn away mode off. Use the app or the thermostat's Away menu to disable it.

## 6. If the symbol remains

After turning away mode off, check the display again. If the symbol remains, check the Away status in the app and make sure geofencing is not turning away mode back on. Review location sharing choices for household members who share their location.

If the problem continues, contact Dormouse support in the app under **Help > Contact support**.
