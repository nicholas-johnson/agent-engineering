# Change the boost temperature

A boost heats your home at the boost temperature for one hour. The boost temperature is 21 degrees by default. You can change it in the Dormouse app, on the web dashboard or on the thermostat.

## Change it in the app or web dashboard

1. Open Settings > Heating > Boost temperature.
2. Choose the temperature you want.
3. Confirm the change if prompted.

The target temperature can be set from 5 to 30 degrees in half-degree steps.

## Change it on the thermostat

1. Press the dial to open the menu.
2. Turn the dial to Settings, then press to choose it.
3. Open Heating > Boost temperature.
4. Turn the dial to select the temperature, then press to confirm.

The thermostat's menu has four items: Schedule, Away, Settings and About. Turn the dial to move between menu items and press to choose one.

## Use the new temperature

Press the boost button below the dial to start a boost. The thermostat heats at the selected boost temperature for one hour, and an upward arrow appears on the display while the boost is running.

Press the boost button again to cancel the boost.
