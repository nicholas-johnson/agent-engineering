# Read your monthly energy report

## Find your report

A monthly energy report is available in the Energy tab of the Dormouse app. A new report arrives on the 1st of each month.

## Understand the heating-hour comparison

The report shows how many hours your heating was on each day. It compares those daily hours with the month before, so you can see how heating time changed from one month to the next.

## Understand the gas-use estimate

The report also estimates how much gas your heating used, in kWh. This is an estimate, not a direct measurement. It depends on the size of your boiler.

## Enter your boiler size

To make the estimate depend on the correct boiler size, go to Settings > Home > Boiler size and enter the size of your boiler.
