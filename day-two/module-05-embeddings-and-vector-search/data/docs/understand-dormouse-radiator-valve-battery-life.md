# Understand Dormouse Radiator Valve battery life

## Battery life

Each Dormouse Radiator Valve runs on two AA batteries. They typically last about two years.

Actual battery life can vary, so check the valve and its batteries if it stops working as expected. Replace both batteries when needed.

## Replacing the batteries

Use two AA batteries in the valve. Follow the instructions supplied with the valve to access its battery compartment and fit the batteries.

## How the valve connects

A Dormouse Radiator Valve communicates with the thermostat for its zone over a low-power 868 MHz radio link. The valve does not use Wi-Fi; only the thermostat connects to Wi-Fi.
