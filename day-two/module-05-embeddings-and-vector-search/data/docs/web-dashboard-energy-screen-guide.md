# Web dashboard: Energy screen guide

## About this guide

This guide describes the monthly energy report and the boiler-size setting used to estimate gas use. The same report is available in the Dormouse app for Android and iOS, and in the web dashboard. Menu paths are the same in the app and the web dashboard.

The guide applies to the Dormouse Thermostat with firmware 4.1. To check the firmware version, press the thermostat dial to open the menu, then choose Menu > About.

## Find your energy report

Open the Energy tab in the app or web dashboard to view your monthly energy report. A new report arrives on the 1st of each month.

The report compares the heating hours for each day with the corresponding information from the month before. It also gives an estimate of the gas used, in kWh.

The report is based on information from your Dormouse home. If you have more than one thermostat, each thermostat controls its own zone and schedule. The report presents the heating information available for your home.

## Understand heating hours

The report shows how many hours the heating was on each day. Daily figures let you see how heating time changes across the month and compare it with the previous month.

Heating hours describe the time the heating was on. They are not a measure of the temperature in your rooms or of the amount of gas used. The gas-use estimate is shown separately.

## Understand the gas-use estimate

The report estimates gas use in kWh. Its accuracy depends on the size of your boiler, so enter the boiler size for your home to help calculate the estimate.

The figure is an estimate, not a direct measurement of gas used. It may not match a reading from a gas meter. If you do not know your boiler size, check the boiler's information before entering a value.

## Enter boiler size

Set the boiler size in the app or web dashboard:

1. Open Settings.
2. Go to Settings > Home > Boiler size.
3. Enter the size of your boiler.

Use the boiler's specified size. The setting is used for the estimated gas use shown in energy reports.

## Review month-to-month information

Use the report's comparison with the month before to review changes in daily heating hours. The report provides a view of heating activity over time; it does not explain why heating hours changed.

Schedules, manual temperature changes and away mode affect how the thermostat controls heating. A manual temperature change lasts until the next schedule period starts. Away mode holds the away temperature until it is turned off.

## Energy history and other information

Energy reports are available in the Energy tab, and heating history is kept for two years. The report shows daily heating hours and estimated gas use; it does not replace the thermostat's schedule or change its heating settings.

The thermostat continues to run its schedules if it cannot reach the Dormouse cloud. Changes made in the app or web dashboard wait until the thermostat reconnects. If the thermostat displays E-110, check that your router is connected to the internet.

## Notifications and help

Each household member chooses their own notifications in Settings > Notifications. The app can notify you when your monthly energy report is ready.

For help with a report or the boiler-size setting, contact Dormouse support in the app under Help > Contact support.
