# E-101: Wi-Fi network not found

## What E-101 means

E-101 means the thermostat cannot find the Wi-Fi network it needs to connect. The thermostat uses Wi-Fi to connect to your Dormouse account and the Dormouse cloud.

The thermostat needs a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network. It cannot join a 5 GHz network. E-101 can appear during pairing or if the thermostat can no longer find the network it was set up to use.

## What to check first

Check that your router is switched on and working. Then check that it offers a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network.

If your router offers both 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz networks, make sure the 2.4 GHz network is available. The thermostat cannot connect to the 5 GHz network.

## If you have changed your Wi-Fi details

If you have changed the Wi-Fi network name or password since pairing the thermostat, it may no longer be able to find or join the network. Pair the thermostat again to connect it to the current network.

Pairing connects the thermostat to Wi-Fi and your Dormouse account. Before you start, make sure you have the Dormouse app and that your phone is connected to the 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network you want the thermostat to use.

## Pair the thermostat again

1. Hold the thermostat dial down for 5 seconds, until the display shows PAIR.
2. In the app, choose Add thermostat and follow the steps.
3. Keep your phone within 5 metres of the thermostat until the app says pairing has finished.

Pairing must finish within 3 minutes of the display showing PAIR. If it takes longer, the display shows E-104 and you need to start again. Hold the dial down for 5 seconds to begin a new pairing attempt.

## If pairing does not finish

Check that the router is on and the 2.4 GHz network is available. Keep your phone within 5 metres of the thermostat throughout pairing, and complete the app steps within 3 minutes.

If the thermostat shows E-101 again, check whether the network name or password has changed. Pair the thermostat again after any change to those details.

## Other connection errors

E-101 means the Wi-Fi network was not found. If the thermostat instead shows E-110, it cannot reach the Dormouse cloud. Check that your router is connected to the internet. The thermostat keeps running its schedules while it cannot reach the cloud, but changes made in the app wait until it reconnects.

The app shows the same error code as the thermostat and can notify you about it. You can contact Dormouse support in the app under Help > Contact support.
