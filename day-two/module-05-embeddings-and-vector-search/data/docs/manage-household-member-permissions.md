# Manage household member permissions

## How household access works

A Dormouse home has one owner and can have up to eight members. The owner and members can use the home's thermostat features, but some actions are reserved for the owner.

Members can change the temperature, schedules and away mode. Only the owner can add or remove devices, invite household members or remove them.

## What members can change

Members can:

- Change the target temperature.
- Change the home's schedules.
- Turn away mode on or off.

These permissions apply to members of the home. The guide does not specify a separate permission for each device or zone.

## Actions reserved for the owner

Only the owner can:

- Add or remove devices.
- Invite someone to join the household.
- Remove a household member.

To add a thermostat, Dormouse Room Sensor or Dormouse Radiator Valve, go to Settings > Home > Add a device. To invite someone, go to Settings > Household > Invite and enter their email address. The invitation link works for 7 days; after that, the owner needs to send a new invitation.

## Geofencing and location sharing

Geofencing uses the locations of household members' phones to turn away mode on or off. Each member chooses whether to share their location in the app. Away mode turns on when every member who shares their location is more than 1 km from home. It turns off when the first of them comes back within 1 km.

To stop geofencing for the whole home, go to Settings > Away > Geofencing. Phone locations are not stored; the system stores only whether each member is home or away.

## Notifications and account settings

Each member chooses their own notifications in Settings > Notifications. These can include alerts about thermostat errors, low backup batteries, heating left on while everyone is away, firmware updates and monthly energy reports.

Account settings are separate from household permissions. For example, each person signs in with their own email address and password. Two-step verification can be turned on in Settings > Account > Two-step verification.

## Choosing the right account

Household permissions determine who can make changes for the home. They do not make a member the owner: only the owner can manage devices and membership.

If you need a device added or removed, or need someone invited or removed, ask the owner to make the change. For changes to temperature, schedules or away mode, a household member can make them directly.
