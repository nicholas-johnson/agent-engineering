# Room Sensor Radio and Battery Policy

## Scope

This policy covers the Dormouse Room Sensor's low-power 868 MHz radio link to its zone thermostat and the sensor's CR2032 coin-cell battery. It applies to Room Sensors used with a Dormouse Thermostat running firmware 4.1.

The Room Sensor measures temperature and humidity in another room in the thermostat's zone. The thermostat uses Wi-Fi; the Room Sensor does not. Its radio link is to the thermostat in its zone.

## Radio link

The Room Sensor communicates with its zone thermostat over a low-power 868 MHz radio link. This link carries the sensor's readings to the thermostat. It does not connect the sensor directly to Wi-Fi.

A Room Sensor can join a zone through Settings > Home > Add a device. A zone can have up to three Dormouse Room Sensors. Each sensor measures conditions in another room of that zone.

The radio link is intended for communication between the Room Sensor and its zone thermostat. Keep the sensor associated with the thermostat for the zone it is intended to measure.

## Battery

The Dormouse Room Sensor is powered by one CR2032 coin-cell battery. The battery lasts about a year. Battery life can vary with use and conditions.

Replace the battery when it is depleted. Use a CR2032 coin cell as the replacement. Dispose of the removed battery in accordance with local battery-recycling requirements; do not put batteries in household waste where local rules prohibit this.

## Battery handling and safety

Coin-cell batteries must be handled with care. Keep new and used batteries out of the reach of children. Do not swallow a battery. If a battery may have been swallowed or placed inside the body, seek urgent medical attention.

Do not recharge, dismantle, crush or heat a CR2032 battery, and do not expose it to fire. Keep batteries away from metal objects that could bridge their terminals. Replace a battery promptly if it is damaged or leaking, and avoid contact with any leaked material.

Fit the replacement battery with the correct polarity. Do not force the battery into its holder. Keep the Room Sensor's battery compartment closed during normal use.

## Recycling and disposal

Recycle the Room Sensor's CR2032 battery through a suitable battery collection point. Follow local requirements for battery collection and disposal.

At the end of its useful life, the Room Sensor is electrical equipment and should be taken to an appropriate electrical-waste collection or recycling service rather than disposed of with household waste. Separate the battery from the sensor only when required for replacement or recycling, and follow local disposal rules for each item.

## Support

For help with a Room Sensor or its battery, contact Dormouse support in the app under Help > Contact support.
