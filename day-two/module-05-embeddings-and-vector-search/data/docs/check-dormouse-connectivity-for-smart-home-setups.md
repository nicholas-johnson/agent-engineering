# Check Dormouse Connectivity for Smart-Home Setups

## What connects to your network

The Dormouse Thermostat uses Wi-Fi to connect to your home network and Dormouse account. It requires a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network and cannot join a 5 GHz network.

Dormouse Radiator Valves and Dormouse Room Sensors do not use Wi-Fi. They communicate with the thermostat for their zone over a low-power 868 MHz radio link. The thermostat is the only one of these devices that connects to Wi-Fi.

This distinction matters when checking a smart-home setup: a valve or sensor does not need to appear as a separate device on your Wi-Fi network. Check the thermostat's Wi-Fi connection instead.

## Wi-Fi requirements

Before pairing the thermostat, make sure your router is on and provides a 2.4 GHz network. If your network uses separate names for its 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz bands, select the 2.4 GHz network during pairing.

The thermostat must be wired to a gas boiler by a qualified installer. It also needs mains power through its wall plate to operate the heating. Its backup batteries keep the clock and schedules during a power cut, for up to 48 hours, but the thermostat cannot switch the heating on while mains power is off.

## Recognise a Wi-Fi disconnection

When the thermostat is not connected to Wi-Fi, its display shows a Wi-Fi symbol with a cross. This symbol indicates that the thermostat is not connected to Wi-Fi.

A cloud connection problem is different from a Wi-Fi disconnection. If the thermostat cannot reach the Dormouse cloud, it displays E-110. Your router may still be on, but the thermostat cannot communicate with the cloud. The thermostat continues running its schedules; changes made in the app wait until it reconnects.

## Check the thermostat and router

1. Look at the thermostat display for the Wi-Fi symbol with a cross or error code E-110.
2. Check that the router is switched on and connected to the internet.
3. Confirm that the Wi-Fi network provides a 2.4 GHz band. The thermostat cannot join a 5 GHz network.
4. If the network name or password has changed since pairing, pair the thermostat again.

The app displays the same error code as the thermostat and can notify you about errors. If the app cannot currently show updated information, check the thermostat display directly.

## Pair the thermostat again

If the Wi-Fi network's name or password has changed, pair the thermostat again to connect it to the network and your Dormouse account.

1. Hold the thermostat dial down for 5 seconds, until the display shows PAIR.
2. In the app, choose Add thermostat and follow the steps.
3. Keep your phone within 5 metres of the thermostat until the app says pairing has finished.

Pairing must finish within 3 minutes of PAIR appearing. If it takes longer, the display shows E-104. Start again by holding the dial for 5 seconds, and keep your phone within 5 metres during pairing.

## If the thermostat cannot find the network

If the display shows E-101, the thermostat has not found the Wi-Fi network. Check that the router is on and offers a 2.4 GHz network. A 5 GHz network alone is not suitable.

If you have changed the network name or password, pair the thermostat again using the steps above. Pairing connects the thermostat to Wi-Fi and to your Dormouse account; it does not change the requirement for a 2.4 GHz network.

## If the thermostat cannot reach the Dormouse cloud

If the display shows E-110, check that your router is connected to the internet. While the thermostat cannot reach the Dormouse cloud, it keeps running your schedules. Changes made in the app wait until the thermostat reconnects.

E-110 does not mean that the thermostat has stopped running its schedules. For this code, check the router's internet connection rather than starting pairing unless the Wi-Fi network's name or password has also changed.

## Check a zone's devices

A home can have up to four Dormouse Thermostats. Each thermostat controls its own zone and has its own schedule. A Room Sensor can measure temperature in another room in its zone, and up to ten Radiator Valves can join a zone.

Valves and sensors communicate with their zone's thermostat over a low-power 868 MHz radio link. They do not connect directly to Wi-Fi. When checking a smart-home setup, distinguish between the thermostat's Wi-Fi connection and the radio connection used by devices in its zone.

## Get help

For error codes, check the thermostat display and the Error codes table in the Dormouse Thermostat User Guide. The app shows the same code and can notify you about it.

If you need further help, contact Dormouse support in the app under Help > Contact support.
