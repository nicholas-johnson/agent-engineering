# Resolve error E-401: Backup batteries low

## What E-401 means

E-401 means the thermostat's two AA backup batteries are low. The batteries keep the clock and your schedules during a power cut. They do not power the heating: if the mains power is off, the thermostat cannot switch the heating on.

The display shows E-401 when the batteries need replacing. The app shows the same error code and can notify you about it.

## When to replace the batteries

Replace both AA batteries within a month of E-401 appearing. The code is an early warning, so you have time to replace them before the batteries run out.

Replace the backup batteries once a year as part of regular care, or sooner if E-401 appears.

## Replace the batteries

1. Pull the bottom of the thermostat's front cover gently towards you to reach the batteries.
2. Replace the two AA batteries.
3. Put the front cover back in place.

## What happens if mains power is interrupted

With working backup batteries, the thermostat keeps its clock and schedules for up to 48 hours during a power cut. It still cannot switch the heating on while mains power is off.

If mains power is interrupted, the thermostat shows E-402. Check that the mains power has been restored. If E-401 is also shown, replace the backup batteries within a month.

## If E-401 remains on the display

Check that both AA batteries have been replaced. If the error code remains after replacement, contact Dormouse support in the app under Help > Contact support.
