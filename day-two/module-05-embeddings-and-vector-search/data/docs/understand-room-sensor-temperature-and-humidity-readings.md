# Understand Room Sensor temperature and humidity readings

## What the Room Sensor measures

A Dormouse Room Sensor measures temperature and humidity in the room where it is placed. It joins a zone, so its readings are associated with that zone and can be viewed in the Dormouse app.

A Room Sensor does not control the heating on its own. The thermostat controls its zone, and the sensor provides readings from another room in that zone. This can help you see conditions away from the thermostat.

## How readings are shown

Temperature readings are shown in degrees. Humidity readings are shown in the app when the sensor is connected and providing them.

The sensor measures both temperature and humidity, but the thermostat display is not described as showing Room Sensor readings. Use the app to view the readings.

## Connect a sensor to a zone

To add a Room Sensor to your home, go to Settings > Home > Add a device and follow the app steps. Choose the zone the sensor should join.

A Room Sensor can join one zone to measure conditions in another room of that zone. A zone can have up to three Dormouse Room Sensors. Each sensor communicates with its zone's thermostat over a low-power 868 MHz radio link. Only the thermostat uses Wi-Fi.

## Place the sensor where you want to measure

Place the sensor in the room whose temperature and humidity you want to monitor. Its readings describe the conditions around the sensor, rather than conditions throughout the whole zone.

For a useful reading, leave the sensor in the room and position where you want to observe conditions. If you move it, the readings will reflect the conditions at its new position.

## Understand differences between readings

Readings from a Room Sensor may differ from the thermostat's room-temperature reading because they come from different places. Temperature and humidity can vary between rooms and within a room.

A reading from one sensor represents the conditions measured at that sensor. It does not mean every room in the zone has the same temperature or humidity.

## If humidity readings are missing

If humidity readings disappear from the app, check the thermostat display for error code E-221. This code means the humidity sensor has a fault. The heating keeps working, but humidity readings disappear from the app.

If E-221 remains for more than a day, contact Dormouse support in the app under Help > Contact support.

## Sensor power and connection

The Room Sensor uses one CR2032 coin cell, which lasts about a year. The sensor communicates with the thermostat in its zone over a low-power radio link; it does not connect to Wi-Fi directly.

If readings are unavailable, check that the sensor is still added to the correct zone and that the thermostat is available. The thermostat is the device that connects to Wi-Fi.

## More about your home

A home can have up to four Dormouse Thermostats, each controlling its own zone. Up to three Room Sensors can join each zone. To manage devices in your home, go to Settings > Home > Add a device.
