# Check how many Room Sensors can join a zone

A zone can have up to three Dormouse Room Sensors. Each sensor measures temperature and humidity in another room in the zone.

## How sensors work with a zone

A Dormouse Room Sensor joins a zone controlled by a Dormouse Thermostat. The sensor measures conditions in another room in that zone. The thermostat, Room Sensors and any Radiator Valves in the zone communicate over a low-power radio link.

Only the thermostat uses Wi-Fi. Room Sensors do not need to connect to your Wi-Fi network.

## Check the limit

Count the Room Sensors already assigned to the zone. You can add Room Sensors until there are three in that zone. If the zone already has three, it has reached its limit.

A home can have up to four Dormouse Thermostats, each controlling its own zone. The limit of three Room Sensors applies separately to each zone.

## Add a Room Sensor

To add a Room Sensor, go to Settings > Home > Add a device in the app or web dashboard, then follow the instructions.

The Room Sensor uses one CR2032 coin cell, which lasts about a year. When choosing where to use your sensors, remember that each one belongs to a zone and measures temperature and humidity in another room of that zone.
