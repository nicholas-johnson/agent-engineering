# Choose app notifications

## About notifications

The Dormouse app can send notifications about events at home and on your thermostat. Each household member chooses their own notifications. Your choices do not change notifications for other members.

To manage your choices, open the app and go to Settings > Notifications.

## Choose which events to receive

In Settings > Notifications, select the events you want the app to report. You can choose from:

| Event | What the app reports |
|---|---|
| Thermostat error | The thermostat shows an error code. The app shows the same code. |
| Low backup batteries | The thermostat's backup batteries are low. |
| Heating while everyone is away | The heating has been on for 2 hours while everyone is away. |
| Firmware update installed | A firmware update has been installed. |
| Monthly energy report | Your monthly energy report is ready. |

## Thermostat errors

When something is wrong, the thermostat displays an error code. The app shows the same code and can notify you about it. Use the code to identify the issue and follow the guidance in the thermostat's user guide.

For example, E-401 means the backup batteries are low. Replace the two AA batteries behind the front cover within a month.

## Low backup batteries

The app can notify you when the thermostat's backup batteries are low. The display also shows E-401. Replace the two AA batteries behind the front cover within a month.

The batteries keep the clock and schedules during a power cut, for up to 48 hours. The thermostat cannot switch the heating on while mains power is off.

## Heating while everyone is away

The app can notify you if the heating has been on for 2 hours while everyone is away. This notification reports the heating activity; it does not turn the heating off.

Away mode and geofencing are separate from notification choices. Geofencing can turn away mode on when every member who shares their location is more than 1 km from home, and off when the first of them returns within 1 km.

## Firmware updates

The thermostat installs firmware updates automatically between 02:00 and 04:00 local time. The app can notify you when an update has been installed.

During installation, the display shows UPDATING and the thermostat restarts. The process takes about two minutes, and the heating pauses until it is finished. Updates cannot be postponed.

## Monthly energy reports

The app can notify you when your monthly energy report is ready. Reports arrive on the 1st of each month in the app's Energy tab.

A report shows how many hours the heating was on each day, compared with the month before, and an estimate of gas used in kWh. The estimate depends on your boiler size, which you can enter in Settings > Home > Boiler size.

## Each member's choices

Every member selects their own notifications in Settings > Notifications. Changing your choices does not change what other members receive.

A home can have one owner and up to eight members. Members can change the temperature, schedules and away mode; only the owner can add or remove devices, invite members or remove them.
