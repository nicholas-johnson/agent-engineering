# Thermostat Power and Backup Battery Data Sheet

## Product and scope

This data sheet covers power for the Dormouse Thermostat: its mains supply through the wall plate and its two AA backup batteries. It describes what the batteries support during a power cut and the battery replacement guidance.

The thermostat is intended for indoor use between 0 and 40 degrees. Its backup batteries support the clock and schedules during a mains power interruption; they do not provide heating control while mains power is off.

## Mains power

The thermostat is powered by mains electricity through its wall plate. It must be wired to a compatible gas boiler by a qualified installer.

When mains power is interrupted, the thermostat switches to its backup batteries. It cannot switch the heating on while mains power is off, even though the clock and schedules are retained. The display shows E-402 when mains power is interrupted.

## Backup batteries

The thermostat uses two AA batteries behind the front cover. They keep the clock and schedules during a power cut for up to 48 hours.

The batteries do not power the heating controls during a mains outage. The thermostat cannot switch the heating on until mains power is restored.

## Power-cut behaviour

| Condition | Thermostat behaviour |
|---|---|
| Mains power is available | The thermostat is powered through the wall plate. |
| Mains power is interrupted | The thermostat runs on its backup batteries and displays E-402. |
| During the interruption | The clock and schedules are retained for up to 48 hours. The thermostat cannot switch the heating on. |
| Mains power is restored | The thermostat can resume operation using mains power. |

## Battery replacement

Replace the two AA batteries once a year, or when the display shows E-401. The E-401 warning indicates that the backup batteries are low. Replace them within a month.

To reach the batteries, pull the bottom of the front cover gently towards you. Fit two replacement AA batteries, then refit the front cover.

## Battery and power alerts

The thermostat uses these error codes to indicate battery and mains-power conditions:

| Code | Meaning | Required action |
|---|---|---|
| E-401 | Backup batteries low | Replace the two AA batteries behind the front cover within a month. |
| E-402 | Mains power interrupted | The thermostat is running on its backup batteries. It retains the clock and schedules for up to 48 hours, but cannot switch the heating on. |

The app shows the same error code as the thermostat and can notify you about it. Each household member chooses their notifications in Settings > Notifications.

## Handling and care

Access the batteries by gently pulling the bottom of the front cover towards you. Replace the batteries as indicated above.

Clean the thermostat with a soft, dry cloth. Do not use sprays or cleaning products, and do not paint over it.
