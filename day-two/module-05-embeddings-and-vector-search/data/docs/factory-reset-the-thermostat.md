# Factory reset the thermostat

A factory reset removes the thermostat's Wi-Fi details, schedules and link to your Dormouse account. The thermostat then restarts as if it were new, ready to pair again.

Use a factory reset before giving the thermostat to someone else or moving house without it.

## Before you reset

A reset removes:

- The Wi-Fi details saved on the thermostat
- Its schedules
- Its link to your Dormouse account

You will need to pair the thermostat again to connect it to Wi-Fi and your account. If you still want to use the thermostat, set up its schedules again after pairing.

## Reset the thermostat

1. Hold the dial and the boost button down together.
2. Keep holding them for 10 seconds, until the display shows **RESET**.
3. Release the buttons. The thermostat restarts as if it were new.

## Pair it again

After the thermostat restarts, pair it using the Dormouse app:

1. Hold the dial down for 5 seconds, until the display shows **PAIR**.
2. In the app, choose **Add thermostat** and follow the steps.
3. Keep your phone within 5 metres of the thermostat until the app says pairing has finished.

Pairing must finish within 3 minutes of **PAIR** appearing. If it takes longer, the display shows **E-104** and you need to start pairing again.
