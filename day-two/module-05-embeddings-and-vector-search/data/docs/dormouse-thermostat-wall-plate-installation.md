# Dormouse Thermostat Wall-Plate Installation

## Scope and safety

This guide covers mounting the Dormouse Thermostat wall plate and wiring it to a compatible gas boiler. The thermostat is designed for combi, system and conventional gas boilers.

The thermostat must be wired to the boiler by a qualified installer. If you are not qualified to work on the boiler wiring, arrange installation by a qualified installer. Do not proceed if the boiler type or its wiring requirements are unclear.

This guide does not provide a boiler-specific wiring diagram or terminal assignment. Boiler connections vary. Use the boiler manufacturer's instructions and confirm the correct connections before wiring the wall plate.

## Before installation

Check that the installation location and household setup are suitable:

- The thermostat is for indoor use between 0 and 40 degrees.
- The thermostat is powered from mains through its wall plate.
- The home needs a Wi-Fi network on 2.4 GHz. The thermostat cannot join a 5 GHz network.
- Setup requires the Dormouse app on a phone running Android 12 or later, or iOS 17 or later, and a Dormouse account.

The thermostat measures temperature to an accuracy of 0.3 degrees. Choose an appropriate indoor position for the thermostat and follow the boiler manufacturer's requirements for the wiring connection. Confirm that the wall and wiring arrangement are suitable before fixing the wall plate.

## Tools and preparation

Use tools and installation materials appropriate to the mounting surface and the boiler wiring. Confirm the boiler and its electrical supply are safe to work on before handling wiring. Follow the boiler manufacturer's isolation and safety procedures.

Before starting, check that you can identify the boiler's required thermostat connections and the corresponding connections on the Dormouse wall plate. Do not infer terminal functions from wire colour alone. If the boiler documentation does not make the connection clear, stop and have a qualified installer confirm it.

Keep the thermostat and its wall plate accessible until mounting and wiring checks are complete.

## Mount the wall plate

1. Select an indoor mounting position suitable for the thermostat and its wiring.
2. Prepare the mounting surface and cable entry in accordance with the wall plate arrangement and the surface material.
3. Fix the wall plate securely using fixings appropriate to the mounting surface.
4. Check that the wall plate is stable and correctly positioned before connecting the thermostat.

The thermostat measures 84 mm across and is 24 mm deep. Its display is a 2.4-inch round colour display. Allow adequate clearance around the unit so it can be fitted to the wall plate and operated at the dial and boost button.

## Connect the boiler wiring

The wall plate provides the mains power connection for the thermostat and the connection to the boiler. The boiler must be compatible with the Dormouse Thermostat and be one of the supported gas boiler types: combi, system or conventional.

1. Keep the boiler isolated while making the wiring connections.
2. Consult the boiler manufacturer's wiring instructions to identify the correct thermostat connection points.
3. Connect the boiler wiring to the appropriate wall plate connections, following the boiler and wall plate markings.
4. Check that each connection is secure and that no conductor is left exposed or liable to contact another terminal.
5. Recheck the wiring against the boiler manufacturer's instructions before restoring power.

This guide does not specify terminal numbers, wire colours or a wiring schematic. Do not connect wires based on assumptions. If the terminal markings or boiler instructions are unclear, do not energise the installation; ask a qualified installer to verify it.

## Fit the thermostat and check operation

Once the wall plate is mounted and wiring has been checked, fit the thermostat to the wall plate according to its physical alignment. Restore power only after completing the wiring checks and following the boiler manufacturer's procedure.

Check that the thermostat display starts normally and that the boiler responds as expected when the heating is called for. The flame symbol means the thermostat is asking the boiler for heat. If the thermostat shows an error code, use the code and its guidance in the user guide before handing the system over.

If the display shows E-305, check that the boiler is switched on and that its pressure is between 1 and 1.5 bar. Check the boiler's own display for a fault. If it shows E-312, the boiler has reported a fault; refer to the boiler manual or contact a registered gas engineer.

## Pair and hand over

Pairing connects the thermostat to the household Wi-Fi and Dormouse account. The network must be 2.4 GHz.

1. Hold the thermostat dial down for 5 seconds, until the display shows PAIR.
2. In the Dormouse app, choose Add thermostat and follow the steps.
3. Keep the phone within 5 metres of the thermostat until pairing finishes.

Pairing must finish within 3 minutes of PAIR appearing. If it takes longer, the display shows E-104. Hold the dial down for 5 seconds to start pairing again, then repeat the steps.

Show the customer how to turn the dial to change the target temperature, press the dial to confirm, and use the boost button. A manual temperature change lasts until the next schedule period starts. Explain that schedules are set in the app or web dashboard; the thermostat menu can show today's periods but cannot edit them.

## Installation notes

The thermostat's backup batteries are two AA batteries behind the front cover. They keep the clock and schedules during a power cut for up to 48 hours, but the thermostat cannot switch the heating on while mains power is off. Replace the batteries once a year or when E-401 appears.

The thermostat connects to Wi-Fi. Dormouse Radiator Valves and Dormouse Room Sensors communicate with their zone's thermostat over a low-power radio link; they do not use Wi-Fi. A home can have up to four thermostats, each controlling its own zone.
