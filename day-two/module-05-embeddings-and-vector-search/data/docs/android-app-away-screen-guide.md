# Android app: Away screen guide

## About this guide

This guide explains how to use the Away screen in the Dormouse app for Android. The same Away controls are available in the iOS app and the web dashboard.

Away mode keeps your home at the away temperature until you turn it off. The away temperature is 16 degrees by default. You can change it in Settings > Away > Away temperature.

## Open the Away screen

In the app, tap Away to open the controls for away mode. The screen shows whether away mode is on or off and provides access to the away temperature setting.

You can also turn away mode on from the thermostat: press the dial to open the menu, turn it to Away, and press the dial to choose.

## Turn away mode on

1. Open Away in the app.
2. Turn away mode on using the control on the screen.

The home then uses the away temperature until away mode is turned off. Frost protection remains active: if the room falls below 5 degrees, the thermostat turns the heating on even while away mode is on.

## Turn away mode off

Open Away in the app and turn away mode off. The thermostat stops using the away temperature. Heating then follows the schedule, unless you have changed the target temperature by hand; a manual temperature change lasts until the next schedule period starts.

You can also turn away mode off from the thermostat menu. Press the dial, choose Away, and press the dial to change the mode.

## Change the away temperature

1. Open Settings > Away > Away temperature.
2. Set the temperature you want to use while away.

The default is 16 degrees. Away mode keeps the home at the temperature you set until you turn away mode off. The thermostat's target temperature range is 5 to 30 degrees, in half-degree steps.

Changing the away temperature does not by itself turn away mode on or off.

## Geofencing

Geofencing can turn away mode on and off automatically using the location of household members' phones. Away mode turns on when every member who shares their location is more than 1 km from home. It turns off when the first of them comes back within 1 km.

Each household member chooses whether to share their location in the app. To stop geofencing for the whole home, go to Settings > Away > Geofencing. You can still control away mode manually from the Away screen.

## Display indicators and heating protection

When away mode is on, the thermostat display shows a house with an arrow. If the room falls below 5 degrees, frost protection heats the home, and the display shows a snowflake while it is heating. Frost protection is always on and cannot be turned off.

The thermostat may also show other symbols, such as a flame when it is asking the boiler for heat. These indicators describe the thermostat's current state; away mode itself is shown by the house-with-an-arrow symbol.

## Household controls and notifications

Members of a home can change the temperature, schedules and away mode. Only the owner can add or remove devices, invite members or remove them.

Each member chooses their own notifications in Settings > Notifications. The app can notify a member when the heating has been on for 2 hours while everyone is away. This notification does not change the away temperature or turn away mode off.

## If the controls do not update

The thermostat needs an internet connection to receive changes made in the app. If it cannot reach the Dormouse cloud, it shows E-110. Check that your router is connected to the internet. The thermostat continues running its schedules, and changes made in the app wait until it reconnects.

For help with the app or thermostat, open Help > Contact support in the app.
