# Check your thermostat hardware revision

You can identify a Dormouse Thermostat's hardware revision from the first three letters of its serial number. Revisions A, B and C have the prefixes DMA, DMB and DMC respectively.

## Find the serial number

Check the label on your thermostat or its packaging for the serial number. Look at the first three characters.

| Serial number prefix | Hardware revision |
|---|---|
| DMA | A |
| DMB | B |
| DMC | C |

For example, a serial number beginning with DMB is for hardware revision B.

## About hardware revisions

Dormouse Thermostats have three hardware revisions:

- Revision A was released in 2024.
- Revision B was released in 2025.
- Revision C was released in 2026.

The prefix identifies the hardware revision. Firmware is separate: to check your thermostat's firmware version, press the dial to open the menu, then choose Menu > About.
