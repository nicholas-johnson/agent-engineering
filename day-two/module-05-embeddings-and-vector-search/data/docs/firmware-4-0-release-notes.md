# Firmware 4.0 release notes

## Warm Wake

Warm Wake starts the heating up to 90 minutes before a schedule period, so your home is warm when the period starts.

You can turn Warm Wake off in Settings > Heating > Warm Wake.

## New display symbols

Firmware 4.0 adds new symbols to the thermostat display, including the sunrise symbol for Warm Wake.

## Dormouse Room Sensor

Firmware 4.0 adds support for the Dormouse Room Sensor. It measures temperature and humidity in another room of a zone and joins that zone's thermostat.
