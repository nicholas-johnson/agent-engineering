# Manage Household Access for Connected Controls

## About household access

Household sharing lets other people access a Dormouse home. A home has one owner and can have up to eight members. Members can change the temperature, schedules and away mode.

The owner has additional controls: only the owner can add or remove devices, invite members or remove them. These permissions apply to the Dormouse home. A connected service does not give a member more control over the home than their Dormouse access allows.

## Invite a household member

Invite members from the app or web dashboard:

1. Go to Settings > Household > Invite.
2. Enter the member's email address.
3. The member receives an email with a link to join.

An invitation works for 7 days. If it expires before the person joins, the owner needs to send a new invitation. The home can have up to eight members; the owner is not counted as a member.

## Member controls

Members can change:

- The target temperature.
- The home's schedules.
- Away mode.

These controls apply to the home's Dormouse heating setup. For example, a member can change a schedule or turn away mode on or off. Only the owner can add or remove thermostats, sensors or valves, invite household members, or remove them.

A home can have up to four Dormouse Thermostats. Each controls its own zone and schedule. A Dormouse Room Sensor can join a zone, and up to ten Dormouse Radiator Valves can join a zone.

## Connected services

A connected service may provide a way to access Dormouse controls through another system, such as home automation or a voice assistant. Access through a connected service does not change the household roles described in this guide. Members can change the temperature, schedules and away mode; owner-only actions remain the owner's responsibility.

The thermostat connects to Wi-Fi. Dormouse Radiator Valves and Room Sensors communicate with their zone's thermostat over a low-power 868 MHz radio link; they do not use Wi-Fi themselves.

## Location sharing and geofencing

Each household member chooses whether to share their phone's location in the app. Geofencing uses the location of members who share it to turn away mode on or off: away mode turns on when every sharing member is more than 1 km from home, and turns off when the first of them comes back within 1 km.

To stop geofencing for the whole home, go to Settings > Away > Geofencing. Phone locations are never stored; Dormouse stores only whether each member is home or away.

## Manage notifications

Each member chooses their own app notifications in Settings > Notifications. Available notifications include thermostat errors, low backup batteries, heating left on for 2 hours while everyone is away, firmware updates and monthly energy reports.

Changing notification preferences does not change household access or connected-service permissions.

## Manage access safely

Invite only people who should have access to the home. If a member should no longer have access, the owner can remove them from the household. Only the owner can do this.

If you are moving house or giving the thermostat to someone else, use a factory reset. This removes the Wi-Fi details, schedules and link to your account. Hold the dial and boost button together for 10 seconds, until the display shows RESET. The thermostat restarts as if it were new and can then be paired again.

## Help and account security

Sign in to the app or web dashboard with your email address and password. Passwords must have at least 10 characters. Two-step verification asks for a 6-digit code when signing in on a new phone or browser. To turn it on, go to Settings > Account > Two-step verification.

For help with household or connected-service access, contact Dormouse support in the app under Help > Contact support.
