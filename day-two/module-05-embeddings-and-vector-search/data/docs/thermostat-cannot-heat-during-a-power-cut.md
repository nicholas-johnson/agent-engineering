# Thermostat Cannot Heat During a Power Cut

## What happens during a power cut

The Dormouse Thermostat needs mains power to switch the heating on. If mains power is interrupted, it cannot ask the boiler for heat, even if the room is cold or a schedule period calls for a higher temperature.

Two AA backup batteries help the thermostat keep its clock and schedules during a power cut, for up to 48 hours. They do not let it operate the heating without mains power. Keeping schedules does not mean the thermostat can run them while the power is off.

## Check for an interruption to mains power

Look at the thermostat display. If it shows E-402, mains power has been interrupted and the thermostat is running on its backup batteries.

Check whether mains power is available to the thermostat. Its power comes through its wall plate. If power is still off, heating cannot operate through the thermostat. The batteries preserve the clock and schedules only.

When mains power returns, check whether the thermostat display is operating normally. If the heating still does not work, follow the relevant checks below.

## Understand what the backup batteries retain

The backup batteries keep the thermostat's clock and schedules for up to 48 hours without mains power. This helps preserve schedule information through a power cut.

The batteries do not provide power for switching the heating on. The thermostat cannot heat the home while mains power is off, regardless of whether:

- the schedule calls for a higher target temperature
- you have changed the target temperature by hand
- a boost is running or requested
- away mode is on or off

Frost protection also cannot switch the heating on while mains power is off. Frost protection is always on, but it still needs the thermostat to have mains power to operate the heating.

## Check the backup batteries

If the display shows E-401, the backup batteries are low. Replace the two AA batteries behind the front cover within a month.

To reach them, gently pull the bottom of the front cover towards you. Replace both batteries, then make sure the front cover is back in place.

Low backup batteries are not a way to restore heating during a power cut. Even with new batteries, the thermostat cannot switch the heating on while mains power is off. The batteries are for keeping the clock and schedules during an interruption, for up to 48 hours.

## Check whether the heating should be operating

Once mains power is available again, check the room temperature and target temperature on the display. A hand-set target temperature lasts until the next schedule period starts, so the target may have changed when a new period began.

A boost heats at the boost temperature for one hour, but it also requires mains power. Pressing the boost button during a power cut cannot make the thermostat operate the heating.

If the thermostat has power but the boiler does not respond, check the display for an error code. For E-305, check that the boiler is switched on and its pressure is between 1 and 1.5 bar, then check the boiler's own display for a fault. For E-312, consult the boiler's manual or call a registered gas engineer.

## If an error code appears

The display and app show the same error code. Use the code to identify the issue:

| Code | What it indicates | What to do |
|------|-------------------|------------|
| E-402 | Mains power interrupted; the thermostat is on backup batteries | Restore mains power. The thermostat cannot switch the heating on while it is off. |
| E-401 | Backup batteries are low | Replace the two AA batteries behind the front cover within a month. |
| E-305 | Boiler not responding | Check that the boiler is on and its pressure is between 1 and 1.5 bar. Check the boiler display for a fault. |
| E-312 | Boiler reports a fault | Look up the fault in the boiler's manual, or call a registered gas engineer. |

For any other error code, follow the guidance for that code in the thermostat user guide.

## After mains power returns

The thermostat uses its backup batteries to retain the clock and schedules for up to 48 hours during a power cut. Once mains power is restored, check that the thermostat is operating and review the displayed target temperature and the current schedule period.

If the power cut lasted longer than 48 hours, the guide does not specify what happens to the clock or schedules. If they appear incorrect, check the schedule in the app or web dashboard. Schedules can be viewed on the thermostat under Menu > Schedule, but can only be changed in the app or web dashboard.

## When to contact support

If mains power is available but the thermostat continues to show an error, use the error-code guidance in the user guide. For a temperature sensor disconnection (E-217), the thermostat turns the heating off to be safe; contact Dormouse support to arrange a replacement.

Contact support in the app under Help > Contact support if you need help with a thermostat issue that is not covered by the error-code guidance.
