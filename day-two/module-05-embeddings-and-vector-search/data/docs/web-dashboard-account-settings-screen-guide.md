# Web dashboard: Account settings screen guide

## About this guide

This guide explains account settings in the Dormouse web dashboard. The account settings described here are also available in the Android and iOS apps. Menu paths are the same in the apps and the web dashboard.

The Dormouse Thermostat works with a Dormouse account. Use your account to sign in to the app and web dashboard and, if you choose, add two-step verification to protect sign-ins on new phones and browsers.

## Sign in

Sign in to the web dashboard with your email address and password. Your password must have at least 10 characters.

If you cannot remember your password, choose **Forgot password?** on the sign-in screen and enter your email address. A password reset link is sent to you. The link works for 30 minutes and can only be used once. If it expires before you use it, request a new link.

## Find account settings

Open **Settings > Account** to find the account options covered in this guide. Two-step verification is managed from this screen.

You can also change most Dormouse settings in the app. Changes made in the app or web dashboard are account settings; the thermostat's physical dial and menu provide access to thermostat functions such as choosing **Menu > About** to check its firmware version.

## Two-step verification

Two-step verification adds a 6-digit code to your password when you sign in on a new phone or browser. The code can arrive by email, or you can use an authenticator app if you have set one up.

To turn on two-step verification:

1. Open **Settings > Account > Two-step verification**.
2. Follow the instructions on the screen to enable it.
3. Choose the code method offered: email, or an authenticator app if you have set one up.

When two-step verification is on, you need your password and the code when signing in on a new phone or browser. Keep access to the selected code method available when you sign in.

## Password reset links

A password reset link is for changing a forgotten password. It is separate from the 6-digit code used for two-step verification.

To request a reset link, choose **Forgot password?** on the sign-in screen and enter the email address for your account. Use the link within 30 minutes. It can be used once; if it has expired or has already been used, request another.

## Account and household settings

Account settings belong to the signed-in account. Household settings control who can access a home and what members can do. A home has one owner and can have up to eight members.

Members can change the temperature, schedules and away mode. Only the owner can add or remove devices, invite members or remove them. The owner can invite someone from **Settings > Household > Invite**. The invitation is sent by email and works for 7 days; after that, the owner needs to send a new one.

## Other settings in the dashboard

Account settings are one part of the Dormouse dashboard. Other settings are grouped by what they control. For example:

| Setting | Where to find it |
|---|---|
| Two-step verification | Settings > Account > Two-step verification |
| Away temperature | Settings > Away > Away temperature |
| Geofencing | Settings > Away > Geofencing |
| Boost temperature | Settings > Heating > Boost temperature |
| Notifications | Settings > Notifications |

Each household member chooses their own notifications. Available app notifications include thermostat error codes, low backup batteries, heating running for 2 hours while everyone is away, installed firmware updates and monthly energy reports.

## Account access and support

Use your Dormouse account credentials to sign in to the app and web dashboard. Two-step verification applies when you sign in on a new phone or browser; it does not change the thermostat's Wi-Fi connection or its heating schedule.

For help with your account or the dashboard, contact Dormouse support in the app under **Help > Contact support**.
