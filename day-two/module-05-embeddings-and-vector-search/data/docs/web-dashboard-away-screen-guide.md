# Web dashboard: Away screen guide

## About this guide

This guide explains the away mode controls and away temperature setting in the Dormouse web dashboard. The same settings are available in the Dormouse app for Android and iOS. Menu paths are the same in the app and web dashboard.

Away mode keeps your home at the away temperature until you turn it off. It is separate from your heating schedule: while away mode is on, the away temperature applies instead of the schedule temperature.

## Open the Away screen

Open the Dormouse web dashboard and select Away to turn away mode on. In the app, tap Away. You can also turn away mode on from the thermostat by choosing Menu > Away.

The Away screen shows the control for away mode and the temperature used while it is on. Use the away mode control to switch the mode on or off. The change applies to the home.

## Turn away mode on

Turn away mode on when you want the home to use its away temperature rather than follow the usual schedule. Away mode stays on until you turn it off, unless geofencing changes it for you.

When away mode is on, the thermostat maintains the away temperature. Frost protection remains active: if the room falls below 5 degrees, the thermostat turns the heating on even when away mode is on.

## Turn away mode off

Turn away mode off when you want the home to return to its normal heating schedule. Once away mode is off, the schedule controls the target temperature again.

A manual temperature change made at the thermostat or in the app lasts until the next schedule period starts. Away mode is a separate control and remains on until it is turned off or geofencing switches it off.

## Change the away temperature

The away temperature is 16 degrees by default. To change it in the web dashboard or app, go to Settings > Away > Away temperature and choose the temperature you want.

The away temperature is used whenever away mode is on, whether you turned it on yourself or geofencing turned it on. Changing this setting does not turn away mode on or off; it changes the temperature the home uses while away mode is active.

## Geofencing

Geofencing can turn away mode on and off using the location of household members' phones. It turns away mode on when every member who shares their location is more than 1 km from home. It turns away mode off when the first of them comes back within 1 km.

Each household member chooses whether to share their location in the app. To stop geofencing for the whole home, go to Settings > Away > Geofencing. Turning geofencing off stops it from automatically changing away mode; you can still control away mode from the Away screen.

Phone locations are never stored. The system stores only whether each member is home or away.

## Thermostat indicators

The thermostat display shows a house with an arrow when away mode is on. If frost protection is heating while away mode is on, the display shows a snowflake. Frost protection cannot be turned off.

The thermostat menu provides a way to turn away mode on, but the away temperature is changed in the app or web dashboard under Settings > Away > Away temperature.

## Household access and support

Members of a home can change away mode and temperatures. Only the home owner can add or remove devices, invite household members or remove them.

If you need help with the Away screen, contact Dormouse support in the app under Help > Contact support.
