# Web dashboard: Add thermostat screen guide

## Purpose

The Add thermostat screen helps you connect a Dormouse Thermostat to your Wi-Fi network and Dormouse account. Pairing must be started on the thermostat and completed in the Dormouse app. The web dashboard does not pair a thermostat.

This guide covers what to prepare, how to start pairing, how to finish setup in the app and what to do if the pairing time limit expires.

## Before you begin

Check that you have:

- A Dormouse Thermostat wired to a gas boiler by a qualified installer.
- A Wi-Fi network that operates on 2.4 GHz. The thermostat cannot join a 5 GHz network.
- The Dormouse app on a phone running Android 12 or later, or iOS 17 or later.
- A Dormouse account. Create an account in the app if you do not already have one.

Keep the phone within 5 metres of the thermostat throughout pairing. The process must finish within 3 minutes of the thermostat displaying PAIR.

## Start pairing on the thermostat

1. Hold the thermostat dial down for 5 seconds.
2. Release it when the display shows PAIR.
3. Begin setup in the app promptly. The 3-minute pairing time limit starts when PAIR appears.

If the display shows E-104, the pairing time limit has expired. Start again by holding the dial down for 5 seconds until PAIR appears.

## Continue in the app

Open the Dormouse app and choose **Add thermostat**. Follow the steps shown in the app to connect the thermostat to Wi-Fi and your Dormouse account.

Keep the app open and the phone within 5 metres of the thermostat until the app says setup has finished. Do not wait until the end of the time limit to begin: pairing must be complete within 3 minutes of PAIR appearing.

## What the setup connects

Pairing links the thermostat to both your Wi-Fi network and your Dormouse account. The thermostat needs a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network; it cannot join a 5 GHz network.

A Dormouse home can have up to four thermostats. Each thermostat controls its own zone and has its own schedule. To add another thermostat, use **Add thermostat** in the app and complete pairing for that device.

## If pairing times out

If pairing does not finish within 3 minutes, the thermostat displays E-104 and you need to start again:

1. Hold the dial down for 5 seconds, until PAIR appears.
2. In the app, choose **Add thermostat** and follow the setup steps.
3. Keep the phone within 5 metres of the thermostat until the app confirms setup is complete.

Each attempt has a new 3-minute time limit, starting when PAIR appears.

## If the Wi-Fi network is not found

If the thermostat displays E-101, check that your router is on and that the network offers a 2.4 GHz connection. The thermostat cannot connect to a 5 GHz network.

If you have changed the network name or password, pair the thermostat again using the app. Start pairing on the thermostat by holding the dial down for 5 seconds, then complete setup within 3 minutes.

## After setup

When the app says setup has finished, pairing is complete. The thermostat is connected to the Wi-Fi network and your Dormouse account.

The thermostat menu includes **Schedule**, **Away**, **Settings** and **About**. You can view today's schedule periods on the thermostat, but change schedules in the app or web dashboard. Most settings can be changed in the app or web dashboard; menu paths are the same in both.

## If you need help

For pairing errors, use the code on the thermostat display and in the app to identify the issue. E-104 means pairing timed out; E-101 means the Wi-Fi network was not found.

Contact Dormouse support in the app under **Help > Contact support**.
