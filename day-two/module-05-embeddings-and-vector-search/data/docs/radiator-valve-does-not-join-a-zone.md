# Radiator Valve Does Not Join a Zone

## Check how you are adding the valve

Add a Dormouse Radiator Valve through the Dormouse app or web dashboard:

1. Open **Settings > Home > Add a device**.
2. Follow the instructions to add the valve to a zone.

Adding devices is done in the app or web dashboard. Do not try to add the valve from the thermostat's Schedule menu.

## Check the zone's valve limit

A zone can have up to ten Dormouse Radiator Valves. If the zone already has ten, it cannot accept another one.

Check how many valves are already in the zone. If it has reached the limit, remove a valve you no longer need from the zone before trying to add another.

## Check which thermostat controls the zone

Each Dormouse Thermostat controls its own zone. A valve must join the zone it is intended to control.

A home can have up to four Dormouse Thermostats, each with its own zone and schedule. Before adding the valve, confirm that you are choosing the correct zone in **Settings > Home > Add a device**.

## Understand how the valve connects

The Dormouse Radiator Valve communicates with its zone's thermostat over a low-power 868 MHz radio link. The valve does not join your Wi-Fi network; only the thermostat uses Wi-Fi.

Follow the app's instructions for adding the valve to the zone. If you are trying to connect the valve to Wi-Fi, return to **Settings > Home > Add a device** and add it to the correct zone instead.

## Check the valve's fit

The Dormouse Radiator Valve fits M30 x 1.5 valve bodies. Adapters for other common valve bodies are included in the box.

If the valve does not fit the radiator valve body, check whether one of the supplied adapters is suitable before continuing. Do not force the valve onto the fitting.

## Try adding the valve again

After confirming that you are using **Settings > Home > Add a device**, that the zone has fewer than ten valves and that you have selected the correct zone, follow the app's instructions to try again.

If the valve still does not join the zone, contact Dormouse support in the app under **Help > Contact support**.
