# iOS app: Heating settings screen guide

## About this guide

This guide covers heating controls in the Dormouse app for iOS. It explains where to change the boost temperature and what happens when you set a target temperature by hand.

The thermostat works with the Dormouse app for Android and iOS, and with the web dashboard. Most settings can be changed in any of them. Menu paths in this guide are the same in the app and the web dashboard.

## Open the heating settings

To find the boost temperature setting:

1. Open the Dormouse app.
2. Go to Settings > Heating.
3. Choose Boost temperature.

The boost temperature is the target used when a boost is running. It is 21 degrees unless you change it.

## Change the boost temperature

Choose a boost temperature in the range supported by the thermostat: 5 to 30 degrees, in half-degree steps. The value you set is used for a boost started with the thermostat's boost button.

To start a boost, press the button below the thermostat's dial. The thermostat heats the home at the boost temperature for one hour. An upward arrow appears on the display while the boost is running. Press the boost button again to cancel it.

## Set a target temperature by hand

You can change the target temperature by turning the thermostat dial or using the app. The thermostat accepts target temperatures from 5 to 30 degrees, in half-degree steps.

A hand-set temperature change lasts until the next schedule period starts. At that point, the schedule sets the target temperature for the new period.

## Target temperature and boost

A hand-set target temperature and a boost are different controls:

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| Target temperature | Changes the target temperature until the next schedule period starts. |
| Boost | Heats at the boost temperature for one hour, unless you cancel it sooner. |

Changing the boost temperature changes the temperature used for boosts. It does not change the temperatures in your schedule.

## Schedules and heating

A schedule sets the target temperature at different times of day. Each day can have up to six periods. Each period has a start time and target temperature, and lasts until the next period starts.

You can set or change schedules in the Schedule tab of the app or web dashboard. On the thermostat, Menu > Schedule shows today's periods, but schedules can only be changed in the app or web dashboard.

## Frost protection

Frost protection is always on and cannot be turned off. If the room temperature falls below 5 degrees, the thermostat turns the heating on, even if the heating is off or away mode is on. A snowflake appears on the display while frost protection is heating.

## Temperature limits

The target temperature can be set from 5 to 30 degrees, in half-degree steps. These limits apply when you set a target temperature by hand and when you choose the boost temperature.

## Related controls

Away mode keeps the home at the away temperature until you turn it off. The away temperature is 16 degrees unless you change it in Settings > Away > Away temperature.

For schedule changes, use the Schedule tab in the app or web dashboard. For help with the thermostat's controls, press the dial to open its menu.
