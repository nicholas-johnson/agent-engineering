# E-305: Boiler not responding

## What E-305 means

E-305 means the Dormouse Thermostat is not getting a response from the boiler. The thermostat may not be able to switch the heating on while this error is present.

The code appears on the thermostat display. The app shows the same code and can notify you about it.

## Common causes

E-305 can appear if the boiler is switched off or its pressure is outside the recommended range. It can also appear when the boiler has detected a fault. The boiler's own display may show information about that fault.

## What you need before you start

You may need to check the boiler's power switch and pressure gauge, then look at the boiler's display. Use the boiler's manual to understand its controls and any fault shown there.

Do not open the boiler or attempt repairs. If the boiler reports a fault that you cannot resolve using its manual, contact a registered gas engineer.

## Step 1: Check that the boiler is switched on

Check that the boiler's power switch is on. If it is off, switch it on using the boiler's normal controls. Then check whether E-305 clears and the heating responds.

## Step 2: Check the boiler pressure

Read the pressure from the boiler's gauge or display. It should be between 1 and 1.5 bar.

If the pressure is outside this range, follow the instructions in the boiler's manual. Do not guess at the boiler's controls. If you are unsure what to do, contact a registered gas engineer.

## Step 3: Check the boiler's display

Look at the boiler's own display for a fault or warning. If it reports a fault, find that code or message in the boiler's manual.

If the boiler reports a fault of its own, it may also show E-312 on the thermostat. Look up the boiler fault in its manual or call a registered gas engineer.

## Step 4: Check whether heating responds

Once you have checked the boiler is on and its pressure is between 1 and 1.5 bar, check whether the heating responds to a change in target temperature. You can change the target temperature using the thermostat dial or the app.

## If E-305 remains

If the boiler is switched on, its pressure is between 1 and 1.5 bar, and the error remains, contact Dormouse support in the app under Help > Contact support. Include the E-305 code and any fault shown on the boiler's display.

If the boiler reports a fault, contact a registered gas engineer. Do not try to repair the boiler yourself.
