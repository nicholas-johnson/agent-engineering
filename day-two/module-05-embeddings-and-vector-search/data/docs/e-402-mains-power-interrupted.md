# E-402: Mains power interrupted

## What E-402 means

E-402 means the thermostat's mains power has been interrupted. The thermostat is running on its two backup AA batteries.

The batteries keep the clock and your schedules for up to 48 hours. They do not let the thermostat switch the heating on while mains power is off. Your home may therefore not be heated during the interruption.

## What can cause it

E-402 appears when the thermostat is no longer receiving power through its wall plate. This can happen if the electricity supply to the thermostat has been interrupted or the thermostat's mains connection has been switched off.

The error code identifies a loss of mains power. It does not, by itself, identify the cause.

## What to do

1. **Check whether your home has power.** If the electricity is off elsewhere in the home, wait until the supply returns.
2. **Check the thermostat's power supply.** If you know that a switch or circuit supplies power to the thermostat, check that it is on. Do not remove the thermostat from its wall plate or attempt electrical work.
3. **Allow time for the thermostat to reconnect to mains power.** When the supply is restored, the thermostat can resume switching the heating on.
4. **Check the display.** E-402 should clear when mains power returns. If it remains, or the thermostat still does not operate as expected, contact Dormouse support in the app under Help > Contact support.

## What the thermostat does during the interruption

The backup batteries keep the clock and schedules for up to 48 hours, so these are retained during a power interruption. However, the thermostat cannot switch on the heating without mains power. Frost protection also cannot make the thermostat switch on the heating while mains power is off.

If mains power is not restored within 48 hours, the backup batteries may no longer keep the clock and schedules.

## Check the backup batteries

E-402 is a mains power error, not a low-battery warning. If the display also shows E-401, replace the two AA backup batteries within a month.

To reach the batteries, gently pull the bottom of the front cover towards you. Replace the batteries once a year, or when E-401 appears. After replacing them, close the cover gently.

## If the error does not clear

If your home has mains power and the thermostat continues to show E-402, contact Dormouse support in the app under Help > Contact support. Include the error code and describe what you have checked. Do not try to repair the thermostat's mains wiring yourself; it must be wired to the boiler by a qualified installer.
