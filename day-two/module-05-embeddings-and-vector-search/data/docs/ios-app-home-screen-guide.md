# iOS app: Home screen guide

## About this guide

This guide explains how to check the room and target temperatures on the Dormouse app for iOS, and how to change the target temperature from the home screen.

The home screen shows information for the selected home and thermostat. A home can have up to four Dormouse Thermostats, each controlling its own zone. If your home has more than one thermostat, check that you are viewing the intended zone before changing its temperature.

## Temperature readings

The home screen shows the room temperature and the target temperature for the selected zone. The room temperature is the temperature the thermostat is measuring. The target temperature is the temperature the heating is set to reach.

The thermostat display also shows the room temperature in large numbers, with the target temperature underneath. In the app, use the labels beside the readings to tell them apart.

## Changing the target temperature

You can change the target temperature from the home screen:

1. Open the Dormouse app on your iOS device and select the home you want to control.
2. Choose the thermostat or zone whose temperature you want to change.
3. Use the temperature control on the home screen to set the target temperature.

The target temperature can be set from 5 to 30 degrees, in half-degree steps. The app applies the new target to the selected zone.

## How long a manual change lasts

A change you make by hand lasts until the next schedule period starts. At that time, the schedule sets the target temperature for the new period.

If you want to change the temperatures or times used by your schedule, open the Schedule tab in the app and choose a day. Each day can have up to six periods. You can use Copy day to apply the same periods to other days.

## Away mode and heating

Away mode keeps the home at its away temperature until you turn it off. The away temperature is 16 degrees unless it has been changed in Settings > Away > Away temperature. Away mode can affect the temperature the selected zone maintains.

Frost protection is always on and cannot be turned off. If the room falls below 5 degrees, the thermostat turns the heating on, even when the heating is off or away mode is on.

## Other temperature controls

For a one-hour boost, press the boost button on the thermostat. The thermostat heats at the boost temperature while the boost is running. The boost temperature is 21 degrees unless it has been changed in Settings > Heating > Boost temperature. Press the boost button again to cancel the boost.

The app home screen is for checking readings and making a manual target-temperature change. Use the Schedule tab to edit schedule periods, and the relevant settings to adjust the away or boost temperature.

## If the reading or control is unavailable

If the thermostat cannot reach the Dormouse cloud, the app may not show current information or apply changes straight away. The thermostat keeps running its schedules, and changes made in the app wait until it reconnects.

If the thermostat shows an error code, the app shows the same code and can notify you about it. For example, E-110 means the thermostat cannot reach the Dormouse cloud. Check that your router is connected to the internet. For other codes, follow the guidance in the thermostat user guide.

## Related controls

The thermostat dial can also change the target temperature. Turn the dial to adjust it, then press the dial to confirm. A manual change made this way also lasts until the next schedule period starts.

For information about setting schedules, away mode, or troubleshooting a thermostat error, refer to the relevant guide or section in the Dormouse user guide.
