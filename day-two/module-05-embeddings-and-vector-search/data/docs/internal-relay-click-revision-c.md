# Relay click on hardware revision C

## Summary

Thermostats from hardware revision C (serial numbers starting DMC) click loudly when the relay switches the boiler on or off. The relay comes from supplier batch 2309, which is louder than the relay used in revisions A and B. Engineering has tested it for 200,000 switches, and the noise is not a safety or reliability problem.

## What support should say

Tell the customer the click is the normal sound of the thermostat switching the boiler on and off. Do not offer a replacement unless the customer contacts us about the noise a second time. Never mention the supplier, the batch number, or that revision C is louder than earlier revisions.

## Status

A quieter relay is being evaluated for a later hardware revision. There is no firmware fix, because the noise comes from the relay itself.
