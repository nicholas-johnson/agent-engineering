# Android app: Heating settings screen guide

## Overview

The Heating settings screen lets you change the temperature used for a boost. You can also set a target temperature manually from the app.

A manual target-temperature change lasts until the next schedule period starts. To set temperatures for different times of day, edit the schedule in the Schedule tab.

The Dormouse app is available for Android and iOS. Most settings can also be changed in the web dashboard. Menu paths in this guide are the same in the app and web dashboard.

## Open Heating settings

1. Open the Dormouse app and select your home.
2. Open Settings.
3. Choose Heating.
4. Choose Boost temperature.

Use the Boost temperature option to change the temperature used when you start a boost. The boost runs for one hour, unless you cancel it sooner.

## Change the boost temperature

Set the temperature you want for a boost in Settings > Heating > Boost temperature. The default boost temperature is 21 degrees. You can change it to suit your home.

When a boost is running, the thermostat heats the home at the boost temperature for one hour. An upward arrow appears on the thermostat display while the boost is active. Press the boost button on the thermostat again to cancel it.

## Set a manual target temperature

You can change the target temperature from the app or by turning the thermostat dial. In the app, use the temperature control to choose a target between 5 and 30 degrees, in half-degree steps.

A manual change remains in effect until the next schedule period starts. When that period begins, the schedule sets the target temperature for that time.

## How schedules affect manual changes

A schedule contains periods, each with a start time and target temperature. Each period lasts until the next one starts. When the next period begins, its scheduled temperature replaces a manual target-temperature change.

To change the temperatures or times in a schedule, open the Schedule tab and choose a day. Each day can have up to six periods. You can use Copy day to apply the same periods to other days.

The thermostat's Schedule menu shows today's periods, but schedules can only be changed in the app or web dashboard.

## Boost and schedules

A boost is a temporary one-hour heating period at the boost temperature. It is separate from changing the target temperature manually: a manual target lasts until the next schedule period starts, while a boost runs for one hour unless cancelled.

To adjust the temperature used by boosts, go to Settings > Heating > Boost temperature. To set the temperatures the home follows throughout the day, edit the schedule in the Schedule tab.

## Other ways to change temperature

You can turn the thermostat dial to change the target temperature. The target temperature appears below the room temperature on the display and becomes larger while you turn the dial. Press the dial to confirm your change.

The app and web dashboard provide another way to change the target temperature. Whichever method you use, a manual change lasts until the next schedule period starts.

## Temperature limits and display symbols

The target temperature can be set from 5 to 30 degrees, in half-degree steps.

The thermostat display uses symbols to show heating activity. The upward arrow means a boost is running. The flame means the thermostat is asking the boiler for heat. These symbols indicate what the thermostat is doing; they do not change the temperature settings.

## When settings do not appear to take effect

Check whether a schedule period has started since you changed the target temperature. A manual change lasts only until the next schedule period begins, when the schedule's target takes over.

If you want to change the temperature used by a boost, check Settings > Heating > Boost temperature. Changing the manual target temperature does not change the boost temperature.
