# Sign in to the Dormouse app and web dashboard

## What you need

You can sign in to the Dormouse app for Android and iOS, or to the web dashboard, using your Dormouse account.

Use the email address and password associated with your account. Your password must have at least 10 characters.

## Sign in

1. Open the Dormouse app or web dashboard.
2. Enter your account email address and password.
3. Choose the sign-in option to continue.

If you are signing in on a new phone or browser and two-step verification is turned on, you will also need a 6-digit code.

## Two-step verification

Two-step verification adds a code to your password when you sign in on a new phone or browser. You can receive the code by email, or use an authenticator app if you have set one up.

To turn on two-step verification, go to Settings > Account > Two-step verification.

## If you have forgotten your password

On the sign-in screen, choose Forgot password? and enter your email address. Dormouse will send you a password-reset link.

The link works for 30 minutes and can only be used once. If it expires, request a new link from the sign-in screen.
